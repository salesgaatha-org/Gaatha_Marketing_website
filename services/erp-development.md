## What is ERP Development & Implementation?

ERP development and implementation is the work of putting one system at the centre of a business — purchase, inventory, production, sales, dispatch, invoicing, accounts and people — so every department works from the same data instead of separate spreadsheets. Implementation configures an existing ERP such as Odoo or ERPNext to your process; development builds custom modules, or a whole ERP, where your process does not fit a standard product.

At gaa-tha an ERP project is system engineering before it is software: we map how orders, materials and money actually move, design the target process and data model, decide what stays in Tally or other tools and how it integrates, and only then configure or code. Rollout is phased module by module, with data migration, parallel runs and role-wise training, so the business keeps running while the system changes.

## How we work

1. **Map the business** — We sit with each department to document how orders, materials and money move today, where data is re-entered and which reports owners actually use.
2. **Design the system** — We design the target process, data model, roles, integrations and reports, and recommend Odoo, ERPNext or a custom build with a phased plan.
3. **Configure and build** — Modules are configured and custom features developed in sprints, with a working demo on your own data at the end of each one.
4. **Migrate and run in parallel** — Masters, opening stock and balances are cleaned and migrated, and the new system runs alongside the old one until the numbers reconcile.
5. **Go live and support** — Users are trained by role, the old process is switched off module by module, and a support plan covers fixes, changes, upgrades and new modules.

## Frequently asked questions

### Custom ERP vs Odoo vs ERPNext vs Tally — which is right for us?

Tally is accounting and inventory software that most Indian accountants know, Odoo and ERPNext are full ERPs that cover operations end to end, and a custom ERP fits when your process is genuinely unusual or is your competitive edge. Many small and mid-sized businesses do best with Odoo or ERPNext for operations, using either its built-in accounting or a Tally integration so the accountant keeps working in Tally. We recommend after mapping your process, not before.

### What is the difference between Odoo and ERPNext?

Both are open-source ERPs with strong inventory, manufacturing and accounting modules and Indian GST support. Odoo has a large app ecosystem and a polished interface, and comes in a free Community edition and a paid per-user Enterprise edition, with some features — including full accounting — reserved for Enterprise. ERPNext, built on the Frappe framework, is fully open source with no per-user licence and uses the India Compliance app for GST, which suits businesses with many users who are comfortable self-hosting or paying for managed hosting.

### How long does an ERP implementation take?

An Odoo or ERPNext implementation typically takes six to sixteen weeks, depending on the number of modules, locations and customisations and the state of your data. A custom ERP is built in phases, with the first module live in eight to twelve weeks. The biggest variables are data clean-up and how quickly process decisions are made, not the software itself.

### How much does ERP implementation cost?

Cost is driven by the number of modules and users, how much is custom development versus standard configuration, the integrations, the data-migration effort and the platform's licence model. Odoo Enterprise carries per-user subscription fees, ERPNext has no licence but needs hosting and support, and a custom ERP has no licence but a larger build. We quote module by module after process mapping so spending can be phased.

### Can we keep using Tally alongside the ERP?

Yes. A common set-up runs purchase, inventory, production, sales and dispatch in the ERP and keeps the books in TallyPrime, with invoices, receipts and ledgers synced automatically. Your accountant keeps the tool they know, and owners get operational data in one place. If you later move accounts fully into the ERP, that can be a separate phase.

### Does the ERP handle GST, e-invoicing and e-way bills?

Yes. Odoo and ERPNext both support Indian GST, and e-invoices and e-way bills can be generated from the ERP through the government systems, either directly or through a GST Suvidha Provider integration depending on the platform. We configure HSN codes, tax rules, place of supply and print formats, and test them with your accountant before go-live.

### Who owns the ERP, the code and the data?

You do. Custom modules sit in a repository in your name, the ERP runs on your cloud account or servers, and all admin credentials are handed over. With Odoo Community or ERPNext there is no lock-in to us or to a vendor, and any Odoo Enterprise subscription is held in your name.

### What support do we need after go-live?

Expect the first two to three months to bring small fixes, report changes and user questions, which is why post-go-live support is part of every project. After that, a support plan covers bug fixes, version upgrades, new reports and modules, and training for new staff, with agreed response times.
