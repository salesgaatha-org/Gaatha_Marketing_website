Nykaa & Nykaa Fashion Onboarding

gaa-tha manages Nykaa and Nykaa Fashion onboarding for beauty, personal care, wellness and fashion brands: eligibility review, brand and product-compliance documentation, registration, an ingredient- and claim-checked catalogue for Nykaa or a style-level catalogue for Nykaa Fashion, images to each platform's specs, ad placements and an event calendar built around Pink Friday and Hot Pink Sale.

The service

What is Nykaa & Nykaa Fashion Onboarding?

Nykaa onboarding is the process of getting a beauty or personal-care brand approved and live on Nykaa, and a fashion brand on Nykaa Fashion, India's leading beauty-led marketplaces: passing brand curation, proving product compliance with manufacturing licences, ingredient lists, shelf life and label requirements, agreeing a commercial model, and building a catalogue that explains ingredients, benefits and usage the way Nykaa's shoppers read it. Nykaa is a curated, brand-led platform, so a compliance-ready pitch matters more than a fast upload.

At gaa-tha we run the two platforms as two different jobs. Nykaa buyers research ingredients, shade and skin type and trust expert-style content, so the beauty catalogue is built around claims that can be substantiated and images that show the product, its label and its texture. Nykaa Fashion buyers shop premium and occasion-led styles, so that catalogue follows fashion attributes and portrait model imagery. Both get an event calendar and ad plan built for Nykaa's audience.

Scope

What's included

  • Eligibility review: brand positioning, price band, category fit and compliance readiness for Nykaa or Nykaa Fashion
  • Compliance file: cosmetics manufacturing licence, ingredient (INCI) lists, shelf life, batch and expiry, legal metrology label checks and claim substantiation
  • Brand pitch and registration, with the commercial model on offer explained before you sign
  • Nykaa catalogue: product type, concern, skin or hair type, key ingredients, benefits, how to use, shade or variant mapping and size or volume
  • Nykaa Fashion catalogue: style-level listings with fit, fabric, occasion, care and size charts
  • Imagery to spec: square white-background product shots, back-label shots, texture or swatch images and shade charts for Nykaa; portrait model imagery for Nykaa Fashion
  • Pricing and offer ladder for regular periods and Nykaa's sale events
  • Ad placements: sponsored product listings, category banners and sampling with orders, planned with Nykaa's ad team
  • Event calendar and nominations for Pink Friday Sale, Hot Pink Sale and category campaigns, plus ongoing management

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Eligibility and compliance

We review positioning, pricing and category fit, then assemble the compliance file — licences, ingredient lists, shelf life, labels and claims — so the application does not stall on paperwork.

02

Pitch and registration

A short brand deck and registration go to the relevant Nykaa or Nykaa Fashion category team, and the commercial model and agreement are walked through with you before signature.

03

Catalogue and imagery

Beauty SKUs are written around ingredients, concerns and usage with shade and variant mapping; fashion styles are built with full attributes and size charts. Images are shot or QC'd to each platform's specification.

04

Pricing, ads and events

An offer ladder is set for regular and event periods, ad placements and sampling are planned for hero SKUs, and the first Pink Friday or Hot Pink Sale nomination is scheduled.

05

Go-live and management

Listings go live after QC, and the account moves to a monthly rhythm: stock, orders, ratings and reviews, new launches, campaign participation and a sell-through review by SKU.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Eligibility report and compliance file ready for submission
  • Registered account and completed agreements on Nykaa or Nykaa Fashion
  • Approved catalogue with ingredient, claim and attribute data and QC-passed images
  • Offer ladder for regular and event periods
  • Ad placement and sampling plan with an event calendar for the season
  • Monthly review of sell-through, ratings and campaign results

Good to know

Questions about Nykaa

Typically a trademark certificate or authorisation letter, GST, PAN and bank details, a cosmetics manufacturing licence or importer registration, ingredient lists, shelf-life and batch details, product images including the back label, and proof for any claims such as dermatologically tested. Nykaa Fashion asks for brand and business documents and size charts instead of product compliance. We share the current checklist at the start.

Approval by a category team typically takes three to eight weeks because compliance is checked, and the catalogue and image QC take another two to four weeks. Nykaa Fashion is usually quicker. We time applications so you are live before Pink Friday or a Hot Pink Sale rather than during one.

Nykaa works on margins or commissions by category, with logistics charges and sometimes marketing contributions, and the structure depends on the commercial model you are offered. The numbers vary and change, so we do not publish them; we walk you through the current terms and model your margin per SKU before you sign.
