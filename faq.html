03AI ads, AI ad shoots & cloning

AI ads are advertising creatives generated or enhanced with generative AI — product visuals, cinematic scenes, voiceovers and edits produced without a full traditional shoot. An AI ad shoot recreates the output of a physical production (models, locations, lighting, camera moves) using AI, at a fraction of the cost and turnaround. We combine both with human art direction so the result looks premium, never robotic. Read the complete guide →

AI cloning creates a licensed digital twin of a founder or spokesperson — voice and face — so you can produce unlimited spokesperson videos without booking a studio every time. It's done with full written consent, watermark-safe workflows and brand approval on every output. It's how founders stay on camera daily without shooting daily.

Not when they're art-directed. Raw AI output does look generic — that's why every gaa-tha AI ad passes through human creative direction: brand-matched grading, typography, sound design and story structure. Our clients' AI ads have outperformed their traditional creatives on cost-per-result while looking richer.

Typically 60–90% cheaper. A traditional product or model shoot in India runs ₹1–10 lakh+ once you count crew, location, models and post-production. An AI ad shoot removes most of those line items and compresses weeks into days. High-stakes hero films may still warrant a hybrid: real footage plus AI enhancement.

Yes — we plan, script and direct real ad shoots and reels productions, and we often blend real footage with AI enhancement for the best of both. The medium follows the story, not the other way around.

04SEO, AEO & GEO

SEO gets you ranked in classic search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets you chosen as the direct answer — in Google AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your brand cited and recommended inside AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. We optimise for all three, because your next customer may never see a classic results page. Read the full guide →

AI assistants recommend brands with clear, structured, consistent and widely-corroborated information: schema markup, genuine FAQs, authoritative content, consistent business data across the web, third-party mentions and reviews. That's GEO — and it's a core gaa-tha service. We structure your site and content so generative engines can understand, trust and cite you.

Meaningful movement typically shows in 3–6 months, with compounding gains after that. AEO and GEO can move faster — structured answers and schema are often picked up by AI engines within weeks. We pair the slow-burn (SEO) with quick wins (AEO/GEO and performance ads) so growth never stalls.

Yes. Content marketing is the fuel for SEO, AEO and GEO — we run keyword and question research, write authoritative articles and FAQs, add structured data, and distribute across social. One story, many formats: an insight becomes a blog post, a reel, a carousel and an ad.
