# How to outsource digital marketing to India: a guide for US and UK businesses

> To outsource digital marketing to India, decide which work to hand over (ads, social media, Shopify and web builds, apps, ERP, video editing or strategy), pick an engagement model (project, retainer, dedicated team or white-label), agree working hours and reporting, sign an NDA and a clear statement of work, then start with a small paid pilot before committing to a retainer.

## Key takeaways

- Outsource well-defined, repeatable work first: ad management, social content, Shopify builds and maintenance, web and app development, and video editing.
- Choose the engagement model before the vendor: fixed-scope project, monthly retainer, dedicated team, or white-label if you are an agency.
- Agree working hours up front. India is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of US Eastern time and 4.5 to 5.5 hours ahead of the UK, and some teams work your full business day.
- Put the NDA, statement of work, IP ownership and account ownership in writing before you share client data.
- Start with a paid pilot with written acceptance criteria, then move to a retainer once quality and communication are proven.

## What marketing and development work can you outsource to India?

You can outsource almost any digital work that is delivered through a screen: performance marketing, social media, creative production, Shopify and website development, mobile apps, ERP implementation and video editing, plus strategy and consulting delivered over video calls. The real test is not whether the work can travel, but whether it can be briefed, reviewed and measured remotely.

| Work | What a partner typically handles | What you usually keep |
|---|---|---|
| Performance marketing (Meta and Google ads) | Campaign builds, creative testing, bid and budget management, tracking checks, weekly reporting | Budget approval, offer and pricing decisions, ownership of the ad accounts |
| Social media management | Content calendar, design, captions, scheduling, community replies within agreed guidelines | Brand voice sign-off, sensitive replies, partnerships |
| Shopify development and store management | Theme builds, custom sections, migrations, app setup, product uploads, fixes and speed work | Store ownership, product and pricing decisions |
| Website development | Design, front-end and CMS builds, landing pages, ongoing maintenance | Domain and hosting accounts, final content approval |
| Mobile and web apps | UX, development, QA, release management and maintenance | Product roadmap, app store accounts |
| ERP and Odoo | Process mapping, configuration, custom modules, integrations, data migration | Process owners and final sign-off on workflows |
| Reel and video editing | Short-form edits, captions, motion graphics, versions for each platform | Raw footage, on-camera talent, final approval |
| Marketing consulting | Audits, strategy, channel plans, fractional CMO support | Decisions and budgets |

Work that depends on being physically present, such as in-store events, local field marketing or live shoots in your city, is harder to hand over, although a remote team can still plan it, edit the footage and run the ads around it.

## Why do US and UK businesses outsource to agencies in India?

US and UK businesses outsource to India mainly for capacity and cost structure: they get access to a full team of designers, developers, editors and media buyers for work that would be slow or expensive to staff locally, without adding permanent headcount.

- **Capacity on demand.** Agencies can take on more clients, and brands can ship more, without hiring and training for every peak.

- **Breadth in one team.** A single partner can cover ads, content, Shopify, apps and automation, so there are fewer vendors to manage and fewer handoffs between them.

- **Cost structure.** Lower salary and overhead costs in India usually mean the same budget buys more hours or a broader team. Savings vary widely by scope and partner, so compare full proposals rather than headline rates.

- **English as the working language.** Business communication, documentation and code comments are in English as standard.

- **Time-zone leverage.** Work can move forward overnight for fast turnarounds, or a team can work your hours for live collaboration.

Outsourcing is a poor fit when the scope changes daily with nobody owning the brief, when the work needs deep local context the partner cannot learn, or when you are hoping a vendor will fix a problem nobody in your business has defined. In those cases, start with an audit or a [consulting engagement](https://gaa-tha.com/services/marketing-consulting-fractional-cmo) rather than handing over execution.

## Which engagement model should you choose: project, retainer, dedicated team or white-label?

Choose a fixed-scope project for work with a clear end, a monthly retainer for ongoing work, a dedicated team when you want capacity you direct yourself, and a white-label arrangement if you are an agency reselling the work under your own brand.

| Model | Best for | How it is usually priced | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-scope project | Website or Shopify builds, app MVPs, migrations, ERP rollouts, audits | One price for an agreed scope, paid by milestone | Change requests outside the scope; agree up front how they are quoted |
| Monthly retainer | Ads management, social media, store management, video editing, maintenance | A monthly fee for an agreed scope or volume of work | Vague scopes; define deliverables and turnaround times |
| Dedicated team | Long product builds, high-volume production, agencies that need extra hands | A monthly fee per role, with the work directed by you | More management load on your side; insist on a named lead |
| White-label | Agencies delivering work to their own clients under their own brand | Project, retainer or per deliverable, resold at the agency's own price | Branding, client contact and non-solicitation must be written into the contract |

Many relationships move through these models in sequence: a paid pilot, then a project, then a retainer. Agencies considering the white-label route can read [how white-label agency services work](https://gaa-tha.com/blog/white-label-agency-services-explained).

## How do time zones and communication work with a team in India?

India Standard Time is UTC+5:30 all year with no daylight saving, so India is 9.5 hours ahead of US Eastern time in summer and 10.5 hours ahead in winter, 12.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of US Pacific time, and 4.5 to 5.5 hours ahead of the UK. That leaves three workable models:

- **Your hours.** The team works your business day, so calls, chat replies and launches happen live. Best for ads management, store operations and anything with frequent back-and-forth.

- **Overlap.** A few shared hours each day for calls and decisions, with focused work outside them. Common for UK clients, whose morning overlaps the Indian afternoon.

- **Overnight handoff.** You brief at the end of your day and wake up to finished work. Works well for video editing, design batches and development tickets.

Whichever model you pick, good communication is a process, not a personality trait. Ask for:

- One named lead who owns your account and answers for delivery.

- A shared channel (Slack, Microsoft Teams or email) with an agreed response time.

- A project board (Asana, ClickUp, Trello, Jira or Notion) where every request has an owner and a due date.

- Written briefs, and short screen recordings for feedback instead of long chains of calls.

- A weekly check-in and a monthly report against the KPIs agreed at the start, such as cost per acquisition, ROAS or MER for ads (see [ROAS vs MER vs contribution margin](https://gaa-tha.com/blog/roas-vs-mer-vs-contribution-margin)).

If the partner will work inside your tools, set up their accounts with only the permissions they need, and keep admin rights for ad accounts, stores and domains in your own name.

## What should the contract and payment terms cover?

At minimum, a contract with an outsourcing partner should include a non-disclosure agreement signed before you share anything sensitive, a statement of work with deliverables and acceptance criteria, ownership of the work and the accounts, payment terms, and how either side can end the relationship.

- **NDA.** Covers your data, your clients and your plans. Sign it before the brief, not after the proposal.

- **Statement of work.** Deliverables, timelines, turnaround times, revision rounds and what is out of scope.

- **IP and accounts.** Code, designs and content transfer to you on payment; ad accounts, stores, domains and app store listings stay in your name.

- **Data protection.** If the partner will handle personal data of UK or EU customers, agree a data processing agreement that reflects UK GDPR or GDPR.

- **Non-solicitation.** Essential for agencies: the partner must not approach your clients directly.

- **Termination and handover.** Notice period, final invoice and a documented handover of files, credentials and access.

Payment is usually straightforward. Indian agencies routinely invoice international clients and accept international bank transfers (SWIFT), and many also accept PayPal or card payments. Agree the invoicing currency, who carries transfer fees, and whether work is billed in advance (typical for retainers) or by milestone (typical for projects). US finance teams often ask a foreign vendor for a W-8BEN-E form for their records, so request one if you need it. For the clauses agencies rely on most, see [white-label agreements explained](https://gaa-tha.com/blog/white-label-agreements-nda-non-solicitation).

## How do you start with a paid pilot project?

Start with a small paid pilot: one well-defined piece of real work, with a fixed fee, a short timeline and written acceptance criteria, so you can judge quality, communication and speed before committing to a retainer.

Good pilots are real work you need anyway, small enough to finish in two to four weeks:

- A landing page, or one custom Shopify section built to your design system.

- An ad account audit plus one month of campaign management on a single account.

- A batch of short-form edits cut from footage you already have.

- A two-week sprint on a backlog of website or app tickets.

- A marketing audit with a 90-day plan.

Decide beforehand how you will score it. Did the work meet the brief the first time? How many revision rounds did it take? Were deadlines and response times kept? Did the team ask good questions before starting? Would you put the result in front of your own customers or clients? If the answers are yes, move to a retainer using the same brief and review format, so the pilot becomes the template for the relationship.

Avoid unpaid test tasks. Paying for the pilot gets you the partner's real team and process, and makes ownership of the output clear.

## What are the red flags when outsourcing to an offshore agency?

The biggest red flag is a partner that cannot show real, live work and explain exactly what it did on each project. Others to watch for:

- Prices far below every other proposal with no explanation of what is excluded.

- Guaranteed rankings, ROAS, lead volumes or follower numbers.

- Ad accounts, stores or domains set up in the agency's name instead of yours.

- No written scope, no acceptance criteria and no change-request process.

- Reluctance to sign an NDA or a non-solicitation clause.

- A different person on every call, or no clarity about who actually does the work, including undisclosed subcontracting.

- Reporting that only shows activity (posts published, hours logged) rather than outcomes, or lead counts with no check on lead quality (see [filtering fake leads from Meta ads](https://gaa-tha.com/blog/filtering-fake-leads-from-meta-ads)).

- Pressure to skip the pilot and sign a long contract upfront.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it safe to outsource marketing to India?

It is safe when the basics are in writing: an NDA before you share data, ad accounts and stores in your own name, limited-permission access and a clear scope. Most problems come from vague scopes and shared logins, not geography. A paid pilot lets you check quality and communication before you commit.

### How much does it cost to outsource digital marketing to India?

It depends on the scope, the volume of work and the engagement model, so reputable agencies quote after a brief rather than publishing one price. Projects are quoted for an agreed set of deliverables, retainers per month of agreed work, and dedicated teams per role. Compare proposals line by line on scope, not on headline rates.

### Can an Indian agency work US business hours?

Yes. Some teams work the full US business day, some keep a few overlapping hours, and some hand work over overnight. Agree the model, response times and meeting windows in the contract so expectations are clear from day one.

### What is the difference between outsourcing and white-label?

Outsourcing means a business hires an outside partner to do its own work. White-label means an agency hires a partner to produce work that the agency sells to its own clients under its own brand, so the partner stays invisible to the end client.

### Should I hire freelancers or an agency in India?

Freelancers suit small, well-defined tasks. An agency suits ongoing or multi-skill work because it brings a team, a named lead, cover when someone is away and a process for quality control.

### How do I pay an agency in India from the US or UK?

Most Indian agencies invoice international clients and accept international bank transfers (SWIFT), and many also accept PayPal or card payments. Agree the currency, the payment schedule and who pays transfer fees before work starts.

### Do I keep ownership of the work and the accounts?

You should. Put IP transfer on payment into the contract, and keep ad accounts, Shopify stores, domains, hosting and app store listings in your own name, with the partner on staff or partner access that you can remove.
