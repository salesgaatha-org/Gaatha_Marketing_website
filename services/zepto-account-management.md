## What is Zepto Account Management?

Zepto account management is the ongoing operation of a brand's account on Zepto after go-live: keeping SKUs in stock across dark stores, forecasting and coordinating purchase orders, maintaining catalogue content, planning pricing and promotions, running Zepto Ads, watching ratings and reconciling settlements against what was supplied. On a 10-minute platform an SKU that goes out of stock in a city drops out of search almost immediately, so the work runs on a weekly cycle.

At gaa-tha a named account manager owns the Zepto account. We track availability and fill rate by city and dark store, keep the PO cycle moving with your warehouse, plan promotions around Zepto's sale events and festive periods, and run ads only for SKUs and cities with stock to sell. Zepto's deepest markets are Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune, so the review is always by city, not just by SKU.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you manage availability and fill rate on Zepto?

We check availability at dark-store level every week, flag SKUs that are out of stock or low in any city, and measure fill rate as quantity received against quantity ordered on each PO. Availability problems are fixed by forecasting earlier; fill-rate problems are fixed at your warehouse with packaging, labelling and dispatch discipline.

### How do purchase orders work on Zepto?

Zepto raises POs to its city warehouses based on demand and stock on hand, and the brand dispatches within the PO window. We forecast expected POs from sell-through, chase late ones, coordinate quantities and packaging with your team, track dispatch and receipt, and log short receipts or rejections for dispute.

### Do you maintain the catalogue and content?

Yes. Titles with pack size, thumbnails, secondary images, attributes and descriptions are kept current, new SKUs and pack variants added, and listings rechecked after Zepto template changes. The thumbnails are produced in-house to win in a two-second scroll.

### How do you plan pricing and promotions on Zepto?

To margin first. We model the discount, Zepto's share and the expected uplift before committing to a sale event or festive campaign, and we keep pricing aligned across Blinkit, Instamart and your own store so no channel undercuts another.

### Do you run Zepto Ads?

Yes. Zepto Ads offers sponsored product placements on search and category pages and banner placements, and we run them by SKU and city against a target ROAS. Ads are paused in any city where the SKU is out of stock so budget is not spent on clicks that cannot convert.

### How do you manage ratings on Zepto?

Ratings and feedback are monitored weekly, product complaints separated from packaging and delivery issues, and each routed to the right owner. Recurring themes are reported monthly so pack, label or formulation changes can be made before they affect sell-through.

### What does reconciliation involve?

Matching Zepto's settlements to POs, goods receipt notes, short receipts, damages, returns, debit notes and ad or marketing invoices. Discrepancies are raised within the dispute window and tracked to closure so your books reflect what was supplied and sold.

### What is in the monthly review?

Sales and units by city and SKU, availability and fill rate, PO status, catalogue changes, promotions and their margin impact, Zepto Ads spend and ROAS, ratings trends and the reconciliation summary, followed by decisions on SKUs, cities and budget for the coming month.
