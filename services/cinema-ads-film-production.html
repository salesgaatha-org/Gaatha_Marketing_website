The service

What is Cinema & Film Ads?

A cinema ad is a short film — usually 10 to 30 seconds — screened before the feature in multiplexes and single-screen theatres, where a captive audience watches it on a large screen with full surround sound and no skip button. The same film is then cut for OTT pre-rolls, YouTube, Instagram and in-store screens so the recall it builds is reinforced everywhere.

gaa-tha produces cinema ads as live-action shoots, as AI-generated films, or as a blend of the two, which is how most brands get trailer-grade visuals inside a local budget. Every film is written for a large screen first — composition in 2.39:1 scope, sound designed for cinema audio — and then reframed for vertical and square, so a Surat retail chain or a Gujarat FMCG launch is seen in the multiplex one evening and on Instagram the next morning.

Scope

What's included

  • Concept, script and voiceover copy in English, Hindi or Gujarati
  • Storyboard and shot list
  • Live-action production: crew, location, talent and direction
  • AI-generated production for scenes a shoot cannot afford
  • Edit, colour grade and motion graphics
  • Sound design and mix built for cinema audio and for mobile speakers
  • 10-, 20- and 30-second cuts
  • 2.39:1 scope master plus 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 versions
  • In-store screen loops and captioned silent versions
  • Cinema media planning support: screen selection, DCP delivery and certification guidance

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Concept

We start from the campaign objective — launch, festive, footfall — and write two or three film ideas with a one-line story each.

02

Script and storyboard

The chosen idea becomes a script, storyboard and shot list, with the production route decided: live-action, AI or a blend.

03

Produce

Shoot days are planned and directed, or AI scenes are generated under the same art direction; the founder or product is filmed for real where it matters.

04

Post-production

Edit, colour, motion graphics, voiceover and a cinema-grade sound mix, reviewed at full scale before the final cuts.

05

Deliver and distribute

Masters go out in every format with the DCP-ready file and certification guidance for cinema; we support media booking and cut the same film for OTT, YouTube and in-store screens.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Approved script and storyboard
  • Master film in 2.39:1 scope, plus 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 versions
  • 10-, 20- and 30-second cuts with cinema-mixed audio
  • DCP-ready export and certification guidance
  • In-store screen loop and captioned silent version
  • Stills pulled from the film for hoardings and social
  • Raw footage and project files archived on a shared drive
