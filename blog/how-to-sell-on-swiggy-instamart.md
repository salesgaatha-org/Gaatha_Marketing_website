# How to sell on Swiggy Instamart: a guide for brands

> To sell on Swiggy Instamart, a brand applies through Swiggy’s partner route, completes a category and documentation review, submits a catalogue to Instamart’s image and data specifications, agrees commercial terms and fulfils a first purchase order for its launch cities. Instamart sits inside the Swiggy app, so its audience arrives with food and beverage habits, which suits snack, drink, dessert and meal-adjacent brands particularly well.

## Key takeaways

- Instamart’s food-ordering audience favours snacks, beverages, desserts and ready-to-eat products.
- Onboarding follows the standard quick-commerce sequence: review, documents, catalogue, terms, first PO.
- Pack sizes should suit occasions, not just top-ups.
- Availability and ratings determine placement more than ads do.

## Why is Instamart different from other quick-commerce apps?

Instamart shares an app with Swiggy’s food delivery, so many of its customers are already in a food mindset. Brands that pair naturally with a meal or an evening in, such as snacks, beverages, ice cream, bakery, condiments and party supplies, tend to find a receptive audience. Everyday essentials sell too, but the food link is the distinctive opportunity.

That audience also shapes timing. Orders cluster around meal times, evenings and weekends, which affects how stock should be planned and when promotions and banners are most useful.

## What does Instamart onboarding involve?

Application and category review, followed by business and brand documents (GST, PAN, bank details, trademark or authorisation, FSSAI for food), commercial terms, catalogue submission, city selection and a first purchase order. Timelines run to several weeks and vary by category. Our [Swiggy Instamart onboarding](https://gaa-tha.com/services/swiggy-instamart-onboarding) page lists the current checklist.

Commercial terms, including margins, promotional contributions and any onboarding charges, vary by category and change over time. Ask for the current structure in writing and check the per-SKU contribution before agreeing.

## How should a brand prepare its catalogue for Instamart?

Thumbnails must be clean and legible at small size; titles carry brand, product and pack size; nutrition, ingredient and allergen information must match the label. Occasion-led combos, such as a snack and drink pairing or a party pack, are worth listing as their own SKUs. [Q-commerce creative](https://gaa-tha.com/services/quick-commerce-content) covers thumbnails, titles and pack strategy.

Launch with a short list of hero SKUs rather than the full range. It keeps availability manageable, gives cleaner velocity data per store, and makes the case for expansion easier to argue with the category team.

## How do you manage availability and growth after launch?

Track fill rate by store weekly, coordinate replenishment purchase orders, monitor ratings, and start search ads on brand terms once availability is stable. Evening and weekend demand spikes are pronounced on a food-led platform, so stock planning should follow the order curve. ## Frequently asked questions

### How long does Swiggy Instamart onboarding take?

Typically several weeks from application to first order, depending on category review, document checks and catalogue approval. Food products with complete FSSAI and labelling information move faster.

### Which products sell best on Instamart?

Snacks, beverages, desserts, bakery, condiments and party or occasion products benefit most from Instamart’s food-led audience, alongside everyday essentials.

### Is there a listing fee on Swiggy Instamart?

Commercial terms vary by category and can include margins, promotional contributions and onboarding charges, and they change periodically. Confirm the current structure before you sign.

### Can I be on Instamart, Blinkit and Zepto at the same time?

Yes. Most brands eventually list on all three, but it is easier to launch on one, establish availability and velocity, then add the others with the learnings.
