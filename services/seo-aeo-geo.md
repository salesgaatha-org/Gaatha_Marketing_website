## What is SEO, AEO & GEO?

SEO, AEO and GEO are three layers of search visibility: SEO (search engine optimisation) gets a website ranked in classic Google results, AEO (answer engine optimisation) gets it chosen as the direct answer in AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants, and GEO (generative engine optimisation) gets the brand cited and recommended inside AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. All three reward the same thing — clear, structured, consistent and corroborated information.

At gaa-tha the three are one programme. Technical SEO and keyword research build the base; answer-first pages, FAQs and schema make the site quotable; digital PR, citations and consistent business data across the web make it trustworthy. We track rankings and AI citations side by side, because your next customer may never see a classic results page.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO gets you ranked in classic search results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) gets you chosen as the direct answer in Google AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants. GEO (generative engine optimisation) gets your brand cited and recommended inside AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. gaa-tha optimises for all three in one programme.

### How long does SEO take to show results?

Meaningful movement typically shows in three to six months, with compounding gains after that. AEO and GEO can move faster — structured answers, FAQs and schema are often picked up by AI engines within weeks. We pair the slow burn with quick wins so growth never stalls.

### How do I get my brand recommended by ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

AI assistants recommend brands with clear, structured, consistent and widely corroborated information: answer-first pages, schema markup, genuine FAQs, consistent business facts across the web, third-party mentions and reviews. We structure your site and content so generative engines can understand, trust and cite you, and track whether they do.

### Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and nobody honestly can — Google and AI engines control the results. What we commit to is the work: fixes shipped, pages published, citations built, and a monthly report showing rankings, traffic, leads and AI mentions moving. Guaranteed rankings are one of the red flags we tell clients to walk away from.

### What is llms.txt and do I need one?

llms.txt is a plain-text file at the root of a website that summarises what the site is about and links to its most important pages, written for AI crawlers and assistants. It costs little to add and makes it easier for AI tools to read and cite you correctly, so every site we build or optimise ships with one.

### Do we need a blog or regular content?

Yes, if you want to be found for the questions buyers ask rather than only your brand name. Content marketing is the fuel for SEO, AEO and GEO: keyword and question research, authoritative articles and FAQs with structured data, then distribution on social. One insight becomes a post, a reel, a carousel and an ad.

### Should I do SEO or Google Ads first?

Both, if budget allows — ads for demand this month, SEO for demand that compounds. If you must choose, start with Google Ads to learn which keywords convert, then invest in SEO for those terms so paid and organic reinforce each other.

### How do you track AI visibility?

By running a fixed set of buyer questions through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews each month and recording whether your brand is mentioned, cited or recommended, alongside classic rankings and Search Console data. The report shows both, so you can see search and AI visibility move together.
