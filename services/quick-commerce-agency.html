The service

What is Quick Commerce Management?

Quick commerce management is the end-to-end operation of a brand's presence on 10-minute delivery platforms: choosing which platforms and cities to enter, planning pack sizes and margins so the listing is profitable, completing onboarding, building the catalogue, keeping products in stock across dark stores, coordinating purchase orders, running ads and promotions, and reconciling what the platform pays. It is closer to running a distribution business than to running a marketplace listing.

At gaa-tha one team owns all six platforms for a brand. We start with a platform-by-city map of where your category sells, fix the pack sizes and price points before onboarding, and then run availability, purchase orders, content, ads and ratings on a weekly rhythm with a single monthly review across Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket.

Scope

What's included

  • Platform and city prioritisation based on category demand, competition and your distribution reach
  • Margin, pack-size and price-point planning so each SKU works after platform margins and promotions
  • Onboarding and documentation on every platform you choose, including category licences
  • Catalogue build: titles with pack size, thumbnails, secondary images, attributes and descriptions
  • Dark-store availability and fill-rate tracking by city, with weekly out-of-stock alerts
  • Purchase-order and replenishment coordination with your warehouse or distributor
  • Quick-commerce ads on Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart planned to a target ROAS
  • Promotions and visibility events aligned to festivals and platform sale days
  • Ratings and customer-feedback monitoring with product and packaging fixes fed back to you
  • Monthly review covering sales, availability, ads, promotions and settlement reconciliation

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Map and prioritise

We map your SKUs and target cities against each platform's category strength, then agree an entry order — usually two platforms first, not six.

02

Fix the economics

Pack sizes, MRP, platform margin, promotional depth and logistics cost are modelled per SKU so nothing goes live at a loss.

03

Onboard and list

Documents, brand approvals, category licences, catalogue and images are completed platform by platform, with the first purchase order as the finish line.

04

Run the operation

Weekly availability checks, PO follow-ups, content fixes, ad optimisation and promotion planning across every live platform.

05

Review and expand

A monthly review with sales, fill rate, ad ROAS and reconciliation, then a decision on new cities, SKUs or platforms.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Platform-by-city entry plan with recommended launch order
  • SKU economics sheet (pack size, MRP, margin, promo depth, landed cost)
  • Live, approved catalogues on each chosen platform
  • Weekly availability and fill-rate tracker by city and dark store
  • Purchase-order log with dispatch and receipt status
  • Monthly quick-commerce review report and reconciliation summary
