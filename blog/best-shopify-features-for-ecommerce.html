The best Shopify features for ecommerce in 2026

By gaa-tha· 22 October 2026· 13 min read· Shopify
Blog cover · 16:9

The best Shopify features for ecommerce in 2026 are the ones most stores already pay for and underuse: Online Store 2.0 sections, metafields and metaobjects, Search & Discovery, Shopify Flow, customer segments with Shopify Email, Bundles and discount Functions, Markets, local delivery and POS. Used well, they replace several paid apps, keep the store fast and lift conversion. This guide explains what each feature does, who should use it and how.

Key takeaways

  • Most growth features are already in your plan: sections, metaobjects, Search & Discovery, Flow, segments and Shopify Email cost little or nothing to switch on.
  • Native features usually beat apps on speed, because every app removed is one less script on a mobile page.
  • Indian stores have one caveat: when a third-party checkout such as GoKwik or Shiprocket Checkout replaces Shopify checkout, test which discounts, bundles and checkout customisations still apply.
  • A few features (checkout UI extensions inside checkout, custom apps with Shopify Functions, Launchpad and the full B2B toolkit) are Shopify Plus territory, so confirm current plan details first.
  • Switch features on in order of revenue impact: search and merchandising, then automation and retention, then expansion.

What are the best Shopify features for ecommerce in 2026?

The best Shopify features are the ones that help buyers find the right product, make the store easier to run and make each order worth more. Shopify ships hundreds of features; around sixteen do most of the commercial work for a direct-to-consumer (D2C) or retail brand. The table summarises them, and the sections below explain how to use each one properly.

How should you use Online Store 2.0 sections, metafields and metaobjects?

Online Store 2.0 is why a marketing team can build a landing page in an afternoon. Every page template is made of sections, each section holds blocks, and apps add their own app blocks rather than editing theme code. Newer Shopify themes go further with nested theme blocks that can be reused across sections, so a well-built theme behaves more like a design system than a set of fixed pages.

Metafields add custom fields to products, variants, collections, customers and orders: a “how to use” field, a shade family, a fabric care code, a shelf life. Metaobjects are reusable content entries with their own fields: an ingredient with an image and a benefit, a size chart, a store location with timings, a designer profile. Both connect to sections through dynamic sources in the theme editor, so content is entered once and appears wherever it is needed.

How to use them well

  • Model the data before designing pages. List what a buyer needs to decide (ingredients, sizes, compatibility, origin) and create a metafield or metaobject for each, instead of pasting it into product descriptions.
  • Let metafields power filters. A “skin concern” or “occasion” metafield becomes a storefront filter in Search & Discovery.
  • Build a small section library (hero, benefits, comparison, FAQ, reviews, UGC) that marketing can recombine for each campaign.
  • Prefer a section to an app. A custom size-guide section or combo builder is lighter than an app script.

The Kitcoz store follows this approach: concern-led collections, a kit selector and a combo builder built into the theme rather than bolted on.

How do you get the most from Shopify Search & Discovery?

Shopify Search & Discovery is a free Shopify app that controls storefront filters, search synonyms, product boosts and product recommendations, and every store with more than a handful of products should configure it. It is the cheapest conversion work most stores never finish.

  • Filters: filter by availability, price, product type, vendor, variant options such as size and colour, and product metafields. Order filters by how buyers actually choose, and hide filters with a single value.
  • Synonyms: map the words customers type to the words you use. Indian stores gain a lot here: “kurti” and “kurta”, “chappal” and “slippers”, “facewash” and “face wash”, Hinglish spellings and common misspellings.
  • Boosts: promote specific products for specific searches, such as the hero SKU for “sunscreen” or the new launch for “gift”.
  • Recommendations: Shopify generates related products automatically; complementary products (“pair it with”) are set manually in the app and are worth setting for every top seller.

Review search terms monthly. Searches with no results tell you which synonyms to add and sometimes which products to launch. Only very large catalogues usually need a dedicated search app.

What can Shopify Flow, Shopify Email, segments and Inbox automate?

Shopify Flow automates repetitive store work with a trigger, a condition and an action, and it is available on every paid Shopify plan. Start from its template library and adapt:

  • Tag repeat or high-value customers and add them to a VIP segment.
  • Alert the team on email or Slack when a hero SKU falls below a stock threshold.
  • Hold and tag orders with high fraud risk or mismatched addresses for review.
  • Tag COD orders above a set value so the team confirms them on WhatsApp before dispatch.
  • Hide products that go out of stock and show them again on restock.

Customer segments are built in the admin from purchase history, location, spend, tags, subscription status and more. Use them as audiences for Shopify Email campaigns and automations (welcome, abandoned checkout, post-purchase, win-back) and to target discounts. Shopify Email includes a free monthly allowance and charges beyond it; check current pricing. Indian D2C brands usually pair email with WhatsApp, where engagement is higher; Shopify integrations for Indian brands covers the WhatsApp options.

Shopify Inbox adds storefront chat that shows the customer’s cart and order history to whoever replies, with automated answers for shipping, returns and order status. It is a good first support channel; brands with high chat volume usually move to a helpdesk or an AI chatbot later.

How do Bundles, discounts and Shopify Functions raise order value?

Shopify Bundles, discount combinations and Shopify Functions are the native tools for raising average order value without a stack of upsell apps. Shopify Bundles is a free Shopify app that sells fixed bundles (a skincare routine, a festive gift box) and multipacks (pack of 3) as single products, with inventory deducted from each component.

Native discounts cover percentage and fixed-amount offers, buy X get Y, free shipping and automatic discounts, and you control which discount classes can combine. Shopify Functions let developers write custom backend logic for discounts, payment methods, delivery options and cart validation: tiered “spend more, save more” offers, hiding COD above an order value or for certain pincodes, reordering delivery options, or blocking a cart below a B2B minimum. Public apps that use Functions work on all plans; custom apps containing Functions are limited to Shopify Plus.

The Indian checkout caveat

Many Indian stores run a third-party one-click checkout such as GoKwik, Shiprocket Checkout or Razorpay Magic Checkout. Functions that customise payment and delivery options act on Shopify checkout, so they may not apply on a replacement checkout, and support for bundles and discount logic varies by provider. Test every offer end to end on the checkout you actually use before a sale goes live; conversion rate optimisation work should include this check.

Which Shopify features help you sell across countries, cities and stores?

Shopify Markets

Shopify Markets lets one store sell to several countries or regions, each with its own domain or subfolder, language, pricing and product availability. For an Indian brand selling to the UAE, UK, US or Canada, it avoids running separate stores early on. One caution: local-currency checkout and some duty features depend on your payment setup, and Shopify Payments is not generally available to Indian-registered stores, so confirm what your gateway supports before launch. A Canadian or other international brand on Shopify Payments gets more of Markets out of the box.

Local pickup and local delivery

Local pickup lets buyers collect from a store; local delivery lets you define zones by pincode list or distance, each with its own minimum order and fee. It suits retailers, bakeries, pharmacies and any brand with city stock. See one website for multiple store locations and our multi-location Shopify and local delivery service.

Shopify POS

Shopify POS sells in stores, pop-ups and exhibitions from the same products, inventory and customer records as the website, so an exhibition buyer becomes a customer you can remarket to. Hardware and in-person payment options differ by country; Indian retailers should confirm what is supported locally and how GST billing at the counter will work.

Shopify B2B

Native B2B adds company accounts with multiple buyers and locations, catalogues with negotiated prices, volume pricing, quantity rules and payment terms, on the same store as D2C. The full native toolkit has been a Shopify Plus feature, so check current plan details before planning around it. It suits manufacturers and brands selling to retailers, salons or distributors.

What can you customise in Shopify checkout?

Every Shopify store can brand its checkout (logo, colours, fonts) and add app blocks to the thank-you and order status pages for surveys, upsells, tracking links or WhatsApp opt-ins. Shopify Plus stores can go further with checkout UI extensions inside the information, shipping and payment steps (delivery date pickers, gift messages, trust content, loyalty redemption) and with custom Shopify Functions. The old checkout.liquid method has been retired in favour of these extensions, which survive platform updates and stay fast.

For Indian stores the bigger decision is which checkout to run: Shopify checkout with an Indian payment gateway such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue or PhonePe, or a third-party one-click checkout with address prefill, COD controls and return-to-origin (RTO) prediction. The COD vs prepaid guide covers the economics, and Shopify Plus and headless development covers checkout extensions.

How useful are Shopify Magic and Sidekick?

Shopify Magic and Sidekick are useful time-savers for drafting and admin tasks, not replacements for brand writing or strategy. Shopify Magic is the set of AI features in the admin: drafting product descriptions, writing email copy, suggesting Inbox replies and editing product images. Sidekick is Shopify’s AI assistant in the admin; you ask in plain language (“which products sold most in Gujarat last month?”, “build a segment of customers who bought twice but not in 90 days”) and it answers or sets up the task.

  • Use Magic for first drafts across a large catalogue, then edit for voice, accuracy and claims, especially in beauty, food and health, where claims are regulated.
  • Use Sidekick to learn the admin, pull quick reports and set up segments, discounts or Flow workflows faster.
  • Never publish AI copy unread, and keep product facts in metafields so generated text cannot drift from them.

Availability and supported languages change often; check what is live for your store.

Which analytics and growth features should you check every week?

Shopify Analytics shows sales, sessions, the conversion funnel, top products, returning customer rate and traffic sources, with a live view for sale days; custom reports and deeper reporting grow with higher plans. Check a short list weekly:

  1. Conversion rate by device and traffic source.
  2. Sessions that added to cart, reached checkout and completed checkout, to see where buyers drop.
  3. Returning customer rate, and top products for first orders versus repeat orders.
  4. Searches with no results, in Search & Discovery.
  5. Sales by discount code, to see which offers pay for themselves.

Shopify’s numbers rarely match Meta or Google exactly; reconcile them weekly, as explained in the D2C ecommerce growth guide. On a third-party checkout, confirm that its orders and attribution reach Shopify reports correctly.

Shopify Collabs connects brands with creators, generates affiliate links and codes, and tracks sales and commission. It suits brands building an influencer or affiliate programme; check that it is available for your store’s country and how creator payouts work there before planning around it.

Which Shopify features should you switch on first?

Switch features on in order of revenue impact and effort. A practical sequence for most stores:

  1. Week 1: Search & Discovery filters and synonyms, complementary products for top sellers, abandoned checkout automation.
  2. Week 2: metafields for the details buyers ask about, a size guide or ingredient metaobject, product videos.
  3. Month 1: three to five Flow workflows (stock alerts, VIP tagging, COD review), core customer segments and a welcome email.
  4. Month 2: Bundles and tiered discounts, tested on the live checkout.
  5. When the business needs it: Markets, local delivery, POS, B2B and Plus checkout extensions.

Each native feature you adopt is a chance to remove an app, so audit the app list at the same time. For settings most stores miss, read hidden Shopify features most stores never switch on.
