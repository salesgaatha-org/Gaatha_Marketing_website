## Frequently asked questions

### What is quick commerce and which platforms matter in India?

Quick commerce (q-commerce) is delivery of groceries, FMCG, beauty and everyday items in roughly 10–30 minutes from local dark stores. In India the platforms that matter are Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket (BB Now).

### What does a quick commerce agency do?

A quick commerce agency gets a brand listed on q-commerce platforms and then runs the account: catalogue and images, city and dark-store availability, purchase orders and replenishment, promotions and ads, ratings and monthly reporting. gaa-tha does this for all major Indian platforms from one team.

### Which quick commerce platform should I launch on first?

Start where your category and cities overlap best. Blinkit and Zepto lead in metro grocery and FMCG; Swiggy Instamart adds strong food and impulse categories; BigBasket suits staples and larger packs. We map your SKUs and target cities against each platform's category demand before recommending an order.

### What documents do I need to sell on Blinkit or Zepto?

Typically GST registration, brand ownership proof or an authorisation letter, bank details, product and pack-size data, and category licences such as FSSAI for food. Requirements change by platform and category, so we share a current checklist before you apply.

### Are there fees to get listed on quick commerce platforms?

Platforms work on margins, listing or onboarding charges and sometimes marketing contributions, and these vary by category and change over time. We share the current structure for your category before you sign anything, and plan pack sizes and pricing so the margin works.
