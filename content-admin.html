Content & media

Upload and swap every image on the site, fill in case-study numbers and quotes, approve testimonials, and switch pages on or off. Text copy lives in the repository (content/); everything here is applied on top of it.

Drop images here or

JPG, PNG or WEBP. Each upload is converted to WebP at 480, 768, 1200 and 1920 px, EXIF is stripped, and alt text is required before the image can be placed.

Publish toggles take effect on the next site publish. Images you place here appear on the live page immediately and are baked into the HTML when you publish.
Only publish a case study after the client has approved it and the numbers are confirmed from real reports. Empty metrics and quotes stay hidden on the page.
Logos are used on the home and services logo walls, case-study cards and case-study heroes. Order is the logo-wall order.
The home-page quote block is hidden until at least one testimonial is approved.
Format: value · label. Applied by the build on the next publish.
How publishing works. Text lives in the repository; images, metrics, quotes and on/off toggles live here. Publishing rebuilds every page with both merged, regenerates the sitemap and llms.txt, and deploys to Netlify. Until then, images you place are still visible on the live page through the preview layer.

If the button reports that the publish hook is not configured, run the Deploy to Netlify workflow from the GitHub Actions tab instead — it does exactly the same thing. Setup notes are in workers/DEPLOY.md.
