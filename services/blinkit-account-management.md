## What is Blinkit Account Management?

Blinkit account management is the ongoing operation of a brand's account on Blinkit after go-live: keeping SKUs available across dark stores, forecasting and coordinating purchase orders, maintaining catalogue content, planning pricing and promotions, running Blinkit Ads, monitoring ratings and reconciling what Blinkit pays against what was supplied. On a 10-minute platform an out-of-stock SKU loses its search rank within days, so availability is managed weekly rather than monthly.

At gaa-tha a named account manager owns the Blinkit account and reports on one dashboard. We track availability and fill rate by city and dark store, keep the PO cycle moving with your warehouse, plan promotions around Blinkit's sale days and festivals, and run ads only where stock can support the demand. The month ends with a review that shows sales, availability, ad ROAS, ratings and reconciled settlements side by side.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you manage availability and fill rate on Blinkit?

We check availability at dark-store level every week, flag SKUs that are out of stock or low in any city, and measure supplier fill rate as quantity received against quantity ordered on each PO. Low availability is fixed by forecasting earlier and correcting quantities; low fill rate is fixed at your warehouse with packaging, labelling and dispatch discipline.

### How do purchase orders work on Blinkit and what do you handle?

Blinkit raises POs for its warehouses based on demand and current stock, and the brand dispatches within the PO window. We forecast expected POs from sell-through, follow up when a PO is late, coordinate quantities and packaging with your team, track dispatch and receipt, and log short receipts or rejections so they can be disputed.

### Do you maintain the catalogue and content?

Yes. Titles with pack size, thumbnails, secondary images, attributes and descriptions are kept current, new SKUs and pack variants are added, and listings are checked after every Blinkit template change. Content that stops a shopper in a two-second scroll is produced by the same in-house team.

### How do you handle pricing and promotions on Blinkit?

Every promotion is planned to margin first: we model the discount, the platform's share and the expected uplift before agreeing to a sale day or festive event. Pricing is also checked across Zepto, Instamart and your own store so one channel does not undercut another.

### Do you run Blinkit Ads?

Yes. Blinkit Ads offers product listing ads on search and category pages and banner placements, and we run them by SKU and city against a target ROAS. Ads are switched off in cities where the SKU is out of stock, because paying for clicks that cannot convert is the fastest way to waste budget on quick commerce.

### How do you manage ratings on Blinkit?

We monitor ratings and feedback weekly, separate product complaints from packaging and delivery issues, and route each to the right owner — your production team, your warehouse or Blinkit's support. Recurring themes are reported monthly so pack or label changes can be made.

### What does reconciliation involve?

Matching Blinkit's settlements to POs, goods receipt notes, short receipts, damages, returns, debit notes and marketing or ad invoices. Discrepancies are raised with Blinkit within the dispute window and tracked until closed, so your books reflect what was actually supplied and sold.

### What is in the monthly review?

Sales and units by city and SKU, availability and fill rate, PO status, catalogue changes, promotions run and their margin impact, Blinkit Ads spend and ROAS, ratings trends and the reconciliation summary — followed by decisions on SKUs, cities and budget for the next month.
