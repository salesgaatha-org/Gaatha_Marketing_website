The service

What is Local SEO & Google Business Profile?

Local SEO is the work of making a business appear when people nearby search for what it offers — in Google's map pack, on Google Maps and in "near me" results — by optimising its Google Business Profile, earning and answering reviews, keeping its name, address and phone number consistent across the web, and building location pages on its website. For a store, clinic, PG or showroom it is often the highest-return marketing there is.

At gaa-tha local SEO starts with the Google Business Profile, because that is what buyers see first: the right categories, complete services and products, current photos, weekly posts and a review process your staff can actually follow. For businesses with several outlets or cities we build a local landing page per location and manage every profile from one place.

Scope

What's included

  • Google Business Profile claim, verification and setup, or audit and clean-up of an existing profile
  • Primary and secondary categories, service areas, attributes, opening hours and booking or WhatsApp links
  • Services, products and menu sections written with the keywords people actually search
  • Photo and video uploads — exterior, interior, team, products — shot or art-directed by us
  • Weekly Google posts for offers, launches, events and festive hours
  • Review generation — QR codes, WhatsApp templates and staff scripts — plus replies to every review
  • NAP consistency: matching name, address and phone across Justdial, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Facebook and directories
  • Local landing pages on your website for each city, outlet or service area
  • Duplicate, suspension and ownership issue resolution with Google support
  • Monthly report on map views, searches, calls, direction requests and website clicks

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit

We check your profile against competitors in the map pack — categories, reviews, photos, posts and consistency — and record baseline calls, directions and views.

02

Setup and verification

The profile is claimed or cleaned up, verified with Google, and every section completed with the right categories, services, products, hours and links.

03

Content and photos

Fresh photos and short videos are shot and uploaded, service descriptions are rewritten with local keywords, and the first month of posts is scheduled.

04

Reviews and citations

A review process is set up with your staff — QR codes at the counter, WhatsApp follow-ups — and your listings are aligned across directories.

05

Report and maintain

Monthly reporting on map-pack visibility, calls and direction requests, with ongoing posts, review replies and Q&A handled on retainer.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Verified, fully completed Google Business Profile for each location
  • Photo and video set uploaded to the profile
  • Review-generation kit: QR code, WhatsApp templates and staff script
  • Citation and directory listing sheet with consistent business facts
  • Local landing pages for each outlet or city
  • Monthly local-visibility report

Good to know

Questions about Local SEO & GBP

Answer-first, so humans and AI assistants get the point quickly.

Local SEO is the practice of getting a business to appear in Google's map pack, on Google Maps and in "near me" searches for its area, mainly by optimising its Google Business Profile, earning reviews, keeping business details consistent across the web and building location pages. gaa-tha sets this up and runs it monthly for stores, showrooms, clinics, PGs and factories.

Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence. You control relevance (correct categories, complete services, keyword-rich descriptions) and prominence (review count and recency, photos, posts, consistent citations, links from your website). Distance you cannot change, which is why multi-outlet businesses need a profile per location.

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free, and so is appearing on Google Maps. What costs time is doing it properly and keeping it active; that is the part we handle, as a one-time setup or on a monthly retainer.

Ask at the right moment, make it effortless and reply to every one. We give your staff a QR code for the counter, WhatsApp follow-up templates for after purchase or delivery and a short script, and we respond to reviews within a day. We never buy or fake reviews; Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

Yes. We set up one verified profile per location, manage them from a single business group, keep hours, offers and photos consistent, and build a local landing page for each outlet on your website so every profile has a matching page to link to.

Profile setup and verification take about two to three weeks. Calls and direction requests usually start rising within the first one to three months as reviews, photos and posts accumulate, and the position holds as long as the profile stays active.

Verification is usually by video call, postcard or phone, and Google may ask for proof such as a signboard, business registration, utility bill or GST certificate showing the name and address. Requirements change by category, so we check the current method for your business before starting.

Usually, yes. Suspensions typically come from keyword-stuffed names, mismatched addresses, virtual offices or duplicate listings. We correct the profile to Google's guidelines, gather the evidence and file the reinstatement or merge request, then keep the profile compliant.
