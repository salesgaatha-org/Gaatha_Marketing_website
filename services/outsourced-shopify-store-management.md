## What is Outsourced Shopify Store Management?

Shopify store management is the ongoing operation of a live store: adding products and variants, changing prices and running promotions, updating collections and banners, keeping apps and the theme healthy, fixing bugs and keeping the store fast. Without it, launches wait for a developer and small issues cost sales.

gaa-tha provides it as a monthly retainer with a named store manager, a shared task board and agreed turnaround times. Because design, copy and paid media sit in the same team, a Black Friday sale or a product launch can be set up end to end. Agencies can resell the service under their own brand.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does outsourced Shopify store management include?

Product and price updates, promotions, homepage and collection changes, app management, bug fixes, speed checks, tracking checks and a monthly report. gaa-tha runs it as a monthly retainer with a named store manager working US business hours.

### How fast are changes made?

Routine changes such as a product upload, price change or banner are typically done within one to two working days, and urgent fixes the same day. Larger work such as a new landing page is scheduled on the task board with a date.

### Can you prepare our store for Black Friday and holiday sales?

Yes. Offer structure, sale collections, banners, countdowns, gift options and free-shipping thresholds are set up and tested ahead of the event and rolled back afterwards.

### Will you audit our apps?

Yes, at onboarding and every quarter. Unused and overlapping apps are a common cause of slow stores and rising monthly bills, so we list every app, its charge and purpose and remove what is redundant.

### Can our agency resell this under our brand?

Yes. We provide store management white-label: reports and documentation carry your agency's name and we work only through you, under an NDA and non-solicitation agreement.

### Do we keep ownership of the store?

Yes. The store, domain, apps and payment accounts stay in your name. We work through staff or collaborator accounts with only the permissions we need.
