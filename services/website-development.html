The service

What is Website Design & Development?

Website design and development is the work of planning, designing, writing and building a website that does a job for the business — earning enquiries, qualifying buyers, supporting a sales team or informing investors — and making sure it loads fast, works on every phone, can be read by search engines and AI assistants and can be updated without a developer. A website is a salesperson that never sleeps, and it should be built like one.

At gaa-tha every site starts with the visitor's question and the action we want them to take, then the structure, the copy and the design follow. We build on the existing brand or create one alongside, write answer-first content, add structured data, sitemaps and llms.txt, and ship with analytics and lead tracking connected — the same SEO, AEO and GEO approach we use on gaa-tha.com.

Scope

What's included

  • Discovery — goals, audiences, competitor sites, content inventory and the actions the site must drive
  • Sitemap, page structure and wireframes for desktop and mobile
  • UI design in your brand system, or a minimum viable identity created alongside
  • Answer-first copywriting and content structure for every page, with FAQs
  • Development — custom HTML/CSS/JS or WordPress, responsive, accessible and fast
  • Product or project catalogues, enquiry forms, WhatsApp and call buttons, maps and downloads
  • Technical SEO — semantic HTML, structured data, XML sitemap, robots and llms.txt
  • Analytics and lead tracking with Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Search Console
  • Domain, hosting, SSL and email setup or migration, with staging before go-live
  • Training and a simple admin so your team can update text and images

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Discovery and structure

We agree the site's job, the audiences and the actions, audit what exists, and produce the sitemap and wireframes.

02

Design

Key pages are designed for mobile and desktop in your brand system and approved before development starts.

03

Content

Copy is written answer-first for people and AI assistants, images and videos are shot, sourced or produced, and FAQs are drafted for the pages that need them.

04

Build and test

The site is developed, connected to forms, WhatsApp, analytics and search tools, and tested for speed, accessibility and every screen size on a staging link.

05

Launch and care

Domain, hosting and SSL are configured, the site goes live with sitemap and Search Console submission, your team is trained, and an optional care plan keeps it updated.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Sitemap, wireframes and approved page designs
  • Live, responsive website on your domain with SSL
  • Written page content and FAQs
  • Structured data, XML sitemap, robots.txt and llms.txt
  • Connected analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console and lead forms
  • Admin training and a handover document with all logins
