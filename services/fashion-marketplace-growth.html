Fashion & Licensed-Merch Marketplace Growth

gaa-tha builds growth plans for fashion and licensed-merchandise brands across Myntra, Flipkart, Nykaa Fashion, AJIO and Amazon: which styles go where, platform-specific content and imagery, a pricing and discount ladder that keeps margin through sale events, sponsored ads per platform, and monthly reviews of sell-through, returns and rank — one plan instead of five disconnected seller accounts.

The service

What is Fashion & Licensed-Merch Marketplace Growth?

Fashion marketplace growth is the practice of running a fashion or licensed-merchandise brand across several marketplaces as one business: deciding the assortment each platform gets, producing content and imagery to each platform's standard, setting a pricing and discount ladder that holds through End of Reason Sale, Big Billion Days and festive events, running sponsored ads per platform, and reviewing sell-through, returns and rank by style every month. For licensed merchandise it also covers licensor approvals, IP paperwork and drop calendars tied to releases.

At gaa-tha the plan starts with the style sheet and the margin, not the platform. A hero style may deserve model imagery and ads on Myntra, a value variant may belong on Flipkart and Shopsy, and a limited licensed drop may need a launch date, artwork approvals and stock held back for one platform's event. We run the accounts so these decisions are made deliberately and reviewed monthly.

Scope

What's included

  • Assortment plan: which styles, colours and price points go on Myntra, Flipkart, Nykaa Fashion, AJIO and Amazon
  • Platform-specific content: titles, attributes, size charts and imagery to each platform's template
  • Pricing and discount ladder for regular, event and end-of-season periods, with a margin floor per style
  • Sale-event calendar across platforms: End of Reason Sale, Big Fashion Festival, Big Billion Days, Big Bold Sale, Pink Friday and festive events
  • Sponsored ads per platform for hero styles and new drops, managed weekly
  • Licensed-merch workflow: licensor artwork and listing approvals, authorisation letters for marketplace IP checks, royalty-ready sales reporting
  • Drop planning for licensed releases and seasonal collections, with stock allocation by platform
  • Returns analysis by style and size, with fixes to size charts, imagery and packaging
  • Monthly sell-through, rank, returns and net-margin review by style and platform

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Range and margin review

We go through the style sheet, costs, current platform performance and, for licensed brands, the licence terms and approval process, and agree the number the plan is run against.

02

Assortment and pricing plan

Each style is assigned to the platforms where it can sell at margin, with a discount ladder that survives event pricing and a stock allocation by platform.

03

Content and catalogue

Listings, attributes, size charts and imagery are produced or fixed to each platform's standard, and licensed artwork and listings are cleared with the licensor before upload.

04

Ads, events and drops

Sponsored ads are set up per platform for hero styles, sale-event nominations are planned a season ahead, and licensed drops are scheduled around release dates.

05

Review and rebalance

Every month we review sell-through, returns, rank and net margin by style and platform, then move stock, budget and discounting to what is working.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Assortment and platform allocation plan by style
  • Pricing and discount ladder with margin floors
  • Cross-platform sale-event and drop calendar for the season
  • Platform-ready catalogues and imagery
  • Licensor approval log and authorisation documents for marketplaces
  • Monthly sell-through, returns and net-margin review by style and platform
