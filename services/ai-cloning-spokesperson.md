## What is AI Cloning (Digital Spokesperson)?

AI cloning, or a digital spokesperson, is a licensed digital twin of a real person — their face, voice and mannerisms captured with consent — that can deliver new scripts on camera without the person being filmed each time. It lets a founder appear in weekly explainers, product updates, festival greetings and ads in several languages while spending a single afternoon in front of a camera.

gaa-tha runs the service consent-first: a written agreement sets what the twin may say, where it may appear and who approves each video, and every output goes through that approval before it is published. Scripts are written by our content team in your tone of voice, produced with the twin in Hindi, Gujarati, English or other languages, and finished with captions, B-roll and brand graphics so the videos look like the rest of your content.

## How we work

1. **Consent and scope** — We agree in writing what the digital spokesperson will be used for, on which platforms, who approves each video, and how consent can be withdrawn.
2. **Capture** — A half-day session records the face and voice in the right conditions — scripted lines, natural conversation, a few expressions — so the twin looks and sounds like the person.
3. **Build and test** — The twin is created and tested with real scripts; the founder reviews and signs off on how it looks and sounds before any content is produced.
4. **Produce** — Each month scripts are written from your calendar, produced with the twin, finished with captions and graphics, and sent for approval.
5. **Publish and review** — Approved videos go out on your channels or into ads; we review performance monthly and adjust formats, lengths and languages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is AI cloning or a digital spokesperson?

It is a licensed digital twin of a real person, built from consented recordings of their face and voice, that can deliver new scripts on camera without a fresh shoot. Brands use it to publish weekly explainers, product updates, greetings and ads featuring their founder or a brand ambassador. gaa-tha sets it up in one to two weeks and produces the monthly videos.

### Is AI cloning ethical and legal?

It is, when it is done with the person's informed written consent, used within the agreed scope, and disclosed where the platform or law requires. Our process starts with a consent and usage agreement, keeps approval with the person or the brand on every output, and lets consent be withdrawn. We do not clone anyone without their agreement.

### Will viewers be able to tell it is AI?

Modern twins are convincing for talking-head formats, especially with good capture footage and a script written in the person's natural speech. We recommend disclosing the use of a digital spokesperson in your bio or captions where appropriate; audiences respond well to honesty, and the content still saves the founder's time.

### What do we need for the setup?

One person willing to be captured, a half-day recording session, a few examples of how they normally speak, your brand guidelines and a first month's list of topics. Setup takes one to two weeks from capture to sign-off.

### Can the spokesperson speak Hindi, Gujarati and other languages?

Yes. The twin can deliver scripts in Hindi, Gujarati, English and other languages with matching lip-sync, which is useful for brands selling across states or to export markets. A native speaker checks each language version before approval.

### What can and cannot the twin be used for?

That is defined in the agreement. Typical scope is brand content, explainers, ads and internal training; typical exclusions are political content, endorsements of other brands, and anything the person would not say themselves. The founder or brand approves every video before it is published.

### Who owns the digital twin?

The person and the brand, as set out in the agreement. gaa-tha holds the working files under access control only for the duration of the engagement, and the twin is never used for another client or purpose.

### Does this replace real shoots?

It replaces the daily grind of being on camera, not the moments that need to be real — a factory walk, a customer meeting, a launch. Most brands pair a monthly real shoot for reels with the digital spokesperson for the weekly updates in between.
