From ad leads to real buyers — a B2B snack manufacturer's content and lead engine.

Meta lead campaigns with a self-filtering WhatsApp reply system, a lead-management SOP and tracker, social content and a cinematic product film for a bulk peanut and white-label snacks manufacturer.

The brief

What the client needed

Ghanshyam Snacks manufactures bulk peanuts and white-label snacks for B2B buyers. Paid campaigns were producing a high volume of leads, but many were fake or low-quality, and the sales team was spending its day filtering rather than selling.

Alongside the lead problem, the brand needed B2B positioning, a proper product catalogue and consistent social content.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Meta ads & lead generation

Lead campaigns rebuilt with a WhatsApp first-reply system designed so fake leads filter themselves out before a human replies, plus a B2B reply template for IndiaMART enquiries.

Meta ads & lead generation · 16:10

Lead-management SOP & tracker

A five-stage pipeline — capture, 24-hour qualification, quote, Day 1/3/7 follow-up, weekly review — with an Excel/Sheets dashboard showing qualification rate, conversion rate, pipeline value and junk percentage by ad source.

Lead-management SOP & tracker · 16:10

Social media

50-post content sets and reel production briefs so the brand's Instagram reads as a manufacturer buyers can trust.

Social media · 16:10

Brand & sales assets

A product catalogue, B2B positioning material and IndiaMART listing assets built to the same visual system.

Brand & sales assets · 16:10

Cinematic product film

A sand-roasted peanut macro ad sequence — the product as the hero, shot to make a commodity look premium.

Cinematic product film · 16:10

Impact & results

What changed

The lead pipeline now has a qualification step, a follow-up rhythm and a dashboard that shows which ad sources produce buyers and which produce noise. Figures for leads, qualification rate, junk reduction and conversions are published once confirmed from the tracker.
