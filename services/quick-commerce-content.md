## What is Quick Commerce Catalogue & Creative?

Quick commerce catalogue and creative is the product content that makes an SKU findable and clickable inside a 10-minute delivery app: the thumbnail, the title with pack size, secondary images, attributes and description, plus the combo and multipack listings and banner creative around them. On quick commerce the shopper scrolls a grid of small tiles on a phone and decides in about two seconds, so a thumbnail has to show the product, the variant and the size at a glance, and a title has to carry the pack size because that is how people search.

gaa-tha builds this content to each platform's specifications from one master library, so the same SKU looks consistent on Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket without six separate shoots. Packshots come from studio photography or AI-assisted production for large catalogues, thumbnails are tested for legibility at grid size, and combo and pack strategy is planned with your margin so the listing that gets the click is also the one that makes money.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes a good thumbnail on Blinkit, Zepto or Instamart?

One that reads at grid size on a phone: a clean packshot filling the frame, the variant and pack size visible, a plain background that meets the platform's specification and no clutter. Shoppers decide in about two seconds, so the thumbnail's job is recognition, not persuasion.

### Why should the pack size be in the title?

Because shoppers search and compare by size — 200 g, 1 L, pack of 3 — and the app truncates titles on small tiles, so the size has to come early. Titles that lead with brand, product, variant and pack size are also easier for the platform's search to match.

### Do you follow each platform's image specifications?

Yes. Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket each have their own dimensions, background and content rules, and listings that break them fail QC or get suppressed. We build from one master file and export to each specification so the SKU stays consistent everywhere.

### What is combo and pack strategy on quick commerce?

Deciding which sizes, multipacks and bundles to list so the basket value works for you and the platform: a trial size for first purchase, a regular size for repeat, and a combo or multipack that lifts order value. We plan it with your margin and the price points that move in your category, and check it against what sells on Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart.

### Can you photograph a large catalogue quickly?

Yes. For ranges of dozens to hundreds of SKUs we combine a studio shoot for hero products with AI-assisted packshot production for the long tail, keeping art direction human so everything looks like one brand. This is usually the fastest way to get a full catalogue live without a multi-week shoot.

### Do you upload the content to the platforms?

Yes, where we manage the account, and otherwise we hand over everything in each platform's upload template with a checklist. Either way we recheck the live listings after upload, because platforms crop, compress and reorder images differently.

### How often should quick commerce content be refreshed?

Review monthly, refresh when a thumbnail underperforms, a pack changes, a festival or season approaches, or the platform updates its template. Combos and seasonal variants usually need new tiles each quarter, and a stale thumbnail is one of the quieter reasons a listing loses rank.
