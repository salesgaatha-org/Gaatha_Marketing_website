The brief

What the client needed

CDS Stores sells premium sweets, dry fruits, hampers and corporate gifting in Vapi and Valsad. Diwali is the season that decides the year, and the brief was simple to say and hard to do: be the first name people think of for gifting in both cities, on a total budget of ₹2 lakh.

That meant one message carried across outdoor, influencers, Meta ads and a cinema screen — planned as one campaign, not four.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Campaign planning

A 6 October – 14 November campaign with the budget split across outdoor banners (store-front, auto-rickshaw and bus panels), influencer collaborations (15–20 collab reels and 5 page collaborations) and Meta ads, with one key visual and one calendar.

Campaign planning · 16:10

Outdoor creative

Premium, minimal category banners for sweets, hampers, dry fruits, corporate gifting and the live sweets counter — each category with its own colour code so the auto-rickshaw panels read from across the road.

Outdoor creative · 16:10

Cinema ad

A 10-second trailer-style AI film: products drop into frame with bold title cards — sweets, hampers, dry fruits, namkeen, chocolates, live sweets, bulk gifting — then the storefront and a closing card with locations and numbers, letterboxed to cinema scope.

Cinema ad · 16:10

AI film (style reference)

An earlier Ganpati festive AI ad set the visual language for the campaign and became the reference for the Diwali film.

AI film (style reference) · 16:10

Shopify

Ongoing support for the cdsstores.in store so online hamper and gifting orders had a clean path during the campaign.

Shopify · 16:10

Meta ads & social

Festive Meta campaigns targeted to Vapi and Valsad alongside the influencer content, with click-to-WhatsApp for bulk and corporate enquiries.

Meta ads & social · 16:10
