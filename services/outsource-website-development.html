The service

What is Outsource Website Development to India?

Outsourcing website development to India means hiring an India-based team to design, build and maintain websites while you keep ownership of the strategy, the accounts and the code. Done well, it gives you senior development capacity with clear communication and documentation; done badly, it creates code nobody can maintain.

gaa-tha builds websites for its own clients, including a managed ecommerce site for a retailer in Canada, and works as a development partner for agencies. Every project starts with a written scope, runs on preview links and a shared task board during US business hours, and ends with code in your GitHub and documentation your next developer can follow.

Scope

What's included

  • Discovery, sitemap and written scope before any design
  • UX and UI design in Figma, or development from your designs
  • WordPress, Webflow, Shopify or custom Next.js builds
  • Responsive, accessible front-end with WCAG basics checked
  • Technical SEO: metadata, schema, redirects, sitemaps and Core Web Vitals
  • CMS training so your team can edit content
  • Code in your GitHub, hosting and domains in your name
  • Post-launch support window and optional maintenance retainer

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Discovery

Goals, audience, pages, integrations and success measures agreed in writing.

02

Design

Wireframes and Figma designs, or a build plan from your existing designs.

03

Build

Development on a staging site with weekly preview links and a shared task board.

04

QA and launch

Device, browser, speed, accessibility and SEO checks, then launch with redirects.

05

Support

A fix window after launch and an optional monthly maintenance retainer.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Written scope and sitemap
  • Figma designs (when design is in scope)
  • Production website with CMS access
  • Technical SEO and redirect map
  • Handover documentation and training
