Graphic Design

The service

What is Graphic Design?

Graphic design is the craft of arranging type, colour, image and space so a message is understood quickly and trusted — on a social post, a hoarding, a product label or a sales deck. Good design is not decoration; it decides what a person reads first, what they feel about the brand, and whether they act.

gaa-tha runs graphic design as a production line with an art director at the front. Every brief starts from the brand guidelines and the campaign idea, is designed once for the lead format, then adapted correctly to every size — Instagram carousel, WhatsApp creative, auto-rickshaw panel, bus shelter, standee, catalogue page — so a festive campaign in Surat or Ahmedabad looks the same on the road as it does on the phone.

Scope

What's included

  • Social media posts, carousels and stories for Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook
  • WhatsApp broadcast creatives and status designs
  • Hoardings, banners and outdoor adaptations
  • Auto-rickshaw, bus and other transit creatives
  • Standees, danglers, shelf talkers and in-store POS
  • Catalogues, brochures and leaflets
  • Product labels and packaging artwork on print-ready dielines
  • Presentations and sales decks
  • Infographics and explainer visuals
  • Print-ready files with bleed, CMYK conversion and printer coordination

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Brief

Each request comes in on a shared tracker with the objective, the message, the formats and the deadline. We ask the questions that stop revisions later.

02

Concept

The lead creative is designed first — the hero post, the master hoarding or the catalogue cover — and shared for one round of direction.

03

Adapt

The approved concept is adapted to every size and surface with correct safe areas, resolution and colour profile for screen or print.

04

Review

Two rounds of revisions are built into every set; copy, prices, dates and mandatory text are checked against the brief before release.

05

Release and archive

Final files go out in the formats each channel needs, and everything is filed in your shared brand library so it can be reused.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Monthly creative sets aligned to a content calendar
  • Print-ready PDFs with bleed and crop marks for hoardings, transit and POS
  • Social-ready exports in every platform size
  • Editable source files in Figma, Illustrator or Canva
  • A shared brand asset library organised by campaign
  • Packaging artwork on printer dielines with a proof approval

Questions about Graphic design

A graphic design agency turns a brand's messages into visual assets: social posts, ads, hoardings, packaging, catalogues, decks and infographics, designed to the brand's guidelines and to the technical specs of each channel. gaa-tha runs this as an ongoing retainer for brands that need creative every week, and per project for launches and festive campaigns.

It depends on the mix: a retainer might cover twenty to forty social creatives plus a few print or outdoor adaptations, while a catalogue or packaging job is scoped separately. We agree a monthly volume and a turnaround of two to seven days per set, and scale up around Diwali, Navratri or a launch.
