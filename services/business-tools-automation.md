## What is Custom Tools & Automations?

Custom business tools and automations are small, purpose-built pieces of software that remove a specific piece of manual work — logging leads and chasing follow-ups, letting a customer check a batch number, compiling a daily sales report, or producing quotes and catalogues from a price list — usually by connecting the systems a business already uses. They are not an ERP; they are the missing piece between the ERP, the spreadsheet and WhatsApp.

At gaa-tha a tool starts with the task that is wasting the most hours, not with a technology. We map how the work happens today, agree the smallest tool that fixes it, build it on plain web technology and the services you already pay for (Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Firebase, your billing software), and hand it over with documentation and your own logins. Most tools are live within a few weeks and pay for themselves in saved time.

## How we work

1. **Find the bottleneck** — A short session with the people doing the work to map the current process and pick the one task where a tool saves the most time or errors.
2. **Scope the smallest tool** — We write a one-page scope — inputs, outputs, users, integrations — with a fixed price and a timeline, and agree what is out of scope for version one.
3. **Build and connect** — The tool is built on plain web technology and connected to the sheets, WhatsApp, billing or CRM systems you already use.
4. **Test with the team** — The people who will use it try it on real data, and the rough edges are fixed before launch.
5. **Launch and support** — The tool goes live with documentation, training and your own logins, with optional monthly support for changes and new features.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are custom business tools and automations?

Small, purpose-built software that removes manual work: a lead tracker with a WhatsApp follow-up SOP, a product batch-number lookup for customers, a sales dashboard pulling from Google Sheets, or automated quotes and catalogues generated from a price list. gaa-tha scopes each one to a single task, builds it in weeks and hands it over with your own logins.

### Which tasks are worth automating first?

The ones that happen every day, involve copying data between two places and go wrong when someone is on leave — lead logging and follow-up, daily sales reports, quotes and proforma invoices, dispatch updates to customers. We map your process in a short session and rank tasks by hours saved and errors avoided.

### Do we need to change our software, Tally or CRM?

No. Tools are built to connect to what you already use — Google Sheets, Tally, Zoho, WhatsApp Business, Shopify, marketplaces — through their APIs or exports, or through Zapier and Make. If a system has no way to connect, we design around a simple export rather than asking you to migrate.

### How long does it take to build a tool?

Scoping takes a few days; most single-purpose tools are live in two to six weeks. A lead tracker or lookup tool sits at the short end; a dashboard with several data sources or a document generator with many templates at the longer end.

### How much does a custom tool cost?

Each tool is quoted as a fixed fee from a one-page scope, so the price is known before work starts and depends on the integrations, the number of users and screens, and whether you want ongoing support. Keeping version one small is how we keep the fee small.

### Who owns the code and the data?

You do. The tool runs on accounts in your name, the code and documentation are handed over at launch, and your data stays in your Google Workspace, Firebase project or server. There is no lock-in to gaa-tha.

### Will it work on mobile for our sales team in the field?

Yes. Tools are built as responsive web apps that work on any phone browser and can be added to the home screen, with WhatsApp as the notification channel because that is where your team already is.

### Is our customer and sales data secure?

Tools use role-based access, HTTPS, per-user logins and backups, and run on Google or Firebase infrastructure or your own hosting. We keep customer data inside your accounts rather than ours, and document who has access to what at handover.
