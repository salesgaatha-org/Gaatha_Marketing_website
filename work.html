Stories we've told.

Reels, websites and the brands we grow all year — 360° retainer case studies across campaigns, ecommerce, quick commerce, print and in-store. Pick a universe and dive in.

gaa-tha is a 360° marketing agency in India that works with brands on long-term retainers. For FMCG and snack brands, gifting retail, D2C skincare, coatings and packaging manufacturers, co-living and an international trading-card retailer in Canada, gaa-tha builds and manages the website, runs Meta and Google campaigns, manages Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, produces print, outdoor and in-store branding, and runs lead generation, founder personal branding and international campaigns. Each case study covers the full scope of work for one brand.
