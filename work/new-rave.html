The brief

What the client needed

New Rave (newrave.ca) sells trading-card games and hobby products from a physical store and a website. The community is knowledgeable and fast-moving: launches, pre-orders and singles matter, and generic retail content is ignored.

The brief was to grow footfall in the store and sales on the website with Instagram content that spoke to collectors, plus the storefront policies a serious TCG retailer needs.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Social strategy

Four content pillars: new launches and pre-orders, card features and USPs, direct brand promotion, and witty community posts that collectors actually share.

Social strategy · 16:10

Content production

Static and carousel posts built on the retailer's supplied product mockups and brand assets, with AI-generated visual frames — never third-party card artwork — so every post stayed on-brand and cleared for use.

Content production · 16:10

Website support

A full storefront policy set written for the category: shipping, in-store pickup, pre-orders, TCG singles, fraud prevention and grading.
