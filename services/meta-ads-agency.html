The service

What is Meta Ads Agency?

Meta ads management is the ongoing work of running paid campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger: setting up the ad account and tracking, deciding who sees which creative, testing hooks and offers, and moving budget to what converts. Done well it is a system, not a boost button.

At gaa-tha the account is built around your unit economics first. We agree the number that matters — cost per lead, cost per purchase or blended ROAS — and structure campaigns, audiences and creative testing to hit it, with a lead-quality process so sales teams are not chasing junk.

Scope

What's included

  • Ad account, Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI) setup and audit
  • Audience and campaign architecture (prospecting, retargeting, retention)
  • Creative testing framework — hooks, formats and offers tested in batches
  • Lead-generation campaigns with instant forms and WhatsApp click-to-chat
  • Sales campaigns including catalogue and Advantage+ shopping
  • Retargeting across site visitors, engagers and customer lists
  • Weekly optimisation of budgets, bids, placements and exclusions
  • Lead-quality SOPs, first-reply templates and a lead tracker
  • Monthly report on CPL, cost per purchase, ROAS/MER and lead-to-sale rate

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and targets

We review the account, pixel, past campaigns and margins, then agree the one number the account is run against.

02

Tracking and structure

Pixel and CAPI are fixed or set up, events verified, and the campaign architecture built for prospecting, retargeting and retention.

03

Creative and offers

Reels, statics, carousels and copy variations are produced in batches against a testing plan — with a clear offer on every ad.

04

Launch and optimise

Campaigns go live with daily monitoring in week one, then weekly optimisation of budgets, audiences and creative rotation.

05

Report and scale

Monthly reporting against the target, lead-quality review with your sales team, and scaling rules for what is working.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Configured ad account with verified Pixel and Conversions API
  • Campaign architecture document and naming conventions
  • Monthly creative batches (reels, statics, carousels, copy)
  • Lead-quality SOP and lead tracker (Sheets/CRM)
  • Weekly optimisation log and monthly performance report
