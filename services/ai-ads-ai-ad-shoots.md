## What is AI Ads & AI Ad Shoots?

An AI ad shoot is the production of product, model and lifestyle imagery or video using generative AI tools instead of a physical set, camera crew and location, with a human art director controlling composition, light, styling and brand consistency. The output is used the same way a photoshoot would be: ads, social posts, listings, catalogues and outdoor.

gaa-tha's pipeline starts with your real product — its pack, its colours, its details — and builds scenes around it: a kitchen in Mumbai, a Diwali table, a monsoon street, a studio with soft light. Each campaign set is art-directed, graded and typeset to your guidelines, checked for pack accuracy, and delivered in every ad size, with variations so the performance team can test hooks and offers without waiting for a reshoot.

## How we work

1. **Brief and references** — We collect your products, pack files, brand guidelines and campaign objective, and agree the mood, scenes and number of variations.
2. **Concept boards** — Two or three visual directions are generated as boards for you to choose from before full production starts.
3. **Produce** — The chosen direction is produced at scale — hero shots, lifestyle scenes, models, motion — with the art director controlling consistency.
4. **Retouch and typeset** — Every image is checked for pack accuracy and artefacts, then retouched, graded and typeset with offer, price and CTA where needed.
5. **Deliver and test** — Files go out in every size with variation sets labelled for testing; performance data comes back to shape the next batch.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are AI ads and AI ad shoots?

AI ads are ad creatives — images, videos, variations — produced with generative AI tools under human art direction instead of a physical photoshoot. An AI ad shoot is the same idea applied to a whole campaign or catalogue: consistent scenes, models and lighting across dozens of assets. gaa-tha produces both and pairs them with its performance team so the creatives are tested, not just delivered.

### Will the product look exactly like our real product?

Yes, that is the point of the process. We start from your actual pack files and product photography, generate the scene around the product rather than the product itself where accuracy matters, and run a pack check on labels, logos, colours and proportions before anything is released. Mandatory declarations are never altered.

### How is this different from stock photos or templates?

Stock is generic and used by everyone; templates look like templates. An AI ad shoot is art-directed for your brand, your product and your campaign, with scenes, models and light chosen for your audience in India, and the assets are exclusive to you.

### Can you make AI videos as well as images?

Yes. Product reveals, motion loops, lifestyle clips and short video ads are produced with AI video tools and finished in the edit with sound, captions and brand typography. For longer or story-led films see our cinema and film ads service, which often blends AI and live-action.

### How long does an AI ad shoot take?

Three to ten days from brief to delivery depending on the number of scenes, variations and video pieces. Concept boards usually arrive within two to three days so the direction is agreed early.

### Do you still do real photoshoots?

Yes. Some products, faces and moments need a camera — a founder, a store, a texture that has to be exact — and we plan and direct those shoots too. Most brands end up with a blend: a real hero shoot plus AI sets for volume, seasons and testing.

### Is it safe to use AI-generated ads in India?

Generally yes for imagery you commission and own, as long as the ad is truthful, follows the ASCI advertising code, and does not use a real person's likeness without consent. We use licensed tools, avoid real-person likenesses unless there is a signed consent, and label AI-generated spokespersons where a platform or regulator requires it.

### Can AI creatives be used on marketplaces and quick commerce?

Yes, provided the main image follows each platform's rules — for example a plain white background and no added text on Amazon's primary image — and the product itself is accurately shown. We produce compliant primary images and use AI lifestyle and infographic images in the secondary slots and A+ content.
