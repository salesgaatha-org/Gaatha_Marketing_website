# Shopify vs WooCommerce in India: which should you build on?

> For most Indian D2C brands, Shopify is the better choice: faster to launch, easier to maintain, and well supported by Indian checkout, payment and logistics integrations. WooCommerce suits brands that want full control, already run WordPress, or want to avoid subscription costs and can manage hosting and updates. The decision rests on who will maintain the store and how much customisation the business genuinely needs.

## Key takeaways

- Shopify: hosted, fast, reliable, subscription plus app costs.
- WooCommerce: flexible and free at the core, but you own hosting, security and updates.
- Indian checkout and payment integrations are strong on both, with Shopify’s ecosystem more turnkey.
- Choose by maintenance capacity and customisation needs, not by platform loyalty.

## How do Shopify and WooCommerce differ in practice?

| Aspect | Shopify | WooCommerce |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting and security | Included and managed | Your responsibility or your host’s |
| Speed to launch | Days to weeks | Weeks, depending on theme and plugins |
| Customisation | Themes, Liquid, custom sections and apps | Unlimited through code and plugins |
| Cost structure | Monthly subscription plus apps and payment fees | Free core; hosting, plugins, development and maintenance |
| Maintenance | Low | Ongoing updates and compatibility checks |

Plans and fees change; check current pricing for both before deciding.

## Which handles Indian payments, checkout and logistics better?

Both support UPI, cards, wallets and COD through Indian payment gateways, and both integrate with shipping aggregators. Shopify’s ecosystem of one-click checkout providers, COD verification tools and pincode logic is more plug-and-play; WooCommerce can do all of it with plugins and some development. For brands that want a smooth checkout on day one with minimal engineering, Shopify is the path of least resistance. ## When does WooCommerce or a custom build make more sense?

When the business already runs a content-heavy WordPress site and wants commerce inside it; when the catalogue or pricing logic is unusual (complex B2B pricing, configurators, quotation flows); when the team has developers who will maintain the stack; or when subscription costs matter more than time. A manufacturer selling to trade buyers with tiered pricing may fit here better than on Shopify.

## What should decide it for your brand?

Ask who will maintain the store in month six, how many custom features are truly required, and how quickly you need to launch. A consumer brand with a standard catalogue and a small team should choose Shopify; a business with technical capacity and unusual requirements should consider WooCommerce or custom. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Shopify expensive for a small Indian brand?

Shopify charges a monthly subscription plus payment processing and any app fees; the amounts change, so check current plans. For most small brands the time saved on hosting, security and maintenance offsets the subscription.

### Is WooCommerce really free?

The core plugin is free, but a working store needs hosting, a theme, plugins, development and ongoing maintenance, which together are rarely free. Budget for those honestly before comparing.

### Can I move from WooCommerce to Shopify later?

Yes. Products, customers and orders can be migrated, and URLs redirected. It is easier to migrate early than after years of custom plugins, so decide carefully at the start.

### Which platform is better for SEO?

Both can rank well when built properly. WooCommerce offers more control over technical details; Shopify covers the essentials by default. Content, structure and speed decide results more than the platform.
