## What is White-Label Digital Agency Partner?

A white-label agency partner is a team that delivers work your agency sells under your own brand. Your client sees your agency's name, your reports and your project manager; the partner does the production behind the scenes. It lets an agency say yes to Shopify builds, app projects, paid media or video work without hiring a specialist for every skill.

gaa-tha works this way for agencies in the US and UK. We sign your NDA before we see a brief, work unbranded inside your Slack, project board and client accounts, never contact your client unless you ask us to, and agree not to solicit your clients. Because our team already runs brand, ecommerce, development and growth work for its own clients, you get one partner for several capabilities instead of a string of freelancers.

## How we work

1. **Intro call and NDA** — A call in your business hours to understand your agency, your clients and the work you want to hand over, followed by your NDA or ours.
2. **Brief and scope** — You share a real brief. We return a written scope, timeline, team and quality checklist in your format.
3. **Paid pilot** — One fixed-scope project or batch so you can judge quality, speed and communication before committing.
4. **Retainer or dedicated team** — We agree a monthly capacity or a dedicated team, the tools we work in, turnaround times and how requests come in.
5. **Review and scale** — A monthly review of quality, turnaround and upcoming pipeline, so capacity grows with your client list.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a white-label agency partner?

A white-label agency partner delivers work that your agency sells under its own brand. Your client deals only with your agency; the partner produces the work behind the scenes, unbranded, in your tools. gaa-tha does this for US and UK agencies across Shopify, websites, apps, paid ads, social media and video editing.

### Will my clients know you exist?

No, unless you want them to. Deliverables, reports and documentation carry your agency's branding, we work from your accounts and channels, and we never contact your client directly. If you prefer to bring us onto a client call as part of your team, we join under your agency's name.

### Do you work US business hours?

Yes. Our team works US business hours, so we can join your stand-ups, answer Slack during your day and ship launches on your schedule. UK agencies get overlap across the UK working day as well.

### How do you protect our client relationships?

We sign an NDA before seeing any client details, agree in writing not to solicit or accept work from your clients, and keep all communication through your agency. Accounts, code and files stay in your or your client's name.

### Can we test you before signing a retainer?

Yes. We start every agency relationship with a paid pilot: one fixed-scope project or batch, such as a Shopify section build, a month of ad management for one account or a batch of reels. You judge the work before committing to monthly capacity.

### How do we pay you from the US or UK?

We invoice internationally and accept bank wire (SWIFT), PayPal and card payments through Stripe. Pricing is quoted per project or as a monthly retainer once we have seen a real brief.

### Which services can we white-label?

Shopify store builds and management, website and app development, ERP and Odoo work, Meta and Google ads management, social media management, reel and short-form video editing, and marketing strategy support. Most agencies start with one service and add others once the pilot goes well.
