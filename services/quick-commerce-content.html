The service

What is Quick Commerce Catalogue & Creative?

Quick commerce catalogue and creative is the product content that makes an SKU findable and clickable inside a 10-minute delivery app: the thumbnail, the title with pack size, secondary images, attributes and description, plus the combo and multipack listings and banner creative around them. On quick commerce the shopper scrolls a grid of small tiles on a phone and decides in about two seconds, so a thumbnail has to show the product, the variant and the size at a glance, and a title has to carry the pack size because that is how people search.

gaa-tha builds this content to each platform's specifications from one master library, so the same SKU looks consistent on Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket without six separate shoots. Packshots come from studio photography or AI-assisted production for large catalogues, thumbnails are tested for legibility at grid size, and combo and pack strategy is planned with your margin so the listing that gets the click is also the one that makes money.

Scope

What's included

  • Catalogue audit against each platform's image, title and attribute specifications
  • Thumbnails designed for grid legibility: product, variant and pack size readable at small sizes
  • Titles with pack size, variant and key claim written to match how shoppers search on each platform
  • Secondary images: back-of-pack, ingredients or usage, size comparison and benefit callouts
  • Attributes, descriptions and category mapping completed for every SKU
  • Combo, multipack and pack-size strategy planned with margin and platform price points
  • Banner and category-page creative for launches, sale events and quick-commerce ads
  • Packshots via studio shoot or AI-assisted production for large or fast-changing ranges
  • Master content library so every platform and your own store use the same approved assets

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and specifications

We review your current listings against each platform's requirements and how competitors in your category appear in the grid.

02

Pack and combo strategy

Pack sizes, combos and multipacks are planned with your margin and the price points that move on quick commerce.

03

Shoot and design

Packshots are produced in studio or with AI-assisted production, then thumbnails and secondary images designed and checked at grid size.

04

Titles and attributes

Titles with pack size, descriptions, attributes and category mapping are written per platform and uploaded or handed over in the platform's template.

05

Test and refresh

Click-through and conversion are watched after upload, thumbnails revised where they underperform, and new SKUs, combos and festive variants added monthly.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Catalogue audit with platform-by-platform fixes
  • Thumbnail and secondary-image sets for every SKU, in each platform's format
  • Title, description and attribute sheet per platform
  • Combo and pack-size plan with price points
  • Banner creative sets for launches and sale events
  • Master content library with approved assets
