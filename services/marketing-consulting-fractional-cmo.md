## What is Marketing Consulting & Fractional CMO?

Marketing consulting is senior, outside advice on what to sell to whom, through which channels, with what budget and how to measure it. A fractional CMO goes further: a part-time marketing leader who owns the plan, directs the team or agencies and reviews results every month.

gaa-tha's founders lead strategy and brand thinking for the studio's clients in India and Canada. We offer the same as a consulting project or monthly fractional retainer for US and UK brands, and because we also execute, the plan can be delivered by our team or handed to yours.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a fractional CMO do?

A fractional CMO is a part-time marketing leader who sets strategy, owns the plan and budget, directs the team and agencies and reviews results monthly. gaa-tha offers this to US and UK brands on a monthly retainer.

### Is marketing consulting worth it before hiring an agency?

Usually, yes. A clear strategy, KPIs and channel plan make every agency or hire more effective, and stop budget being spent on channels that do not fit your buyers.

### Can you also execute the plan?

Yes. Our in-house team runs ads, social, content, Shopify and web work, but the plan is written so your own team or other agencies can deliver it too.

### Do you consult for agencies?

Yes. Agencies use us for strategy support on pitches and client plans, delivered under the agency's name.

### How do sessions work across time zones?

We work US business hours, so working sessions and reviews happen during your day by video call.

### How do we start?

With an audit and strategy project. If it is useful, it becomes a monthly fractional retainer.
