## What is Copywriting & Content Writing?

Copywriting is writing whose job is to make someone act — click, enquire, add to cart, reply — while content writing is writing whose job is to inform and build trust over time, such as articles, guides and FAQs. Both need the same things: a clear understanding of the buyer, a specific promise, and language plain enough to be quoted.

gaa-tha writes both from a single brand voice document, so a product bullet on Amazon, a Meta ad, a reel script and a blog answer all sound like the same company. Every piece is written answer-first and entity-rich so it reads well to a buyer, ranks on Google, and can be cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity — the same approach we use on gaa-tha.com.

## How we work

1. **Voice and audience** — We read what you already have, interview the founders or sales team, and write a short tone of voice and messaging document everyone works from.
2. **Research** — For each piece we gather product facts, customer questions, competitor language and search terms, so the writing is specific rather than generic.
3. **Write** — First drafts are written answer-first, with headlines, hooks and CTAs, and structured for the channel — a listing, a landing page, a script or an article.
4. **Review** — Two rounds of revisions with your team; facts, claims and mandatory text are checked, and product data is reconciled to your master sheet.
5. **Publish and measure** — Copy is handed over in the format each platform needs, or uploaded by our teams, and we watch rankings, click-through and conversion to refine it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between copywriting and content writing?

Copywriting persuades: ads, landing pages, product listings, emails, scripts. Content writing informs and builds trust: articles, guides, FAQs, case studies. Most brands need both, and gaa-tha writes both from the same tone of voice document so the brand sounds consistent.

### Can you write product listings for hundreds of SKUs?

Yes. We work from your master product sheet, write titles, bullets and descriptions to each marketplace's character limits and keyword needs, and deliver them in a bulk-upload-ready sheet. Catalogues of 200 or more SKUs are handled in weekly batches with a style guide so every listing reads the same.

### Do you write for SEO and AI search?

Yes. Every article, service page and FAQ is written answer-first, with clear headings, named entities and structured data in mind, so it ranks on Google and can be cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Our SEO, AEO and GEO team sets the keywords and questions; the writers make the answers worth quoting.

### Do you use AI to write?

We use AI tools for research, outlines, variations and first-pass translation, and human writers for the thinking, the facts, the voice and the final draft. Nothing is published without a person checking claims, tone and accuracy, and copy is never left sounding generic.

### Can you write in Hindi and Gujarati?

Yes. We write and transcreate in Hindi and Gujarati for ads, packaging, reels and WhatsApp campaigns, with native speakers reviewing each version, which matters for brands selling in Gujarat and across tier-2 India.

### How long does website copy take?

One to three weeks for a corporate or D2C site, depending on the number of pages and how quickly interviews and approvals happen. Individual pieces such as an ad set, a script or an article take two to five days.

### What do you need from us to start?

Your existing website and materials, product data, a short conversation with the founder or sales lead about customers and objections, brand guidelines if they exist, and a list of the pieces you need first. From that we write the voice document and start drafting.

### Do you write B2B and export sales copy?

Yes. Company profiles, IndiaMART and Alibaba listings, cold email sequences, LinkedIn posts and export brochures for manufacturers and exporters are a regular part of the work, written to answer a procurement buyer's questions — capacity, certifications, MOQ, lead time — plainly.
