## What is Outsource App Development to India?

Outsourcing app development to India means hiring an India-based team to design, build and release your mobile or web app while you keep the product decisions, the accounts and the intellectual property. The work that matters most is discovery and specification, because unclear scope is where most outsourced app projects go wrong.

gaa-tha starts every app with a paid discovery phase: user flows, a clickable prototype, a feature list ranked by priority and a release plan. We then build in sprints with demos in US business hours, keep code in your GitHub and publish under your App Store and Google Play developer accounts.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I outsource app development to India without losing control?

Keep the developer accounts, repositories and cloud accounts in your name, agree a written scope from a discovery phase, and review working builds every sprint. gaa-tha works this way by default and signs an NDA before the brief.

### Flutter or React Native?

Both build iOS and Android from one codebase. We recommend one in discovery based on your UI, integrations and the team who will maintain it; native Swift or Kotlin is used when an app needs deep device features.

### Who owns the app and the code?

You do. Apps are published under your App Store and Google Play accounts and the code lives in your GitHub.

### Can you build a SaaS MVP?

Yes. We build web-based SaaS MVPs with authentication, billing, roles and an admin panel, and plan a path from MVP to a maintainable product.

### How do you handle time zones?

Our team works US business hours, so sprint reviews, stand-ups and urgent fixes happen during your day.

### Do you work white-label for agencies?

Yes. Agencies can resell our app development under their brand with an NDA, non-solicitation of their clients and unbranded documentation.
