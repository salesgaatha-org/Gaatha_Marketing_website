The service

What is Custom Tools & Automations?

Custom business tools and automations are small, purpose-built pieces of software that remove a specific piece of manual work — logging leads and chasing follow-ups, letting a customer check a batch number, compiling a daily sales report, or producing quotes and catalogues from a price list — usually by connecting the systems a business already uses. They are not an ERP; they are the missing piece between the ERP, the spreadsheet and WhatsApp.

At gaa-tha a tool starts with the task that is wasting the most hours, not with a technology. We map how the work happens today, agree the smallest tool that fixes it, build it on plain web technology and the services you already pay for (Google Sheets, WhatsApp, Firebase, your billing software), and hand it over with documentation and your own logins. Most tools are live within a few weeks and pay for themselves in saved time.

Scope

What's included

  • Process mapping — who does what today, in which spreadsheet or chat, and where time is lost
  • Lead tracker with source tagging, status, reminders and a WhatsApp follow-up SOP
  • Product batch-number or warranty lookup tools for customers, with QR codes on packaging
  • Sales, stock or collection dashboards pulling from Google Sheets, Tally or your CRM
  • Automated quotes, proforma invoices, catalogues and price lists generated from one master sheet
  • Form-to-sheet-to-WhatsApp automations for enquiries, orders and dispatch updates
  • Integrations with Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business, Zoho, Tally, Shopify and marketplaces via APIs, Zapier or Make
  • Simple admin screens so your team can update data without a developer
  • Role-based access, backups and hosting setup
  • Documentation, training and optional monthly support

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Find the bottleneck

A short session with the people doing the work to map the current process and pick the one task where a tool saves the most time or errors.

02

Scope the smallest tool

We write a one-page scope — inputs, outputs, users, integrations — with a fixed price and a timeline, and agree what is out of scope for version one.

03

Build and connect

The tool is built on plain web technology and connected to the sheets, WhatsApp, billing or CRM systems you already use.

04

Test with the team

The people who will use it try it on real data, and the rough edges are fixed before launch.

05

Launch and support

The tool goes live with documentation, training and your own logins, with optional monthly support for changes and new features.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Process map and one-page tool scope
  • Working tool on your domain or inside Google Workspace
  • Integrations with your sheets, WhatsApp, billing or CRM
  • Admin screen and role-based access
  • Documentation, training session and all credentials
  • Optional monthly support plan
