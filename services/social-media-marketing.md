## What is Social Media Marketing?

Social media marketing is the ongoing work of building and engaging an audience on platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube through planned content, community management and collaborations, so that followers become enquiries, footfall and customers. It is different from paid ads: ads buy attention for a day, while social content earns an audience that keeps paying back.

At gaa-tha social media is planned backwards from your business calendar — launches, festive seasons, sale periods, exhibitions — and forward from what your audience already watches. Reels, carousels, statics and copy are produced by one in-house team with AI-assisted production where it helps, and every month is reported against reach, engagement, profile visits and the clicks or footfall that matter to you.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a social media marketing agency do?

A social media marketing agency plans, produces, publishes and reports on a brand's content across platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, and manages the comments and messages that content generates. gaa-tha runs this as a monthly retainer with one in-house team for strategy, shooting, design, editing and reporting.

### How many posts a month do we need?

Enough to stay consistent, not enough to burn out the team or the audience. For most brands that is three to five reels and eight to twelve feed posts a month on the primary platform, with LinkedIn at two to three posts a week for B2B. We set the cadence from your goals and content sources during onboarding rather than a fixed package.

### Which platform should we start with?

Instagram for retail, D2C, food, beauty, real estate and most consumer brands in India; LinkedIn for manufacturers, exporters and B2B services; YouTube when your product needs longer explanation or demonstration. Facebook still matters for tier-2 and tier-3 audiences and for older buyers. We usually recommend doing one platform properly before adding a second.

### Do you shoot the content or do we need to send footage?

We shoot it. On-ground shoots at your store, factory or site are planned monthly, and AI ad shoots cover product visuals when a physical shoot is impractical. Founders who want to appear on camera get scripts and direction; those who do not can use an AI clone with consent.

### How do you measure whether social media is working?

By one primary metric per objective, agreed upfront: reach and follower growth for awareness, saves, shares and profile visits for consideration, and website clicks, WhatsApp enquiries or store footfall for revenue. Likes and follower counts are tracked as diagnostics, never as the goal.

### Will you reply to comments and DMs?

Yes. Community management is part of the retainer, with agreed response hours, reply templates for common questions and an escalation rule so pricing, complaints and leads reach your team the same day.

### Do we still need ads if we do organic social media?

Usually yes, in a small way. Organic content builds trust and an audience; a modest boosting or Meta ads budget makes the best content reach people who do not follow you yet. The two are planned together so the best-performing reels become ads.

### How long before social media shows results?

Consistent engagement and profile-visit growth usually show within the first two to three months; enquiries and footfall follow once the audience and the content formats have settled. Social media compounds, which is why it runs as a monthly retainer rather than a one-off campaign.
