The service

What is White-Label PPC & Meta Ads Management?

White-label PPC is paid media management that an agency sells under its own brand while a partner runs the campaigns. The agency owns the client relationship and strategy conversations; the partner handles account structure, campaign builds, audiences, creative testing, bidding, tracking and reporting.

gaa-tha runs Meta and Google Ads for its own ecommerce and lead-generation clients, including campaigns across Canada for a retail client, and does the same work white-label for agencies. We are added through Meta Business Manager partner access or a Google Ads manager account, the client pays the platforms directly, and reports go out in your agency's template.

Scope

What's included

  • Account and tracking audit with a prioritized fix list
  • Campaign structure, audiences and budgets for Meta and Google Ads
  • Search, Performance Max, Shopping and YouTube campaigns
  • Creative testing plans, ad copy and static or video ad production
  • Meta Pixel with Conversions API, GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking
  • Weekly optimization of bids, budgets, audiences and creative
  • Lead quality checks and offline conversion feedback for lead-gen clients
  • Reports and dashboards in your agency's branding
  • Monthly strategy notes you can present to the client as your own

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Access and audit

We join through partner access, audit structure, tracking and spend, and agree KPIs such as CPA, ROAS or cost per qualified lead.

02

Fix tracking

Conversions API, GA4 and Google Ads conversions are verified before any scaling.

03

Build and launch

Campaigns, audiences and creative tests go live against an agreed budget plan.

04

Optimize weekly

Bids, budgets, audiences and creative are adjusted weekly with notes on every change.

05

Report monthly

A branded report and strategy notes ready for your client meeting.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Account and tracking audit
  • Campaign structure and media plan
  • Creative test plan with ad copy and assets
  • Weekly change log
  • Monthly white-label report and dashboard
