The service

What is Influencer & UGC Marketing?

Influencer marketing is partnering with creators who already have an audience's trust so they feature a brand in their own content; UGC (user-generated content) marketing is commissioning authentic, creator-made videos and photos for the brand to use in its own ads, pages and listings. The first borrows an audience, the second borrows a voice — and the strongest campaigns use both.

At gaa-tha creator work is planned like media, not like a favour. We shortlist creators by audience fit and real engagement rather than follower count, write briefs and scripts that keep the message on-strategy while leaving room for the creator's style, negotiate usage rights upfront, and track every collaboration with codes and links so the report shows enquiries and sales, not just views.

Scope

What's included

  • Creator discovery and vetting — audience location, demographics, engagement quality and past brand work
  • Campaign strategy — objective, creator tiers (nano, micro, macro), formats and budget split
  • Briefs, scripts and hooks for each creator, with brand do's and don'ts
  • Negotiation, contracts and usage-rights agreements for paid and barter collaborations
  • Collab reels and Instagram page collaborations so content appears on both profiles
  • UGC video production — creator-shot testimonials, unboxings and demos for ads and product pages
  • Product seeding, shipping coordination and content approval rounds
  • Tracking codes, UTM links and WhatsApp keywords per creator
  • ASCI-compliant disclosure guidance for every post
  • Performance report on reach, engagement, clicks, codes redeemed and cost per result

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Objective and creator map

We agree what the campaign must do — awareness in a city, launches, sales — and build a shortlist of creators whose audience actually matches yours.

02

Vetting and outreach

Shortlisted creators are checked for real engagement and audience location, then contacted with a clear offer, timeline and usage terms.

03

Briefs and production

Each creator receives a brief, hooks and a script outline; UGC creators shoot to a shot list and we edit where the content will run as ads.

04

Publish and amplify

Content goes live on schedule as collab posts, and the best-performing pieces are boosted or run as Meta ads with the rights already secured.

05

Track and report

Codes, links and platform insights are collected into one report on reach, engagement and tracked enquiries or sales, with recommendations for the next round.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Vetted creator shortlist with audience data and rates
  • Campaign brief, scripts and content calendar
  • Signed usage-rights agreements per creator
  • Published collab reels and posts, plus edited UGC videos for ads
  • Tracking sheet with codes, links and per-creator results
  • Post-campaign performance report

Good to know

UGC (user-generated content) pays a creator or customer to make authentic-looking video or photos that you publish on your own channels, ads and product pages. gaa-tha runs both, often in the same campaign, with usage rights sorted upfront.
