The brief

What the client needed

Kitcoz is a D2C skincare brand from Elvey Essentials. It needed to launch with a distinctive identity, a store built around skin concerns and product kits rather than a flat catalogue, and advertising that could be tracked accurately from the first rupee.

Products were already listed on Amazon, so the store also had to absorb that catalogue cleanly.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Brand identity

A teal colour system, a serif-plus-sans typography pairing, concern-led navigation and a brand manifesto that gave the store and the ads one voice.

Brand identity · 16:10

Shopify development

The initial build and an Amazon-to-Shopify product migration, with custom sections: a combo-kit selector, concern-based collection filters, a pincode checker, product carousels and a cart drawer with coupons. GoKwik checkout integration and cash-on-delivery configuration.

Shopify development · 16:10

Tracking

Meta Pixel and Conversions API setup with attribution troubleshooting, so campaign decisions were made on real purchase data.

Tracking · 16:10

Meta ads

A nine-kit creative pack and campaign structure, with extensive ad copy variations for testing.

Meta ads · 16:10

Content

300 Instagram copy variations, each with a unique AI image direction, giving the brand months of on-brand content.

Content · 16:10
