Good to know

Questions about growth marketing

Growth marketing is the practice of using strategy, content and paid media together to grow a measurable business number — leads, orders, footfall or revenue — rather than running channels in isolation. gaa-tha plans the channels to one budget and reports on outcomes, not impressions.

It depends on where your buyers already look. Retail and D2C brands usually start with Meta ads and Instagram content; B2B and manufacturers with Google Search, LinkedIn and marketplace listings; local businesses with Google Business Profile. We recommend a starting mix in the strategy phase before spending.

Ad spend is paid directly by you to Meta or Google from your own ad account. Our fee is a separate management fee, so there is no hidden markup on media.

Every retainer has a monthly report against the KPI agreed at the start: cost per lead, cost per purchase, ROAS, reach, engagement, rankings or AI citations. Dashboards are shared, and you keep the accounts.

Yes — that is the point. Strategy, social, ads, SEO/AEO/GEO and creative are one team, so a festive campaign can go from idea to outdoor banners, reels, Meta ads and a cinema film without three agencies in the loop.
