Reviews & Ratings Management

gaa-tha manages reviews and ratings on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa and other marketplaces the policy-compliant way: review requests through each platform's tools, Amazon Vine where it fits, packaging inserts that stay within the rules, response templates for negative reviews, root-cause fixes for recurring complaints, and a rating recovery plan for listings that have slipped below the level buyers trust.

The service

What is Reviews & Ratings Management?

Reviews and ratings management is the work of earning more genuine reviews for a product, responding to the ones it gets, and recovering a rating that has fallen — all within the marketplace's policies. It includes review requests through the platform's own tools, programmes such as Amazon Vine, compliant packaging inserts, response templates and service-level targets for negative reviews and buyer questions, analysis of complaint themes, and fixes to the product, packaging or listing that remove the cause. Buying reviews, incentivising positive reviews or asking only happy customers to review are policy violations that put the whole account at risk, so none of that is part of it.

At gaa-tha reviews are treated as a signal about the product and the listing, not only as a number to push up. Every negative review is tagged by cause — size, damage in transit, expectation set by the listing, quality — and the monthly review shows which causes are growing so the fix goes into the product or the page rather than into more requests.

Scope

What's included

  • Review audit: rating, review count, velocity and complaint themes by SKU against competitors
  • Automated review requests through Seller Central and each platform's equivalent, timed to delivery
  • Amazon Vine enrolment for eligible new products and other platform review programmes where available
  • Policy-compliant packaging inserts and thank-you cards that ask for honest feedback without incentives
  • Response templates and a service-level target for negative reviews, ratings and buyer questions
  • Complaint-theme analysis with fixes to size charts, imagery, packaging or product
  • Reporting of reviews that breach platform guidelines through the proper channels
  • Rating recovery plan for listings below the rating buyers filter for, including variation and relaunch options
  • Monthly report on rating, review count, velocity and complaint themes by SKU

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and targets

We review rating, velocity and complaint themes for every SKU, compare them with competitors, and agree which listings need volume, which need recovery and which need a product fix.

02

Compliant request system

Platform review requests are switched on and timed, Vine or equivalent programmes are enrolled where eligible, and inserts are designed to stay inside the rules.

03

Response process

Templates, tone and a response-time target are set for negative reviews and buyer questions, so no complaint sits unanswered on the page.

04

Fix the causes

Complaints are tagged by cause each week and the recurring ones are fixed at the source — listing expectations, size charts, packaging or the product itself.

05

Recover and report

Listings below the trusted rating get a recovery plan, and the monthly report shows rating, velocity and complaint trends by SKU.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Review audit and per-SKU targets
  • Configured review-request system and Vine or programme enrolments
  • Compliant packaging insert designs
  • Response templates and service-level guide
  • Complaint-theme tracker with fixes logged
  • Monthly ratings and reviews report
