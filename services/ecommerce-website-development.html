The service

What is Ecommerce Website Development?

Ecommerce website development is the design and coding of an online store on a platform other than a hosted builder: WordPress with WooCommerce, a headless front end connected to a commerce back end, or a fully custom application. It suits businesses whose catalogue, pricing or integrations do not fit a standard template — tiered B2B pricing, request-a-quote flows, dealer logins, configurable products or a connection to an existing ERP.

gaa-tha recommends Shopify for most D2C brands and builds elsewhere when there is a clear reason. WooCommerce is the common choice for brands already on WordPress or needing plugin-level control; headless builds are for brands where speed and a custom front end matter; custom applications are for genuinely unusual commerce logic. Whatever the stack, the store ships with Indian payment gateways, shipping integrations, structured data and verified tracking.

Scope

What's included

  • Platform recommendation — Shopify, WooCommerce, headless or custom — with reasons
  • UX and information architecture for catalogue, product, cart, checkout and account pages
  • WooCommerce theme and plugin development, or a headless front end on a commerce API
  • B2B features: tiered pricing, dealer or distributor logins, minimum order quantities, request-a-quote
  • Payment integration with Razorpay, PayU or Cashfree, plus Stripe or PayPal for international buyers
  • Shipping integrations such as Shiprocket and Delhivery, pincode checks and COD rules
  • ERP, inventory, CRM or accounting integrations via API or scheduled sync
  • Product, Review and Breadcrumb schema, sitemap and technical SEO from day one
  • Meta Pixel, Conversions API and GA4 event tracking through Google Tag Manager
  • Hosting setup, security hardening, backups and a performance baseline

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Discovery and platform choice

We map your catalogue, pricing rules, buyers and integrations, then recommend the stack and document the scope.

02

Architecture and design

Site structure, data model and mobile-first designs for the key templates, including any B2B account or quote flows.

03

Development

Theme, plugin or front-end development, payment and shipping integrations, and API connections to your ERP or inventory system.

04

Data, content and SEO

Products, categories, images and copy are loaded, schema and redirects added, and the site is checked against a technical SEO list.

05

Testing, launch and handover

Test orders on every payment method, load and security checks, go-live and a training session with your team.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Live ecommerce site on your domain and hosting, with full source access
  • Configured payments, shipping, tax and COD rules
  • Integration documentation for ERP, inventory or CRM connections
  • Verified tracking setup for Meta, Google Ads and GA4
  • Admin guide and recorded training for your team
  • Technical SEO and performance report at launch
