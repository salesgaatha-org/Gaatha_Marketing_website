The partnership

What the brand needed from us

Ghanshyam Snacks manufactures bulk peanuts and white-label snacks for B2B buyers. Paid campaigns were producing plenty of leads, but many were fake or low-quality, the brand was not positioned for the buyers it wanted, and the founder — the company's most credible voice — was not visible online.

The company needed a partner for the whole picture: brand strategy, campaigns that bring real buyers and traffic, a system to qualify them, and a personal brand for the founder that builds trust with distributors and white-label clients.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

Branding strategy

B2B positioning that presents Ghanshyam as a manufacturer buyers can trust, carried through a product catalogue, IndiaMART listing assets and every campaign.

Branding strategy · 16:10

Lead-generation campaigns

Meta lead campaigns rebuilt with a WhatsApp first-reply system that lets fake leads filter themselves out before a human replies, plus a B2B reply template for IndiaMART enquiries.

Lead-generation campaigns · 16:10

Traffic campaigns

Traffic campaigns that bring buyers to the website and catalogue, so prospects see the full range before they call.

Traffic campaigns · 16:10

Lead-management SOP and tracker

A five-stage pipeline — capture, 24-hour qualification, quote, Day 1/3/7 follow-up and weekly review — with a dashboard showing qualification rate, conversion rate, pipeline value and junk percentage by ad source.

Lead-management SOP and tracker · 16:10

Founder personal brand and founder campaigns

Personal-brand management for the founder — positioning, content and founder-led campaigns on Instagram and LinkedIn — so the person behind the factory becomes the brand's strongest proof.

Founder personal brand and founder campaigns · 16:10

Social media and product films

50-post content sets, reel production briefs, and a cinematic sand-roasted peanut macro film that makes a commodity look premium.

Social media and product films · 16:10

Website — built and managed

We built and manage the Ghanshyam Snacks website as the destination for traffic campaigns and the first stop for B2B buyers.
