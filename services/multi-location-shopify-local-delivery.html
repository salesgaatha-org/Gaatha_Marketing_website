Multi-location Shopify & Local Delivery

gaa-tha builds multi-location Shopify stores for retail chains, franchises and local-delivery brands in India: one website where the customer enters a pincode or picks an outlet, and the order is routed to that store with a WhatsApp notification, outlet-level availability toggles, unified pricing and prepaid or COD rules set per location.

The service

What is Multi-location Shopify & Local Delivery?

A multi-location Shopify store is a single ecommerce website that serves several physical outlets: the customer enters a pincode or chooses a store, the site shows what that outlet has in stock and can deliver, and the order is sent to that outlet to fulfil. It replaces the alternatives of one website per branch, or one central warehouse shipping everything by courier, for businesses whose products are made or held locally.

gaa-tha builds these for chains that sell fresh or heavy goods — sweets, snacks, bakery, dairy, pharmacy, groceries — and for franchise networks that want one brand storefront with outlet-level control. Each outlet gets its own WhatsApp order alerts, its own availability switches and its own delivery rules, while the brand keeps one catalogue, one price list and one set of reports.

Scope

What's included

  • Location or pincode-based store assignment with a serviceability check before checkout
  • Order routing logic to the assigned outlet using Shopify Locations and fulfilment rules
  • WhatsApp order notifications to each outlet, with order details and a confirmation step
  • Outlet-level availability toggles so a store can switch products off when they run out
  • Prepaid-only or COD rules per outlet, per pincode or per order value
  • Unified pricing and promotions across outlets, with optional outlet-specific offers
  • Local delivery, pick-up and slot options with delivery charges by distance or zone
  • Outlet dashboard and staff roles so each store sees only its own orders
  • Reporting by outlet: orders, revenue, cancellations and delivery times

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Map the network

We list every outlet, its pincodes or delivery radius, opening hours, product range and who takes the order, then define the routing rules.

02

Design the location flow

The pincode or store-picker experience is designed so a customer knows within seconds which outlet will serve them and what it can deliver.

03

Build routing and notifications

Shopify Locations, fulfilment rules, WhatsApp alerts, availability toggles and prepaid or COD rules are built and tested outlet by outlet.

04

Train the outlets

Store staff get a short guide and a walkthrough for confirming, packing and closing orders, plus a rule for what to do when a product runs out.

05

Launch and expand

The site goes live with a pilot set of outlets, edge cases are fixed in the first two weeks, and new outlets are added from a template.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Live Shopify store with pincode or outlet selection and routing rules
  • Outlet-wise WhatsApp notification setup and confirmation flow
  • Availability, pricing and payment rules configured per outlet
  • Outlet onboarding template for adding new stores or franchisees
  • Staff guide and recorded walkthrough for outlet teams
  • Outlet-level reporting view
