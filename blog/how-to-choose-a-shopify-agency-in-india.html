How to choose a Shopify agency in India: what top agencies do

By gaa-tha· 22 October 2026· 8 min read· Shopify
Blog cover · 16:9

To choose a Shopify agency in India, first decide which type of partner you need (freelancer, theme customiser, Shopify-only studio, full-service D2C agency, or Plus and headless specialist), then compare them on discovery, conversion-led design, tracking, speed, Indian checkout and COD expertise, and post-launch support. Ask for live stores, a written scope and full ownership in your name. Price matters, but scope and process decide the result.

Key takeaways

  • Match the partner type to the job: a freelancer for a quick setup, a studio for a conversion-led build, a full-service agency when the store and growth must work together.
  • The best agencies start with discovery and tracking, not the homepage design.
  • Indian checkout, COD and RTO experience separates good Shopify partners from generic web designers.
  • Red flags: no live examples, accounts in the agency’s name, vague scope, app-heavy builds and no post-launch plan.
  • Score every proposal on the same weighted criteria before comparing price.

What types of Shopify partners are there in India?

Shopify partners in India fall into six broad types, and each is the right choice for a different job.

Partner typeBest forWatch out for
FreelancerSmall setups, quick fixes, tight budgetsAvailability, a single point of failure, limited design or tracking depth
Theme customiserInstalling and adjusting a theme fastLittle strategy or conversion thinking; heavy reliance on apps
Shopify-only studioConversion-led builds, custom sections, migrationsMay not handle ads, content or marketplaces
Full-service D2C agencyStore, brand, content, ads and retention under one planCheck that Shopify depth is in-house, not outsourced
Shopify Plus and headless specialistCheckout extensions, B2B, Hydrogen, ERP integrationsHigher cost; more than a first store needs
Offshore development shopLarge volumes of code at low hourly ratesCommunication, D2C context and ownership of outcomes

No type is better in general. Decide whether you need a website, a website plus growth, or engineering depth, then shortlist two or three partners of that type. For marketing-agency types, see our guide to the best digital marketing agency in India.

What do the best Shopify agencies do differently?

The best Shopify agencies treat the store as a sales system rather than a design project, which shows up in seven habits.

  • Discovery before design. They study your products, margins, buyers, competitors and current analytics, and write down what the store must achieve before opening Figma.
  • Conversion-led design. Navigation by buyer need, product pages that answer objections, bundles that raise order value, mobile first, with every design choice explained by buyer behaviour rather than taste.
  • Tracking as a deliverable. Meta Pixel with Conversions API, GA4 and Google Ads conversions, verified on a real order, including on third-party checkouts.
  • Speed discipline. Fewer apps, custom sections instead of scripts, compressed media and a mobile performance target agreed upfront.
  • Indian checkout and COD. Gateway choice, one-click checkout trade-offs, COD rules by pincode and order value, WhatsApp confirmation and shipping integration; see Shopify integrations for Indian brands.
  • Content readiness. Photography and copy are planned with the build, so the store launches complete.
  • Life after launch. A bug-fix window, training, a conversion rate optimisation roadmap and optional store management.

A quick test: ask each agency what it would change on your current store or a store you admire. An average answer talks about colours and a new homepage; a strong one talks about product pages, checkout friction, COD rules, tracking gaps and the few pages that carry most of the revenue.

What are the red flags when hiring a Shopify agency?

The biggest red flag is an agency that cannot show live Shopify stores and explain exactly what it did on each. Others to watch for:

  • The store, domain, theme or app accounts set up in the agency’s name rather than yours.
  • A quote without a list of templates, sections and integrations.
  • Another app as the answer to every requirement.
  • No mention of tracking, mobile speed or checkout testing.
  • Guaranteed sales or rankings, or “number one agency” claims without evidence.
  • A design copied wholesale from another brand’s store.
  • No redirect and SEO plan when replacing an existing site.
  • Vagueness about who will actually do the work, or undisclosed subcontracting.

Price itself can be a flag. A quote far below the others usually means content, tracking or integrations are missing from the scope; a quote far above them should come with features or depth the others do not offer. Ask either to explain the gap line by line.

Which questions should you ask a Shopify agency before signing?

Ask questions that force specific answers about scope, process and ownership; vague answers to any of these are a warning.

  1. Which live Shopify stores have you built, and what exactly did you do on each?
  2. Will you customise a theme or build a custom one, and why is that right for us?
  3. How many templates and custom sections are included?
  4. Which gateway, checkout, shipping and WhatsApp integrations are included and tested?
  5. How will you set up and verify Meta Pixel, Conversions API and GA4?
  6. What mobile speed target will you meet, and how many apps will the store run?
  7. Who supplies photography and copy?
  8. How do you handle redirects and SEO in a Shopify migration?
  9. What happens in the first 30 days after launch, and what does ongoing support cost?
  10. Will the store, theme code, accounts and files all be owned by us?

How should you run the agency selection process?

Run Shopify agency selection as a short, structured process over two to four weeks, so you compare like with like.

  1. Write a one-page brief: products and catalogue size, target buyers, must-have features, integrations, content you already have, budget band and launch date.
  2. Shortlist three partners of the type that fits the job, from referrals, live stores you admire and the Shopify Partner Directory.
  3. Hold a 45-minute call with each and listen to their questions; good agencies ask about margins, COD share and repeat rate before talking about design.
  4. Request written proposals against the same brief and scope sheet.
  5. Call two references per agency and test their live stores on a mid-range Android phone.
  6. Score with the scorecard below, then negotiate scope rather than only price.
  7. Consider a paid discovery phase for large builds, so both sides agree the plan before committing to the whole project.

How do you score Shopify agencies side by side?

Score each shortlisted agency from 1 to 5 on the criteria below, multiply by the weight and compare totals before you compare price.

CriterionWeightWhat a strong answer looks like
Relevant live work20%Several live stores in a similar category, with the agency’s role explained
Discovery and strategy15%A written discovery stage with analytics review and clear goals
Conversion-led design15%Design rationale tied to buyer behaviour; mobile-first prototypes
Tracking and analytics10%Pixel, Conversions API and GA4 verified on a real order
Speed and app discipline10%A mobile performance target and a short app list
Indian checkout, COD and integrations10%A clear gateway, checkout, COD and shipping plan
Post-launch support10%A bug-fix window, training and an optional retainer
Scope clarity and ownership10%Itemised scope, with all accounts and code in your name

For example, an agency scoring 4 on live work (4 × 20 = 80), 3 on discovery (45), 4 on design (60) and 4 on each of the five 10% criteria (200) totals 385 out of a possible 500. Compare totals, then ask the top two to close their weakest gaps. A high score with a higher price usually costs less than a low score with a cheap quote, because rework, lost tracking and slow pages are expensive. To sense-check the numbers, read how much a Shopify website costs in India.

Hire a freelancer for a small, well-defined setup or quick fixes on a tight budget. Hire a studio or agency when you need design, conversion, tracking and integrations delivered together, or when the store must keep improving after launch.

A Shopify Partner is a person or company registered in Shopify’s Partner Program to build stores, themes or apps for merchants. Registration on its own is not a quality signal, so judge partners on live work, process and references.

A customised theme typically takes four to eight weeks once content is ready, and a custom theme eight to fourteen weeks. Be wary of much shorter timelines for custom work, and of open-ended ones without milestones.
