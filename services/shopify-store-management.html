The service

What is Shopify Store Management & Maintenance?

Shopify store management is the ongoing operation of a live store: adding and updating products, changing prices and MRPs, refreshing collections and banners, keeping apps and the theme working, fixing bugs, watching speed and preparing the store for sales and festivals. It is the difference between a store that was launched once and a store that is looked after.

gaa-tha takes this on as a monthly retainer with a named store manager, a shared task board and an agreed turnaround time for routine changes. Because design, copy, photography and performance marketing sit in the same team, a new launch or a Diwali sale is set up end to end — product pages, banners, offers, tracking — rather than being passed between vendors.

Scope

What's included

  • Product uploads and edits: new SKUs, variants, images, descriptions and tags
  • Bulk price and MRP changes, compare-at pricing and discount setup
  • Collection, navigation, homepage and banner updates on a monthly calendar
  • Quarterly app audit: remove unused apps, review charges, fix conflicts
  • Bug fixes and theme tweaks with a tracked turnaround time
  • Monthly speed and Core Web Vitals check with fixes
  • Festive and sale-season setup: offers, landing pages, countdowns, gift options
  • Checkout, payment, shipping and COD rule changes as your policies evolve
  • Tracking checks so Pixel, CAPI and GA4 keep reporting purchases correctly
  • Monthly store health report: orders, conversion, speed, errors and tasks completed

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Store audit and onboarding

We review the theme, apps, tracking, speed and catalogue quality, fix urgent issues and agree the monthly scope and turnaround times.

02

Shared task board

Every request — new product, price change, banner, bug — goes on a shared board with an owner and a due date, so nothing lives only in WhatsApp chats.

03

Monthly content calendar

Homepage, collections and banners are planned a month ahead around launches, festivals and sale events, with creative made in-house.

04

Maintain and protect

Apps, theme updates, speed and tracking are checked on a schedule, and fixes are made before customers notice.

05

Report and improve

A monthly health report covers what changed, what broke, conversion trends and the recommendations for next month.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Shared task board with agreed turnaround times
  • Monthly homepage, collection and banner refresh
  • Updated catalogue with clean product data and pricing
  • Quarterly app and charges audit
  • Monthly store health report with speed and tracking checks
  • Festive and sale-season store setup
