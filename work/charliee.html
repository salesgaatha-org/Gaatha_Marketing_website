The partnership

What the brand needed from us

Charliee is a large regional snack brand with more than 200 SKUs across namkeen, chikki, khakhra, chips, sweets and bakery, sold through its own website, on Amazon and Flipkart, and on 10-minute delivery apps. Each channel has its own catalogue rules, ad system, pricing pressure and reporting — and the brand also sells white-label to export buyers and through a field sales team in Gujarat and Mumbai.

Charliee did not need another one-off campaign. It needed one partner who could run every digital channel to the same plan, all year: build and handle the website, grow the marketplaces and quick-commerce apps, keep brand campaigns running on Meta and Google, and supply the creative and sales material that ties it together. That is the retainer gaa-tha runs.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

Shopify website — built and managed

We designed and built Charliee's Shopify store from a UI/UX audit and homepage prototype, with a product-content workbook covering all 200+ SKUs, rebuilt product cards, a corporate-gifting page, category cover imagery and a batch-number lookup tool customers use to check a pack's details. We handle the store month to month — product uploads, bulk MRP updates across categories, banners, festive setups and fixes.

Shopify website — built and managed · 16:10

Quick commerce: Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart

We manage Charliee on the quick-commerce apps end to end: catalogue and thumbnails that win a two-second scroll, pack sizes, dark-store availability, purchase orders, pricing and promotions, and sponsored search, category and banner ads run by SKU and city.

Quick commerce: Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart · 16:10

Ecommerce marketplaces: Amazon and Flipkart

Account management for Amazon.in and Flipkart — listings and titles written for search, image stacks, pricing, deals and sale events, Sponsored ads and Flipkart PLA/PCA campaigns, account health and monthly reporting on sales by platform.

Ecommerce marketplaces: Amazon and Flipkart · 16:10

Brand campaigns on Meta and Google

Always-on and festive brand campaigns across Facebook, Instagram, Google Search, Performance Max and YouTube — building awareness for the brand and pushing shoppers to the website, the marketplaces and the quick-commerce apps.

Brand campaigns on Meta and Google · 16:10

Creative, AI product imagery and product films

Category visuals for the store, marketplaces and social; AI-produced product imagery for the long tail of SKUs; and cinematic product ads for launches and festive pushes.

B2B, export and sales enablement

Advertising creative that positions Charliee as a white-label manufacturing partner for export buyers, and an annual sales-meet kit for the field team across Gujarat and Mumbai — target workbooks, price lists, a standee and the speech.
