The service

What is Social Media Marketing?

Social media marketing is the ongoing work of building and engaging an audience on platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube through planned content, community management and collaborations, so that followers become enquiries, footfall and customers. It is different from paid ads: ads buy attention for a day, while social content earns an audience that keeps paying back.

At gaa-tha social media is planned backwards from your business calendar — launches, festive seasons, sale periods, exhibitions — and forward from what your audience already watches. Reels, carousels, statics and copy are produced by one in-house team with AI-assisted production where it helps, and every month is reported against reach, engagement, profile visits and the clicks or footfall that matter to you.

Scope

What's included

  • Platform strategy — which of Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube deserve effort, and what each is for
  • Content pillars, tone of voice and a monthly content calendar planned around launches and seasons
  • Posts, carousels, statics and reels produced in-house, including scripting, shooting or AI ad shoots and editing
  • Copywriting, captions, hashtags and hooks in English, Hindi or Gujarati as needed
  • Community management — replies to comments and DMs within agreed hours, with escalation rules
  • Page collaborations and cross-posting with partner brands, creators and local pages
  • Profile optimisation — bio, highlights, link-in-bio, LinkedIn company page sections
  • Trend and format monitoring so the calendar can react within the month
  • Monthly report on reach, engagement, follower growth, profile visits and website clicks or footfall

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and strategy

We review your current profiles, best-performing content and competitors, then set the platforms, pillars, posting cadence and the one number each platform is run against.

02

Calendar and briefs

A monthly calendar is built around your launches, seasons and sales goals, with a brief for every reel, carousel and post before production starts.

03

Production

Content is shot, designed, written and edited in batches by the in-house team — on-ground shoots, AI ad shoots or a mix — and sent for approval in one round.

04

Publish and engage

Posts go out on schedule, comments and DMs are handled within agreed hours, and collaborations with partner pages and creators are coordinated.

05

Report and adjust

A monthly report shows what moved reach, saves, profile visits and clicks; the next calendar is adjusted around what worked.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Social media strategy and content pillars document
  • Monthly content calendar with approved briefs
  • Finished reels, carousels, statics and captions each month
  • Optimised profiles (bio, highlights, LinkedIn page sections)
  • Community management log and DM reply templates
  • Monthly performance report with next-month recommendations

Enough to stay consistent, not enough to burn out the team or the audience. Instagram for retail, D2C, food, beauty, real estate and most consumer brands in India; LinkedIn for manufacturers, exporters and B2B services; YouTube when your product needs longer explanation or demonstration.
