## What is Ecommerce Website Development?

Ecommerce website development is the design and coding of an online store on a platform other than a hosted builder: WordPress with WooCommerce, a headless front end connected to a commerce back end, or a fully custom application. It suits businesses whose catalogue, pricing or integrations do not fit a standard template — tiered B2B pricing, request-a-quote flows, dealer logins, configurable products or a connection to an existing ERP.

gaa-tha recommends Shopify for most D2C brands and builds elsewhere when there is a clear reason. WooCommerce is the common choice for brands already on WordPress or needing plugin-level control; headless builds are for brands where speed and a custom front end matter; custom applications are for genuinely unusual commerce logic. Whatever the stack, the store ships with Indian payment gateways, shipping integrations, structured data and verified tracking.

## Frequently asked questions

### When should I choose WooCommerce or a custom build over Shopify?

When you already run a WordPress site with content and rankings, need plugin-level control, have B2B pricing or quote logic, sell configurable or made-to-order products, or must integrate tightly with an ERP. For a standard D2C catalogue Shopify is usually faster and cheaper to run. gaa-tha tells you honestly which fits before quoting.

### How long does an ecommerce website take to build?

Four to ten weeks. A WooCommerce store on a customised theme with a straightforward catalogue sits at the faster end; headless builds, B2B account systems and ERP integrations take longer because of data mapping and testing. Content and product data readiness is the biggest variable.

### Can you build a B2B ecommerce site with dealer pricing?

Yes. Dealer and distributor logins, tiered or customer-group pricing, minimum order quantities, credit-terms checkout and request-a-quote flows are common in our manufacturer and exporter builds. Prices can be hidden from the public and shown only after approval.

### Which payment gateways work for Indian and international buyers?

Razorpay, PayU and Cashfree cover UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and EMI for Indian buyers; Stripe or PayPal are added for export customers paying in other currencies. Gateway fees vary by provider and payment method, and we share the current structures before you choose.

### Can you connect the store to my ERP or inventory system?

Yes, where the system has an API or an export we can schedule. Typical connections sync stock, prices and orders between the store and Tally, Zoho, Busy, SAP Business One or a custom system, so your team is not keying orders twice.

### Who hosts and maintains the site after launch?

Hosting is in your name on a provider we recommend for your traffic, and you own every login. Maintenance — updates, security patches, backups, speed checks and fixes — is available as a monthly retainer, or your team can take over with our documentation.

### Will the site be found on Google and by AI assistants?

It is built to be: clean semantic HTML, Product, Review and Breadcrumb schema, fast Core Web Vitals, an XML sitemap, an llms.txt file and answer-first category copy. Ongoing ecommerce SEO is a separate service if you want rankings actively grown.
