The service

What is Copywriting & Content Writing?

Copywriting is writing whose job is to make someone act — click, enquire, add to cart, reply — while content writing is writing whose job is to inform and build trust over time, such as articles, guides and FAQs. Both need the same things: a clear understanding of the buyer, a specific promise, and language plain enough to be quoted.

gaa-tha writes both from a single brand voice document, so a product bullet on Amazon, a Meta ad, a reel script and a blog answer all sound like the same company. Every piece is written answer-first and entity-rich so it reads well to a buyer, ranks on Google, and can be cited by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity — the same approach we use on gaa-tha.com.

Scope

What's included

  • Website and landing-page copy: home, services, about, product and campaign pages
  • Product titles, bullets and descriptions for Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify and quick commerce, including 200+ SKU catalogues
  • Ad copy variations for Meta, Google and WhatsApp campaigns
  • Reel, video and cinema ad scripts with hooks and CTAs
  • Blog articles, guides and FAQ pages written for SEO, AEO and GEO
  • Brochure, catalogue and company-profile copy
  • B2B and export sales copy: email sequences, IndiaMART listings, LinkedIn posts
  • Tone of voice document and messaging pillars
  • Hindi and Gujarati versions and transcreation

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Voice and audience

We read what you already have, interview the founders or sales team, and write a short tone of voice and messaging document everyone works from.

02

Research

For each piece we gather product facts, customer questions, competitor language and search terms, so the writing is specific rather than generic.

03

Write

First drafts are written answer-first, with headlines, hooks and CTAs, and structured for the channel — a listing, a landing page, a script or an article.

04

Review

Two rounds of revisions with your team; facts, claims and mandatory text are checked, and product data is reconciled to your master sheet.

05

Publish and measure

Copy is handed over in the format each platform needs, or uploaded by our teams, and we watch rankings, click-through and conversion to refine it.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Tone of voice and messaging document
  • Final copy in Google Docs, or a structured sheet for bulk listings
  • Ad copy sets with variations labelled for testing
  • Scripts with shot notes for reels and films
  • SEO/AEO-ready articles with titles, meta descriptions and FAQ blocks
  • Translations or transcreations in Hindi and Gujarati
