Brand & Creative

Brands people remember and content people stop for.

Brand & Creative is the gaa-tha practice that decides what a brand looks, sounds and feels like — and then makes the assets: identity systems, graphic design, reels, AI ads, cinema films, product photography, copy and sales kits. Every service below is delivered by the same in-house team, so the story stays consistent across all of them.

Good to know

Questions about brand & creative

A brand and creative agency defines how a business looks, sounds and behaves (identity, tone, guidelines) and produces the creative that carries it: social content, reels, ad films, packaging, catalogues and product imagery. gaa-tha runs both halves under one roof so the identity and the daily content never drift apart.

Usually yes, at least a minimum viable identity: a logo, colours, type and a tone of voice. Marketing spends money to make people notice you; branding decides what they remember. If you already have an identity that works, we build on it rather than replacing it.

Yes. Reels, graphic design and AI imagery run on monthly retainers with a content calendar, and our AI-assisted production pipeline lets us ship batches in days. Art direction stays human so the output looks like your brand, not like a template.

Both. We plan and direct on-ground shoots for reels and films, produce AI ad shoots when speed or SKU count makes a physical shoot impractical, and often blend the two.

FMCG and food, D2C beauty, retail, manufacturing and B2B, real estate and education are the categories we work in most. The process is the same; the references and channels change.
