## What is Swiggy Instamart Onboarding?

Swiggy Instamart onboarding is the process of getting a brand approved, catalogued and stocked on Instamart, the quick-commerce service inside the Swiggy app. It covers business and brand documents, category and licence checks, commercial terms, catalogue creation to Instamart's image and attribute standards, and the first purchase order into Swiggy's warehouses and dark stores. Instamart is inventory-led for most categories, so the brand supplies against POs.

gaa-tha prepares and runs the application end to end and plans the range for how Instamart is used. Because it sits beside Swiggy's food delivery, Instamart sees strong demand for snacks, beverages, ready-to-eat, party and late-night baskets, so we lead with the pack sizes that suit those moments. Instamart's deepest markets are Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Pune and Kolkata, with an expanding tier-2 footprint, and we plan launch cities from where your category already sells.

## Frequently asked questions

### What documents do I need to sell on Swiggy Instamart?

Usually GST registration, PAN, bank proof, a trademark certificate or brand authorisation letter, FSSAI licence for food and beverages, and any category-specific licence such as cosmetics approvals. Swiggy also asks for product master data with MRP, barcodes, shelf life and pack images. We share a current checklist because requirements change by category.

### How long does Swiggy Instamart onboarding take?

Typically 3–6 weeks from a complete document pack to the first purchase order. Vendor registration and category review take the first stretch, then catalogue QC, then the first PO and dispatch. Incomplete product data and missing licences are the usual causes of delay, so we verify everything before submission.

### Are there fees to get listed on Swiggy Instamart?

Instamart works on category margins and may ask for listing, onboarding or marketing contributions that vary by category and change over time. We do not quote fixed figures; we obtain the current terms for your category and explain them before you commit.

### Which categories sell best on Instamart?

Snacks, beverages, ice cream, ready-to-eat and instant food, dairy and bakery, party supplies, personal care and beauty, and household essentials all do well because Instamart shoppers often buy for the next hour rather than the week. Fresh, staples and larger packs also sell, but the impulse categories are where a new brand is most likely to be noticed.

### How does Instamart's dark-store model work for a brand?

Swiggy raises purchase orders to its city warehouses and distributes stock to Instamart dark stores that each carry a curated range. Brands earn and keep shelf space through sell-through and reliable supply, so a focused range with dependable fill rate beats a long list of SKUs.

### Which cities should I launch on Instamart first?

Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR and Mumbai are Instamart's deepest markets, with Chennai, Pune and Kolkata close behind and a growing tier-2 network. Start where your brand already has distribution and demand, and expand as sell-through proves the range.

### What happens at go-live?

We confirm every listing is live and searchable in each launch city, check pricing, thumbnails and pack-size titles, verify stock is showing as available in the dark stores that received it, and set up the availability tracker. The account then moves into monthly management or to your own team with an SOP.

### Should I onboard Instamart before Blinkit and Zepto?

If your range is snack, beverage or ready-to-eat led, Instamart can be the best first platform because of the food-delivery audience beside it. For broader grocery and FMCG, Blinkit or Zepto usually come first. We map your SKUs and cities against all three before recommending, and most of the document pack carries across.
