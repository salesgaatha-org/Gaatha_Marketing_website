The partnership

What the brand needed from us

CDS Stores sells premium sweets, dry fruits, hampers and corporate gifting from stores in Vapi and Valsad. Its year is a series of festive peaks — Ganpati, Raksha Bandhan, Diwali, weddings — and each one is won or lost in the same two cities, on the street, on Instagram and inside the store.

The retailer needed one partner who could keep demand steady between festivals and then turn each season into a city-wide campaign: Meta ads, outdoor and print across both cities, in-store branding and screen content that sells at the counter, and a website that takes hamper and gifting orders. gaa-tha handles all of it on a retainer.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

Meta ad campaigns, all year

Always-on and festive campaigns on Facebook and Instagram targeted to Vapi and Valsad, with click-to-WhatsApp for bulk and corporate gifting enquiries and creative refreshed for every occasion.

Meta ad campaigns, all year · 16:10

City-wide banners and print media

Outdoor and print across both cities — store-front banners, auto-rickshaw and bus panels, hoardings and leaflets — designed as premium, minimal category systems with a colour code per category so they read from across the road.

City-wide banners and print media · 16:10

In-store marketing and branding

Everything the shopper sees inside the stores: content for the in-store TV screens, media banners, category signage and counter displays, so the message from the street carries through to the shelf and the billing counter.

In-store marketing and branding · 16:10

Festive campaigns and cinema ads

360° festive campaigns planned as one idea, one budget and one calendar — for example a Diwali campaign split across outdoor, 15–20 influencer collab reels, Meta ads and a 10-second trailer-style AI cinema ad, with an earlier Ganpati AI film setting the visual language.

Festive campaigns and cinema ads · 16:10

Website — built and managed

We built and handle the cdsstores.in Shopify store so hamper, gifting and festive orders have a clean online path, with seasonal collections and banners set up for every campaign.

Social media and influencers

Instagram content and influencer collaborations that keep CDS Stores visible between festivals and amplify each campaign when it lands.
