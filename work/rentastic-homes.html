Rentastic Homes: strategy, lead generation and on-ground branding for a multi-city PG brand.

Marketing and offer strategy, lead-generation campaigns and lead conversion, banners and branding at PG locations across cities, a founder-led video series and the website gaa-tha built for Rentastic Homes.

gaa-tha is the marketing partner for Rentastic Homes, a PG and co-living brand operating across cities in India. We set its marketing and offer strategy, run lead-generation campaigns and help convert those leads into tenants, produce the banners and branding at every PG location, created its founder-led video series and built and manage its website.

The partnership

What the brand needed from us

Choosing a PG is a trust decision made by students, working professionals and parents who have never met the operator. Rentastic Homes runs PGs in more than one city, so it needed demand in every location, offers that filled beds in the right season, and a brand that looked the same on Instagram, on the website and on the building.

The brief was a full partner: strategy, leads, conversion, on-ground branding and the website — run all year, not just at admission season.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

Marketing and offer strategy

Positioning by audience and location, and offer strategies — seasonal offers, referral and early-booking deals — planned around admission and job-joining cycles.

Marketing and offer strategy · 16:10

Lead generation and conversion

Lead-generation campaigns that bring enquiries for each property, plus the follow-up process, scripts and tracking that turn those leads into site visits and tenants.

Lead generation and conversion · 16:10

On-ground branding across cities

Banners, signage and branding at the PG locations across cities, so every property looks like one brand and the building itself sells.

On-ground branding across cities · 16:10

Sales and operations material

A Girls PG brochure covering rooms, safety, food, rules and pricing in a format parents can forward, and a tenant check-out form so move-outs are documented the same way every time.

Founder-led content

A 15-episode founder series — scripts and a branded script book — for Instagram and YouTube so the founder can record in batches and build trust before the first call.

Website — built and managed

We built and manage the Rentastic Homes website, with property pages and enquiry routes that feed the lead process.
