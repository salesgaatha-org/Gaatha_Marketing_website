## What is Catalogues, Presentations & Sales Kits?

A sales kit is the set of documents a business uses to win an order: a product catalogue, a company profile, a pitch or investor deck, a price list and the brochures a buyer takes away from a meeting or a trade fair. Its job is to answer a buyer's questions — who are you, what do you make, at what capacity, at what price, and why should I trust you — before the salesperson gets a second call.

gaa-tha designs these as one family, so the catalogue, profile and deck share the same structure, photography and tone, and can be updated without a redesign. Documents are built to be sent on WhatsApp and email as light PDFs, printed properly for a sales meet or Diwali distribution, and reused as IndiaMART and LinkedIn assets — with the product data kept in a sheet so price and SKU changes are a quick edit.

## How we work

1. **Structure** — We map who reads the document and what they need to decide, then write the page-by-page structure before any design.
2. **Content** — Product data, specifications, certifications and imagery are collected into a master sheet; missing photos and copy are produced by our team.
3. **Design** — A master layout system is designed and applied across the catalogue, profile and deck, so every product page and slide follows the same grid.
4. **Proof** — Two review rounds with sales and management; prices, codes and claims are checked against the master sheet before sign-off.
5. **Deliver** — Press-ready PDFs go to the printer, light PDFs go to WhatsApp and email, and editable files stay with you for quick updates.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should a product catalogue include?

A short company introduction, a clear product index, one page or block per product or range with images, specifications, pack sizes and codes, certifications and compliance details, and how to order. Price lists are usually kept separate so the catalogue stays current when rates change. gaa-tha structures the catalogue around how your buyers actually search and order.

### How long does a catalogue or deck take?

One to three weeks depending on page count, the number of SKUs and whether photography and copy already exist. A company profile or pitch deck sits at the short end; a catalogue of 100 or more SKUs with new photography at the long end.

### Can you design a pitch deck or investor deck?

Yes. We work with founders on the narrative — problem, product, traction, market, ask — then design the deck so the numbers and the story land quickly, with templates for the financial slides so you can update them yourself.

### Do you also write the copy and shoot the products?

Yes, if you need it. Our copywriting and product photography teams produce what is missing, so a manufacturer can hand over a price list and a factory tour and receive a finished catalogue.

### Can you make it easy to update prices and SKUs?

Yes. Product data lives in a master sheet, price lists are designed as separate inserts or linked tables, and you receive editable files, so a rate change is an edit rather than a redesign. For frequent changes we offer a small update retainer.

### Will the same catalogue work for export buyers?

With adjustments. Export brochures need certifications, capacity, MOQ, lead times, packaging and Incoterms visible, and imagery that reads well to buyers in the Gulf, Europe or the US. We produce an export version alongside the domestic catalogue where needed.

### What format do we get for WhatsApp and IndiaMART?

A compressed PDF small enough to send comfortably on WhatsApp, plus JPG or PNG images for each product and short descriptions sized for IndiaMART and TradeIndia listings, so the sales team can share and list without asking a designer.

### Do you handle printing?

We prepare press-ready files with bleed, crop marks and CMYK, recommend paper and finishes, coordinate with your printer or ours, and check the proof before the full run. Printing itself is paid to the printer directly.
