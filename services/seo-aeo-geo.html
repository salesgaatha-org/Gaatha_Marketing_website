The service

What is SEO, AEO & GEO?

SEO, AEO and GEO are three layers of search visibility: SEO (search engine optimisation) gets a website ranked in classic Google results, AEO (answer engine optimisation) gets it chosen as the direct answer in AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants, and GEO (generative engine optimisation) gets the brand cited and recommended inside AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. All three reward the same thing — clear, structured, consistent and corroborated information.

At gaa-tha the three are one programme. Technical SEO and keyword research build the base; answer-first pages, FAQs and schema make the site quotable; digital PR, citations and consistent business data across the web make it trustworthy. We track rankings and AI citations side by side, because your next customer may never see a classic results page.

Scope

What's included

  • Technical SEO audit — crawlability, indexing, Core Web Vitals, mobile, duplicate and thin pages
  • Keyword and question research grouped by intent, including the questions people ask AI assistants
  • On-page optimisation — titles, headings, internal links, images and answer-first rewrites
  • Schema and structured data (Organization, Service, Product, FAQPage, Article, LocalBusiness)
  • FAQ and answer content written to be quoted out of context
  • Content clusters — pillar pages and supporting posts planned and written monthly
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation where you have a physical location
  • Digital PR, directory citations and consistent business facts across LinkedIn, GBP and listings
  • llms.txt, XML sitemap and robots configuration so AI crawlers can read the site
  • Monthly report on rankings, organic traffic, leads and AI-assistant citations

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and baseline

We crawl the site, check Search Console and analytics, and record current rankings and whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity mention you for your core questions.

02

Research and plan

Keywords and questions are mapped by intent and buying stage, and a six-month content and fix roadmap is agreed with priorities.

03

Technical and on-page fixes

Speed, indexing and structure issues are fixed, key pages are rewritten answer-first, and schema, sitemap and llms.txt are added.

04

Content and authority

Pillar pages, FAQs and posts are published on a monthly cadence, and citations, directory listings and PR mentions are built to corroborate the facts.

05

Track and compound

Rankings, traffic, leads and AI citations are reported monthly, and the plan is adjusted to whatever is moving fastest.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Technical SEO audit with a prioritised fix list
  • Keyword and question map by intent
  • Optimised pages with schema, FAQs and internal linking
  • Monthly content — pillar pages, posts and FAQ additions
  • llms.txt, XML sitemap and structured-data files
  • Monthly ranking, traffic, lead and AI-visibility report
