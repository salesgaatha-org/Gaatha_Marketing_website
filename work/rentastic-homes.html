The brief

What the client needed

Choosing a PG is a trust decision made by students and parents who have never met the operator. Rentastic Homes needed material that answered their questions before the first call, and content that put the founder in front of the audience consistently.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Sales material

A Girls PG brochure covering rooms, safety, food, rules and pricing structure in a format parents could forward.

Sales material · 16:10

Operations

A tenant check-out form so move-outs are documented the same way every time.

Operations · 16:10

Content

A 15-episode founder series — scripts and a branded script book — designed for Instagram and YouTube so the founder could record in batches.

Content · 16:10

To confirm
