Colortek

Colortek: rebranding a 25-year coatings manufacturer and building the website to match.

Branding briefs, a rebranding structure with the 'Colortek Veris' direction, a website gaa-tha designed, built and manages, plus SEO/AEO/GEO and social for Colourtech Coating India.

gaa-tha is the branding and website partner for Colortek (Colourtech Coating India), a 25-year industrial coatings manufacturer in Gujarat. We wrote its branding briefs and rebranding structure, set the 'Colortek Veris' sub-brand direction, designed and built its website from scratch, and manage the site, search visibility and social channels.

The partnership

What the brand needed from us

Colortek (Colourtech Coating India) has 25 years of technical substance — compliance, specialised coating lines and demanding industrial customers. Its online presence undermined it: contradictory figures, broken metadata, duplicate content, no structured data and a slow site. The name also sat in a crowded field of similar-sounding companies.

The company needed a partner to rethink the brand without losing two decades of recognition, then rebuild and run the digital presence so buyers, search engines and AI assistants see the manufacturer as it really is.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

Branding briefs

A seven-page branding brief covering the case for change, naming rationale, identity continuity, discoverability and a phased rollout the company could approve section by section.

Branding briefs · 16:10

Rebranding structure

A naming exercise leading to the 'Colortek Veris' direction: keep the existing wordmark and colour swoosh for continuity, and add a premium sub-brand for the advanced coating range — with a clear brand architecture for parent and product lines.

Rebranding structure · 16:10

Website — designed, built and managed

We audited the old site for credibility, brand, SEO/AEO/GEO and UX issues, then designed and built a new website from scratch with clear product lines, accurate company facts and structured data. We publish updates and manage the site on an ongoing basis.

Website — designed, built and managed · 16:10

SEO, AEO and GEO

Technical fixes, schema, answer-first product content and consistent company facts so Colortek ranks on Google and can be cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

SEO, AEO and GEO · 16:10

Company presentation and social

A company presentation for sales meetings and content for LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook built on the new brand system.
