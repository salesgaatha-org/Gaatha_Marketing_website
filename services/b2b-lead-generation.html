The service

What is B2B & Export Lead Generation?

B2B lead generation is the systematic work of finding the companies that should be buying from you, reaching the right person inside them and turning the first reply into a qualified enquiry — through LinkedIn outreach, targeted ads, B2B marketplaces such as IndiaMART and TradeIndia, search and email. Export lead generation applies the same system to buyers in specific countries.

At gaa-tha the lead system is built before the outreach starts: who the ideal buyer is, which countries and categories, what the first message says, who replies on WhatsApp and within how long, and how every lead is tagged so the monthly report shows quotes and orders rather than a count of messages sent. The channels are then run together, with the tracker as the single source of truth.

Scope

What's included

  • Ideal-buyer profile and country or region targeting by product, capacity and margin
  • LinkedIn company and decision-maker lists built for each target segment
  • LinkedIn profile and company-page optimisation for the founder and sales team
  • Outreach sequences — connection notes, follow-ups and email — written to earn a reply, not to spam
  • Meta and LinkedIn lead-generation ads with instant forms and WhatsApp click-to-chat
  • Google Search campaigns for product and export-intent keywords
  • IndiaMART and TradeIndia listing optimisation — catalogue, titles, images, specifications and pricing
  • WhatsApp first-reply templates and a response-time SOP for your sales team
  • Lead-qualification SOP — questions, scoring and hand-off rules
  • CRM or Excel tracker with a dashboard, and a monthly report on leads, qualified leads, quotes and orders

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Buyer profile and targets

We define the ideal buyer with your sales team — industry, size, country, order value — and agree what a qualified lead means and how many quotes a month is the goal.

02

Assets and lists

LinkedIn profiles, company page, product one-pagers and marketplace listings are brought up to standard, and decision-maker lists are built per segment.

03

Outreach and ads

Sequences go live on LinkedIn and email, lead ads run on Meta and LinkedIn, and search campaigns catch buyers already looking — all pointing to one WhatsApp or form.

04

Qualify and hand off

Every reply is answered with the first-reply template, scored against the SOP and handed to sales with context in the tracker.

05

Review and refine

A monthly review with the sales team on lead quality, quote conversion and which channel or segment produced the best buyers, then the lists and messages are adjusted.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Ideal-buyer profile and country or segment targeting document
  • Optimised LinkedIn profiles, company page and marketplace listings
  • Outreach sequences and lead-ad campaigns live
  • WhatsApp first-reply templates and lead-qualification SOP
  • CRM or Excel lead tracker with dashboard
  • Monthly report on leads, qualified leads, quotes and orders by channel
