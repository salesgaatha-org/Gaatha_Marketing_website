## What is Shopify Store Design & Development?

Shopify store design and development is the work of planning, designing and coding an online store on Shopify: the theme and page layouts, custom sections such as kit builders and pincode checkers, checkout and payment integrations, shipping and cash-on-delivery rules, analytics tracking and the product catalogue itself. A good build is judged by how easily a visitor finds, trusts and buys a product on a phone, not by how the homepage looks on a designer's monitor.

At gaa-tha every store starts with a UX audit of what Indian shoppers actually do — browse on mobile, compare on WhatsApp, prefer COD until they trust you — and the theme is built around those habits. The same team handles product photography, copy and the Meta and Google tracking, so the store is ready to run ads on the day it goes live.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a Shopify store take to build?

Two to six weeks. A store on a customised premium theme with a small catalogue is at the faster end; custom themes, kit builders, pincode logic and migrations from Amazon or another platform add time for design and data cleanup. Content and approvals arriving on time is the biggest factor. gaa-tha shares a week-by-week plan before starting.

### Shopify or WooCommerce — which is better for a D2C brand in India?

Shopify for most D2C brands: it is faster to launch, easier for your team to maintain, and the Indian checkout, COD, shipping and WhatsApp apps are mature. WooCommerce or a custom build makes sense when you already run WordPress, need deep customisation or have specific B2B pricing logic. We build both and recommend based on your catalogue and team.

### Can you move my products from Amazon or Flipkart to Shopify?

Yes. We export your listings, clean titles, descriptions, variants, images and pricing, map them to Shopify's product structure and import with collections and tags in place. Marketplace listings are written for marketplace search, so we usually rewrite the key product pages for your own store's buyers.

### Which payment and checkout options do you set up?

Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree or CCAvenue for UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and EMI in India (Shopify Payments for stores registered where it is offered, such as Canada), and one-page checkouts such as GoKwik or Shopflo where they suit your volume. COD is set up with rules — pincode limits, order-value caps and OTP or WhatsApp verification — to reduce returns to origin. Gateway fees are set by the providers and vary; we share the current structure before you choose.

### Do you handle the Meta Pixel, Conversions API and GA4 setup?

Yes, on every build. Pixel and CAPI are configured for view, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase events, GA4 is connected through Google Tag Manager, and every event is verified with test orders before launch so your ads report real purchases.

### Will the store be fast on mobile?

It is built to be. Most Indian D2C traffic is on mid-range Android phones over mobile data, so we compress images, limit apps, defer scripts and check Core Web Vitals before go-live. Speed checks continue under a maintenance retainer, because apps added later are the usual cause of slowdowns.

### What do I need to give you before the build starts?

Your brand identity files, product data (names, descriptions, variants, MRP, images), policies for shipping, returns and privacy, domain access, and your GST and bank details for the payment gateway. If any of these are missing we can produce them — photography, copy and branding are in-house.

### How much does a Shopify website cost in India?

It depends on whether the store starts from a theme or a custom design, how many custom sections and integrations it needs, the catalogue size and who produces the content. A theme-based store with light customisation is the most affordable route; a custom-designed theme with one-click checkout, ERP or app integrations costs more, and Shopify Plus or headless builds sit at the top. Our Shopify website cost guide lists indicative market ranges by tier, and every gaa-tha quote is itemised after a short discovery call.
