Marketplaces

Live, visible and profitable on India's biggest marketplaces.

Marketplaces is the gaa-tha practice that gets brands listed, visible and profitable on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, AJIO, Meesho and JioMart: seller onboarding and category approvals, catalogue and A+ content, listing optimisation (marketplace SEO), sponsored ads, deals and events, ratings management and reconciliation.

Good to know

Questions about marketplaces

Marketplace account management is the ongoing operation of a brand's seller account on platforms like Amazon and Flipkart: listings and content, pricing and deals, sponsored ads, inventory and fulfilment planning, account health, reviews and monthly reporting. gaa-tha runs it as a monthly retainer with a named account manager.

Yes — seller registration, Brand Registry or brand approval, category approvals, catalogue creation, A+ content and the go-live checklist, then optional monthly management.

Amazon for most categories because of reach and Brand Registry tools; Flipkart close behind for value-conscious and tier-2 buyers; Myntra, Nykaa and AJIO for fashion and beauty; Meesho and JioMart for price-led mass reach. We prioritise by category, margin and where your competitors already sell.

Usually, yes. Most stalled listings have one of four problems: weak titles and images, no reviews, uncompetitive pricing, or no advertising. We audit the account, fix the listings, set up sponsored ads and a review process, then track rank and conversion weekly.

Orders, revenue, ACoS/TACoS on ads, organic rank for target keywords, conversion rate, rating and review count, account health, and net margin after fees and returns — reported monthly in a P&L-style view.
