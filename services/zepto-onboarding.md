## What is Zepto Onboarding?

Zepto onboarding is the process of getting a brand approved, catalogued and stocked on Zepto, the Mumbai-founded 10-minute delivery platform. It covers business and brand documents, category and licence checks, commercial terms, catalogue creation to Zepto's image and attribute specifications, and the first purchase order into Zepto's warehouses and dark stores. Zepto is inventory-led for most categories, so the brand supplies against POs rather than shipping to customers.

gaa-tha prepares and runs the application end to end. We check that your category, shelf life and pack sizes fit Zepto's range, model the margin after platform terms, assemble and verify the document pack, build the catalogue and thumbnails, and coordinate the first PO. Zepto's strongest markets are Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune, with a fast-growing beauty and personal care range alongside grocery, so we plan launch cities and the lead SKUs around where your category already moves.

## Frequently asked questions

### What documents do I need to sell on Zepto?

Usually GST registration, PAN, bank proof, a trademark certificate or brand authorisation letter, FSSAI licence for food and beverages, and any category-specific licence such as cosmetics or Ayurvedic product approvals. Zepto also asks for product master data, MRP and barcode details and pack images. We share a current checklist because requirements change by category.

### How long does Zepto onboarding take?

Typically 3–6 weeks from a complete document pack to the first purchase order. Registration and category review come first, then catalogue QC, then the first PO and dispatch. Delays almost always trace back to missing licences, barcodes or product data, which is why we verify the pack before submitting.

### Are there fees to get listed on Zepto?

Zepto works on category margins and may ask for listing, onboarding or marketing contributions that differ by category and change over time. We do not publish figures because they vary brand to brand; we obtain the current terms for your category and explain them before you commit to anything.

### Which categories does Zepto accept?

Grocery and staples, fresh and dairy, snacks and beverages, beauty and personal care, health and wellness, baby care, pet care, home and kitchen, toys, stationery and seasonal ranges, with pharmacy and café handled separately. Zepto has invested heavily in beauty and premium grocery, so those categories often get faster attention if the range fits.

### How does Zepto's dark-store model work for a brand?

Zepto raises purchase orders, receives stock at city warehouses and distributes it to dark stores that each carry a curated range. Brands earn shelf space through sell-through, so a focused range with reliable supply does better than a long list of SKUs. We plan first-PO quantities city by city.

### Which cities should I launch on Zepto first?

Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad are Zepto's deepest markets, and the right answer is where your brand already has distribution and demand among them. Adding cities as sell-through proves the range keeps availability high and protects your search rank.

### What happens at go-live?

We confirm every listing is live and searchable in each launch city, check pricing, thumbnails and pack-size titles, verify stock is showing as available in the dark stores that received it, and set up the availability tracker. The account then moves to monthly management or your own team with an SOP.

### Should I onboard Zepto or Blinkit first?

If Mumbai, Bengaluru or Hyderabad are your priority cities, Zepto is usually first; if Delhi NCR and the north, Blinkit. Most brands are on both within a quarter, and the document pack is largely reusable. We compare category demand in your cities before recommending an order.
