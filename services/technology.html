Good to know

Questions about websites & apps

Corporate, B2B, manufacturing and real-estate websites; portfolio and brand websites; Shopify and custom ecommerce stores; iOS and Android apps built with Flutter, React Native or native code; ecommerce and sales-team ordering apps; and SaaS products from MVP to multi-tenant platforms. Design, development, content and launch are handled by one in-house team.

Landing pages take about a week, corporate and brand websites two to five weeks, and larger sites six to eight. An app MVP usually takes eight to fourteen weeks and a SaaS MVP ten to sixteen, depending on features, integrations and how quickly decisions and content arrive.

Shopify for stores selling a standard catalogue; WordPress or Webflow for content-heavy marketing sites your team edits daily; custom code (for example Next.js) when speed, unusual features, integrations or scale matter most. We recommend the platform after discovery, based on who will maintain it and what it has to do.

Both. Most business and ecommerce apps are built cross-platform in Flutter or React Native so one codebase ships to iOS and Android; native Swift and Kotlin are used when an app needs deep device features or maximum performance.

Websites ship with clean semantic HTML, structured data, fast Core Web Vitals, an XML sitemap, llms.txt and answer-first copy — the same SEO, AEO and GEO approach we use on gaa-tha.com. Apps get app-store optimisation and a companion web presence so they can be found and recommended.

You do. Domains, hosting, app-store accounts, repositories and data are set up in your name, and everything is handed over at launch with documentation. There is no lock-in to gaa-tha.
