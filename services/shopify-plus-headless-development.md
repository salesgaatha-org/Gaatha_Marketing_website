## What is Shopify Plus & Headless Shopify Development?

Shopify Plus is Shopify's enterprise plan, adding deeper checkout customisation, automation, B2B and wholesale tools, expansion stores and higher API limits; headless Shopify means keeping Shopify as the commerce engine — products, inventory, checkout and orders — while the storefront customers see is a separate front end built with Hydrogen, Next.js or another framework. Both are ways to go beyond what a standard theme on a standard plan can do, and neither is right for every brand.

At gaa-tha we start by asking what is actually limiting growth — checkout logic, wholesale, international pricing, speed, content or integrations — and solve it with the least complex option that works. Often that is Plus features on a well-built theme; sometimes it is a headless storefront. Either way the work uses Shopify's current extension model, so it survives platform updates instead of breaking on them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Shopify Plus worth it?

Shopify Plus is worth it when you need what Plus provides — full checkout customisation, B2B at scale, expansion stores, higher API limits and advanced automation — or when your sales volume makes its fee structure work out cheaper than a lower plan. Many brands move to Plus too early, for the badge rather than a feature they will use. We compare your current plan, the features you need and Shopify's current Plus pricing before recommending a switch.

### Headless Shopify or a Shopify theme — which should we choose?

A well-built Shopify theme is the right choice for most brands: it is faster to build, cheaper to run, and your marketing team can edit it in the theme editor. Headless makes sense when you need a highly custom experience, content from a separate CMS, one storefront across several systems, or performance and flexibility a theme cannot reach. It brings a higher build cost and a developer dependency for changes, so we recommend it only when those benefits are clear.

### Hydrogen or Next.js for a headless Shopify store?

Hydrogen, Shopify's own React-based framework with commerce components and Oxygen hosting, is usually the fastest route when Shopify is your main system. Next.js on Vercel suits teams that already use it, combine several data sources, or run a large content site alongside the store. Both use the Storefront API and hand off to Shopify's checkout, so the choice comes down to your team and the rest of your stack.

### What can checkout extensibility and Shopify Functions do?

Checkout UI extensions add your own blocks to checkout — upsells, gift messages, delivery notes, trust badges and loyalty — while Shopify Functions change the logic behind it, such as custom discounts, tiered bundles, hiding or reordering payment and delivery options, and blocking invalid carts. Some of this, particularly extensions on the information, shipping and payment steps, is reserved for Shopify Plus, while other parts work on every plan, so we confirm what your plan supports before designing.

### Can we sell B2B and D2C from the same Shopify store?

Yes. Shopify's B2B features let one store serve retail shoppers and business buyers, with company accounts, customer-specific catalogues and price lists, payment terms and quantity rules. The fullest B2B toolset is aimed at Shopify Plus, and some B2B features have reached other plans in more limited form, so we check current plan availability against your wholesale needs.

### How do we sell internationally from one Shopify store?

Shopify Markets lets one store sell to several countries with local currencies, languages, domains or subfolders, and region-specific prices and catalogues. For Indian brands the work also covers an international payment gateway, cross-border shipping, duties and taxes, product compliance and localised content. Brands with very different ranges or operations per region may run separate expansion stores instead.

### Can Shopify integrate with our ERP or order-management system?

Yes. We integrate Shopify with SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, Odoo, Zoho, Tally Prime and order-management systems such as Unicommerce or EasyEcom, using ready connectors where they are reliable and custom middleware where they are not. Products, prices, stock, orders, invoices and returns sync on a schedule or in real time, with alerts when something fails.

### What should we look for in a Shopify Plus or headless development partner?

Look for a partner that builds on Shopify's current extension model, can explain when not to go headless, has integrated systems like yours, and leaves the code, apps and accounts in your ownership. Ask how they test discount and pricing logic, handle Shopify's platform updates and support you after launch. At gaa-tha, custom apps sit in your Shopify organisation, code lives in your repository, and everything is documented for handover.
