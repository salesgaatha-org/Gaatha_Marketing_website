## What is White-Label PPC & Meta Ads Management?

White-label PPC is paid media management that an agency sells under its own brand while a partner runs the campaigns. The agency owns the client relationship and strategy conversations; the partner handles account structure, campaign builds, audiences, creative testing, bidding, tracking and reporting.

gaa-tha runs Meta and Google Ads for its own ecommerce and lead-generation clients, including campaigns across Canada for a retail client, and does the same work white-label for agencies. We are added through Meta Business Manager partner access or a Google Ads manager account, the client pays the platforms directly, and reports go out in your agency's template.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is white-label PPC?

White-label PPC is paid ads management that your agency sells under its own name while a partner runs the campaigns. gaa-tha manages Meta and Google Ads for US and UK agencies, reporting in your branding and working only through you.

### Do you need ownership of the ad accounts?

No. The client keeps ownership of the ad accounts and pays Meta and Google directly. We are added through Meta Business Manager partner access or your Google Ads manager account with only the permissions we need.

### Which KPIs do you report on?

Whatever the client is judged on: CPA, ROAS, cost per qualified lead, MER or new-customer revenue. We agree the primary KPI during the audit and report against it every month.

### Do you also make the ad creative?

Yes. Our in-house team produces static ads, UGC-style edits and short video ads for testing, so creative is not the bottleneck. Agencies can also supply their own creative.

### Can you work with our existing reporting tools?

Yes. We report in your Looker Studio, AgencyAnalytics, DashThis or slide template, or build a dashboard in your branding if you do not have one.

### How do you start?

With a paid pilot on one account: an audit, tracking fixes and a month of management. You see the change log and results before adding more accounts.
