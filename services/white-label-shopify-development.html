The service

What is White-Label Shopify Development?

White-label Shopify development means an outside team builds and maintains Shopify stores that an agency delivers to its clients under its own name. The agency keeps the client relationship, the design direction and the account management; the development partner turns approved designs into a fast, editable Online Store 2.0 theme, handles migrations and integrations, and fixes issues after launch.

gaa-tha builds Shopify stores for its own clients and for agencies. We work from your Figma files or brief, develop on preview themes with Shopify's GitHub integration, test on real devices and Core Web Vitals, and hand over documentation in your agency's name. US and UK brands that want to outsource directly get the same team, with the store, apps and code always owned by them.

Scope

What's included

  • Custom Shopify themes built from your Figma or Adobe XD designs
  • Online Store 2.0 sections and blocks your clients can edit themselves
  • Migrations from WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Wix or Squarespace with redirects
  • Shopify Plus work: checkout extensibility, Shopify Functions, B2B and multi-market setups
  • App and integration setup: subscriptions, reviews, loyalty, ERP, email and SMS
  • Tracking setup: GA4, Meta Pixel with Conversions API and Google Ads conversions
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals optimization before launch
  • Code in your GitHub repository and collaborator access, never our own accounts
  • Unbranded handover documentation and a post-launch fix window

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Brief and access

You share designs or a brief; we request collaborator access and agree the repository, preview theme and QA checklist.

02

Build plan

We break the store into templates, sections and integrations with a timeline you can share with your client.

03

Develop on preview

Development happens on an unpublished theme connected to GitHub, with progress links you can review any time.

04

QA and speed

Cross-browser and device testing, Theme Check, accessibility basics, tracking verification and Core Web Vitals checks.

05

Launch and support

We launch with you, monitor the first days, fix issues in the agreed window and document everything unbranded.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Production-ready Shopify theme in your or your client's GitHub
  • Editable sections and blocks with a usage guide
  • Migration and redirect map for replatforming projects
  • Tracking verified on a real test order
  • Unbranded handover documentation
