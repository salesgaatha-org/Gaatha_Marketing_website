The service

What is D2C Brand Launch?

A D2C brand launch is the end-to-end process of taking a product from concept to its first customers online: positioning and naming, brand identity and packaging, product photography and copy, an ecommerce store with payments and shipping, tracking, a launch campaign and the operating rhythm for the first weeks. Done as one project it avoids the usual gap where the brand agency finishes and the store and ads start from scratch.

gaa-tha runs launches for founders and for manufacturers moving from white-label or B2B supply into their own consumer brand. Strategy comes first — who the buyer is, what the product is for, why it costs what it costs — and every asset from the logo to the first reel is made against that. The Shopify store, Meta ads, influencer seeding and WhatsApp follow-ups are built by the same team, so launch day is a campaign, not a hope.

Scope

What's included

  • Positioning workshop: buyer, category, price point, promise and launch range
  • Naming, logo and brand identity system with guidelines
  • Packaging design and print-ready artwork with mandatory label information
  • Product photography and AI ad shoots for store, marketplace and social use
  • Shopify store design and development with checkout, shipping, COD rules and tracking
  • Launch content: Instagram profile, reels, statics and copy for the first 30 days
  • Launch campaign: Meta ads, influencer and UGC seeding, WhatsApp broadcast and Google Business Profile
  • Pre-launch waitlist or early-access offer and a first-order incentive
  • First-month operations: order handling SOP, customer replies, reviews and a launch report

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Strategy and positioning

We define the buyer, the category story, the launch range and the price architecture, and write the brand brief every team works from.

02

Brand and packaging

Name, identity, packaging and label artwork are designed, tested with real buyers where possible and prepared for print.

03

Store and content

The Shopify store is built while photography, copy and the first month of social content are produced in parallel.

04

Pre-launch and launch

A waitlist or early-access offer warms the audience, then ads, influencer drops, reels and WhatsApp go live together on launch day.

05

First month and handover

Orders, replies, reviews and ad performance are managed daily, then reported with a plan for the growth phase.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Brand strategy and positioning document
  • Brand identity, guidelines and packaging artwork
  • Product photo and video library
  • Live Shopify store with verified tracking
  • 30-day launch content and ad creative
  • Launch campaign setup across Meta, Instagram, influencers and WhatsApp
  • Launch report with first-month numbers and the growth plan
