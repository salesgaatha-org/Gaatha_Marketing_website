The service

What is Catalogues, Presentations & Sales Kits?

A sales kit is the set of documents a business uses to win an order: a product catalogue, a company profile, a pitch or investor deck, a price list and the brochures a buyer takes away from a meeting or a trade fair. Its job is to answer a buyer's questions — who are you, what do you make, at what capacity, at what price, and why should I trust you — before the salesperson gets a second call.

gaa-tha designs these as one family, so the catalogue, profile and deck share the same structure, photography and tone, and can be updated without a redesign. Documents are built to be sent on WhatsApp and email as light PDFs, printed properly for a sales meet or Diwali distribution, and reused as IndiaMART and LinkedIn assets — with the product data kept in a sheet so price and SKU changes are a quick edit.

Scope

What's included

  • Product catalogues for print and PDF, with SKU tables, specifications and imagery
  • Company profile decks and brochures
  • Investor and pitch decks with narrative, financial slide templates and design
  • Price lists and rate cards, sheet-linked for fast updates
  • Sales-meet and dealer-meet material: presentations, standees and handouts
  • Export brochures with certifications, capacity and compliance details
  • IndiaMART, TradeIndia and B2B marketplace listing assets
  • Product photography and infographics where needed
  • Copywriting or copy editing for every document
  • Print coordination and press-ready files

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Structure

We map who reads the document and what they need to decide, then write the page-by-page structure before any design.

02

Content

Product data, specifications, certifications and imagery are collected into a master sheet; missing photos and copy are produced by our team.

03

Design

A master layout system is designed and applied across the catalogue, profile and deck, so every product page and slide follows the same grid.

04

Proof

Two review rounds with sales and management; prices, codes and claims are checked against the master sheet before sign-off.

05

Deliver

Press-ready PDFs go to the printer, light PDFs go to WhatsApp and email, and editable files stay with you for quick updates.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Print-ready and digital PDF versions of every document
  • Editable source files in InDesign, Figma, PowerPoint or Google Slides
  • Master product data sheet linked to the catalogue
  • Lightweight WhatsApp and email versions
  • IndiaMART and B2B listing images and descriptions
  • Print coordination and proof approval
