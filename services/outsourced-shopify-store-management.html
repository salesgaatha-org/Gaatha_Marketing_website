The service

What is Outsourced Shopify Store Management?

Shopify store management is the ongoing operation of a live store: adding products and variants, changing prices and running promotions, updating collections and banners, keeping apps and the theme healthy, fixing bugs and keeping the store fast. Without it, launches wait for a developer and small issues cost sales.

gaa-tha provides it as a monthly retainer with a named store manager, a shared task board and agreed turnaround times. Because design, copy and paid media sit in the same team, a Black Friday sale or a product launch can be set up end to end. Agencies can resell the service under their own brand.

Scope

What's included

  • Product uploads and edits: SKUs, variants, images, descriptions, tags and metafields
  • Price changes, compare-at pricing, discount codes and automatic discounts
  • Homepage, collection, navigation and banner updates on a monthly calendar
  • Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday and seasonal sale setup
  • Quarterly app audit to remove unused apps and cut monthly charges
  • Bug fixes and theme tweaks with tracked turnaround times
  • Monthly speed and Core Web Vitals check with fixes
  • Tracking checks for GA4, Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversions
  • Monthly store report: orders, conversion, speed, errors and tasks completed

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Store audit

We review the theme, apps, tracking, speed and catalog quality, fix urgent issues and agree the monthly scope.

02

Task board

Every request goes on a shared board with an owner and due date, in your tool or ours.

03

Monthly calendar

Homepage, collections and promotions are planned a month ahead around launches and sale events.

04

Maintain

Apps, theme updates, speed and tracking are checked on a schedule and fixed before customers notice.

05

Report

A monthly report covers what changed, what broke, conversion trends and next month's recommendations.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Shared task board with agreed turnaround times
  • Monthly homepage, collection and promotion updates
  • Clean, consistent catalog data
  • Quarterly app and charges audit
  • Monthly store health report
