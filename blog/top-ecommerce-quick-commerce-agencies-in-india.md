## Key takeaways

- Compare agency types before comparing names; the type determines what you will get.
- Ask for scope, pricing model, reporting and proof in writing.
- Beware of guaranteed results and of fees that are not explained.

## What types of ecommerce agency exist in India?

| Type | What they do well | Where they fall short |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace specialists | Amazon and Flipkart onboarding, listings, ads, account health | Often weak on brand, content and the D2C store |
| Quick-commerce operators | Platform relationships, availability, purchase orders, q-com ads | May not cover creative, D2C or marketplaces |
| D2C and Shopify studios | Store builds, CRO, retention | May not run marketplaces or quick commerce |
| Performance-only shops | Meta and Google ads at scale | Creative, store and channel operations left to the brand |
| Full-service teams | Brand, content, store, marketplaces, quick commerce and ads as one plan | Fewer deep specialists; check depth per channel |

This post deliberately does not rank named agencies. Claims about other companies’ results and fees change and cannot be verified from outside, and a list that ranked competitors would not be honest.

## What questions separate good agencies from the rest?

- Which platforms have you onboarded brands on in the last year, and what was the timeline?

- What exactly is included in the monthly fee, and what is billed separately?

- How is ad spend handled, and is it paid directly to the platform?

- What does the monthly report contain, and can we see a sample?

- Who will work on our account day to day?

- Can we speak to a current client in a similar category?

- What happens to our accounts, assets and data if we leave?

## What pricing models will you see?

Most agencies charge a one-time onboarding or setup fee plus a monthly management fee, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms. Some charge a percentage of ad spend or of marketplace revenue; some offer project fees for store builds and creative. Fees vary widely by scope and change over time, so compare on what is included rather than on the headline number, and be cautious of any guarantee of sales, rank or return.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I choose an ecommerce agency in India?

Decide which channels you need, match that to the agency type, then compare scope, pricing model, reporting and verifiable proof. Ask to speak with a current client in a similar category.

### What does a quick-commerce agency charge?

Typically a one-time onboarding fee plus a monthly management fee, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms. Amounts vary by scope and platform count and change over time; get the structure in writing.

### Should I hire a specialist or a full-service agency?

A specialist suits a brand with one channel and in-house creative. A full-service team suits a brand that wants store, marketplaces, quick commerce and content run as one plan. Check depth per channel either way.

### Is it a red flag if an agency guarantees results?

Yes. Marketplace rank, ad return and quick-commerce placement depend on factors no agency controls. Look for clear process, honest reporting and specific past work instead.

### Why does this comparison not rank agencies by name?

Because claims about other companies’ results and fees cannot be verified from outside and change constantly. A framework you can apply is more useful, and more honest, than a ranked list.
