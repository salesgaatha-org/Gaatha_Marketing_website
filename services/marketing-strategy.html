The service

What is Marketing Strategy & Positioning?

A marketing strategy is the set of choices about who you serve, what you promise, which channels you will use and how success will be measured — written down before any campaign starts. It is not a content calendar or a list of posts; those are tactics, and strategy is what makes the tactics agree with each other.

At gaa-tha the strategy phase starts with research, not opinion: your market, your competitors, the people who already buy from you and the reasons they give. From that we write the positioning, the messaging, the offer, the channel plan with a budget split, a 90-day roadmap and the KPI framework — so every rupee spent afterwards has a job.

Scope

What's included

  • Market and competitor research — category, pricing, claims, channels and gaps
  • Customer interviews and audience personas with buying triggers and objections
  • Positioning statement and messaging hierarchy (promise, proof points, tone)
  • Offer design — what you sell, at what price point, with what hook
  • Channel plan and budget split across social, Meta and Google ads, search, marketplaces and offline
  • 90-day roadmap with owners, milestones and content and campaign priorities
  • KPI framework — one primary number per objective, with targets and a reporting cadence
  • Founder and sales-team workshop to align on the strategy before execution
  • Creative and content brief for the team executing the plan

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Discovery

A working session with the founders and sales team on goals, margins, current channels and what has and has not worked, plus access to analytics and ad accounts.

02

Research

We study the category, competitors, search demand and customer conversations, and interview a handful of existing customers to hear the words they use.

03

Positioning and offer

We write the positioning, the messaging hierarchy and the offer, and test them against the alternatives your buyers are already considering.

04

Channel plan and roadmap

Channels are ranked by where your buyers actually look, budget is split accordingly, and a 90-day roadmap sets the order of work.

05

Workshop and handover

The strategy is presented to your team, questions are worked through live, and the KPI framework and briefs are handed to whoever executes — us or your in-house team.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Marketing strategy document (research, positioning, messaging, offer)
  • Audience personas with triggers, objections and channel habits
  • Channel plan with budget split and expected roles per channel
  • 90-day roadmap with owners and milestones
  • KPI framework and a reporting template
  • Creative and content brief for execution

Questions about Marketing strategy

Two to three weeks for most Indian SMEs and D2C brands: one week of discovery and research, one week of positioning and channel planning, and a few days for the workshop and handover. Brands with several product lines or export markets may need an extra week of research.

Yes, at least a short one. Without agreed positioning, an offer and a primary metric, ads and content are tested against nothing, and money goes to whatever looks busy. A two-week strategy phase typically saves far more than it costs in wasted spend and redone creative.
