## What is Outsourced Reel & Short-Form Video Editing?

Outsourced reel editing means sending your raw footage to an editing team that returns finished short-form videos ready to post or run as ads. The value is speed and consistency: a steady weekly batch in your style, rather than editing squeezed between other work.

gaa-tha edits reels and ad videos for its own brand clients every week. For agencies and creators we work from a shared drive or Frame.io, follow your brand kit and style references, add captions, hooks, b-roll and sound, and deliver unbranded files in every aspect ratio you need.

## How we work

1. **Style and brief** — Brand kit, references, platforms and posting cadence agreed under NDA.
2. **Paid test batch** — A small batch edited from your footage so you can judge style and speed.
3. **Footage handoff** — You upload raw footage to a shared drive or Frame.io with a short brief per video.
4. **Edit and review** — Edits delivered in batches with comments and revisions in your review tool.
5. **Deliver and repeat** — Final files in every ratio, named and organized, on a weekly or monthly rhythm.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I outsource reel editing to India?

Yes. Send raw footage through a shared drive or Frame.io with a short brief and style references; an editing team returns finished reels in batches. gaa-tha starts with a paid test batch so you can judge style and speed first.

### What do you need from us?

Raw footage, your brand kit (fonts, colors, logo), two or three reference videos you like, the platforms and the posting schedule. A short brief per video helps with hooks and calls to action.

### Do you add captions and music?

Yes. On-screen captions are standard. For music we use licensed tracks you provide or advise using platform audio at posting, because licensing rules differ between organic posts and ads.

### Can you edit white-label for our agency?

Yes. Files are unbranded, we work only through your agency and we sign an NDA and non-solicitation agreement.

### Do you also shoot or create AI video?

For India-based shoots, yes. For US and UK clients we edit your footage, UGC and stock, and can produce AI-assisted visuals and motion graphics.

### How are revisions handled?

Through timestamped comments in Frame.io or your review tool, with revision rounds agreed in the retainer.
