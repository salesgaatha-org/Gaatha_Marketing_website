Colortek

Making a 25-year coatings manufacturer look as advanced as its technology.

A credibility, brand and SEO/AEO/GEO audit, a naming exercise and the 'Colortek Veris' sub-brand direction for Colourtech Coating India.

The brief

What the client needed

Colortek (Colourtech Coating India) has 25 years of technical substance — compliance, specialised coating lines, demanding industrial customers. The website undermined it: contradictory figures, broken metadata, duplicate content, no structured data and performance bloat. The brand name also sat in a crowded space of similar-sounding companies.

The brief was to make the company look as advanced online as it is on the factory floor, without losing recognition built over two decades.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Website audit

A credibility, brand, SEO/AEO/GEO and UX audit that documented every contradiction, broken tag, duplicate page, missing schema and performance issue — with a prioritised fix list.

Website audit · 16:10

Brand evolution

A naming exercise leading to the 'Colortek Veris' direction: keep the existing wordmark and colour swoosh for continuity, add a premium sub-brand for the advanced coating range.

Brand evolution · 16:10

Brand brief

A seven-page branding brief covering the case for change, naming rationale, identity continuity, discoverability strategy and a phased rollout the company could approve section by section.

Brand brief · 16:10

Proposed engagement

Website redesign, a company presentation, LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook content, and ongoing SEO/AEO/GEO maintenance.
