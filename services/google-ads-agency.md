## What is Google Ads Agency?

Google Ads management is the ongoing work of running paid campaigns across Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display and Maps: researching the keywords and intents worth paying for, structuring campaigns and ad groups, writing ads, setting up conversion tracking, managing bids and budgets, and cutting the searches that waste money. Unlike social ads, Google reaches people who are already looking.

At gaa-tha every Google Ads account is built from the conversion backwards. We agree what a lead or sale is worth, make sure GA4 and Google Tag Manager record it correctly, and then structure Search, Performance Max and Shopping campaigns to hit that cost per result — with a landing-page review, because the best ad in the world cannot fix a slow page with no phone number.

1. **Audit and targets** — We review the account, past search terms, tracking and landing pages, then agree the cost per lead or cost per sale the account is run against.
2. **Tracking and structure** — GA4 and Google Tag Manager conversions are fixed or set up and verified, Merchant Center is connected where needed, and the campaign structure is built by intent.
3. **Ads and landing pages** — Responsive search ads, Performance Max assets and video ads are written and produced, and we send a short list of landing-page fixes before launch.
4. **Launch and optimise** — Campaigns go live with daily monitoring in week one, then weekly optimisation of bids, budgets, search terms and negative keywords.
5. **Report and scale** — Monthly reporting against the target, a review of lead quality with your sales team, and scaling into new keywords, locations or campaign types once cost per result is stable.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a Google Ads agency do?

A Google Ads agency plans, builds and optimises paid campaigns on Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display and Maps: keyword research, account structure, ad copy and assets, conversion tracking, bid and budget management and reporting. gaa-tha runs this as a monthly retainer on an account you own.

### Google Ads or Meta ads — which should I start with?

Google Ads when people already search for what you sell — B2B products, services, education, real estate, repairs, anything with clear intent. Meta ads when the product needs to be shown to people who were not looking, which is most D2C, fashion, food and lifestyle. Many brands run both, with Google catching demand and Meta creating it.

### How much should I spend on Google Ads to start?

Enough to get clicks on your core keywords every day for two to three weeks so the account has data to learn from. The right figure depends on how expensive your keywords are and what a lead is worth to you, so we size the starting budget from your target cost per lead and the search volume, not from a fixed rule.

### Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. Our fee is a flat monthly management retainer scoped to the campaign types and the work involved. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google from your own account, so you see exactly what was spent.

### How long before Google Ads shows results?

Search campaigns usually generate leads in the first week because they reach people already looking. The first month is spent removing wasted searches and learning which keywords convert; stable cost per lead and scaling typically follow in the second and third month.

### Do I need a landing page or can ads go to my homepage?

A dedicated landing page almost always converts better than a homepage because it matches the search, loads fast and has one clear action — call, WhatsApp or form. We review your existing pages before launch and build a landing page if nothing suitable exists.

### Can you run Google Shopping ads for my store?

Yes. We set up Google Merchant Center, fix the product feed so items are approved, and run Shopping and Performance Max campaigns for Shopify, WooCommerce and custom stores, reported on ROAS and cost per purchase.

### What is Performance Max and should I use it?

Performance Max is a Google campaign type that runs across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps from one set of assets, with Google's automation choosing placements. It works well once conversion tracking is clean and there is some history; we usually start with Search and Shopping, then add Performance Max with brand exclusions so it does not just buy your own name.
