The service

What is Marketing Consulting & Fractional CMO?

Marketing consulting is senior, outside advice on what to sell to whom, through which channels, with what budget and how to measure it. A fractional CMO goes further: a part-time marketing leader who owns the plan, directs the team or agencies and reviews results every month.

gaa-tha's founders lead strategy and brand thinking for the studio's clients in India and Canada. We offer the same as a consulting project or monthly fractional retainer for US and UK brands, and because we also execute, the plan can be delivered by our team or handed to yours.

Scope

What's included

  • Marketing audit: positioning, funnel, channels, creative and tracking
  • Positioning and messaging framework
  • Go-to-market and channel plan with budget split
  • KPI framework and reporting setup
  • Creative direction and content pillars
  • Agency and freelancer briefs and reviews
  • Monthly performance review and next-month priorities
  • Optional execution by the gaa-tha team

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit

We review your numbers, funnel, channels, creative and competitors.

02

Strategy

Positioning, audience, channel mix, budget and KPIs written into one plan.

03

Roadmap

A 90-day roadmap with owners, whether your team, your agencies or ours.

04

Direct

Weekly or fortnightly working sessions in your business hours to steer execution.

05

Review

Monthly review of results against KPIs and the next month's priorities.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Marketing audit report
  • Positioning and messaging framework
  • Channel plan and budget model
  • 90-day roadmap
  • Monthly performance review
