Quick commerce (q-commerce) is the delivery of groceries, FMCG, beauty and everyday products in roughly 10–30 minutes from local dark stores. In India the platforms that matter are Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Flipkart Minutes, Amazon Now and BigBasket (BB Now).

gaa-tha onboards brands onto all of them and runs the catalogue, availability, ads and ratings as one operation.
