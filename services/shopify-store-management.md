## What is Shopify Store Management & Maintenance?

Shopify store management is the ongoing operation of a live store: adding and updating products, changing prices and MRPs, refreshing collections and banners, keeping apps and the theme working, fixing bugs, watching speed and preparing the store for sales and festivals. It is the difference between a store that was launched once and a store that is looked after.

gaa-tha takes this on as a monthly retainer with a named store manager, a shared task board and an agreed turnaround time for routine changes. Because design, copy, photography and performance marketing sit in the same team, a new launch or a Diwali sale is set up end to end — product pages, banners, offers, tracking — rather than being passed between vendors.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Shopify store management include?

Product and price updates, collection and banner changes, app management, bug fixes, speed checks, festive-season setup, tracking checks and a monthly health report. It is everything a store needs after launch that would otherwise wait for a developer. gaa-tha runs it as a monthly retainer with a named store manager.

### How quickly are changes made?

Routine changes such as a price update, a new banner or a product upload are typically turned around within one to two working days, and urgent fixes on the same day. Larger changes such as a new landing page or a custom section are scheduled on the shared board with a date.

### Can you handle bulk price or MRP changes?

Yes. Bulk changes are done through a spreadsheet import with a backup taken first, checked against a sample of products, and scheduled for a time when traffic is low. Compare-at prices, discount codes and automatic discounts are updated together so the store never shows a mismatched offer.

### Do you set up Diwali and other festive sales?

Yes. Offer structure, sale collections, homepage takeover banners, countdowns, gift options, free-shipping thresholds and a checkout stress check are set up ahead of the event and rolled back afterwards. Ads and creative for the sale can be run by the same team.

### Will you audit the apps I already have?

Yes, at onboarding and then quarterly. Unused and overlapping apps are the most common cause of slow stores and rising monthly bills, so we list every app, its charge and its purpose, remove what is redundant and replace what is slowing pages.

### Do we still own the store and all accounts?

Yes. The store, domain, payment gateway, apps and ad accounts stay in your name. We work through staff accounts with the permissions needed, and everything we build is documented so you can change agencies or bring it in-house.

### Can you also manage our marketplace and quick-commerce listings?

Yes. Many brands ask us to keep the Shopify store, Amazon and Flipkart listings and Blinkit or Zepto catalogues consistent — same images, same prices, same launches — and we run those as one operation with one monthly review.
