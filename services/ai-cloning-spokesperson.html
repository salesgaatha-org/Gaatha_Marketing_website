The service

What is AI Cloning (Digital Spokesperson)?

AI cloning, or a digital spokesperson, is a licensed digital twin of a real person — their face, voice and mannerisms captured with consent — that can deliver new scripts on camera without the person being filmed each time. It lets a founder appear in weekly explainers, product updates, festival greetings and ads in several languages while spending a single afternoon in front of a camera.

gaa-tha runs the service consent-first: a written agreement sets what the twin may say, where it may appear and who approves each video, and every output goes through that approval before it is published. Scripts are written by our content team in your tone of voice, produced with the twin in Hindi, Gujarati, English or other languages, and finished with captions, B-roll and brand graphics so the videos look like the rest of your content.

Scope

What's included

  • Consent and usage agreement covering rights, scope, approvals and revocation
  • Voice and likeness capture session with scripted and conversational footage
  • Digital twin creation and quality testing
  • Script-to-video production from your content calendar
  • Multilingual versions with lip-sync in Hindi, Gujarati, English and other languages
  • Captions, B-roll, brand graphics and music
  • Brand-approval workflow on every output before publishing
  • Secure storage and access control for the twin
  • Monthly review of what performed and next month's scripts

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Consent and scope

We agree in writing what the digital spokesperson will be used for, on which platforms, who approves each video, and how consent can be withdrawn.

02

Capture

A half-day session records the face and voice in the right conditions — scripted lines, natural conversation, a few expressions — so the twin looks and sounds like the person.

03

Build and test

The twin is created and tested with real scripts; the founder reviews and signs off on how it looks and sounds before any content is produced.

04

Produce

Each month scripts are written from your calendar, produced with the twin, finished with captions and graphics, and sent for approval.

05

Publish and review

Approved videos go out on your channels or into ads; we review performance monthly and adjust formats, lengths and languages.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Signed consent and usage agreement
  • Tested digital twin (voice and likeness) with founder sign-off
  • Monthly batch of approved spokesperson videos in 9:16 and 16:9
  • Multilingual versions with captions
  • Approval log for every published video
  • Access-controlled asset store
