The brief

What the client needed

Charliee is a large regional snack brand with more than 200 SKUs across namkeen, chikki, khakhra, chips, sweets and bakery. Its online store did not reflect the quality of the products, the catalogue was inconsistent, and the brand needed marketing assets that worked for two very different buyers: domestic consumers and white-label export customers.

The brief was to rebuild the store as a proper sales channel, bring the catalogue under control, and give the field sales team and export desk material they would actually use.

What we did

The work, stream by stream

Shopify website

A full UI/UX audit and overhaul of the store, starting with a homepage prototype and a product-content workbook covering all 200+ SKUs. We rebuilt the product cards and contact page, added a corporate-gifting page and category cover imagery, and built a batch-number lookup tool so customers can check a pack's details. Store handling is ongoing, including bulk MRP updates across categories.

Shopify website · 16:10

Branding & creative

Category visuals for the store and social, AI-produced product imagery for the long tail of SKUs, and cinematic product ads for launches and festive pushes.

Branding & creative · 16:10

B2B & export creative

Advertising creatives that position Charliee as a white-label manufacturing partner for export buyers, separate in tone and format from the consumer-facing work.

B2B & export creative · 16:10

Sales enablement

An annual sales-meet kit for the field team across Gujarat and Mumbai — target workbooks, price lists, a standee and the speech — so the plan for the year was in every rep's hands on day one.
