Quick Commerce

Quick Commerce Ads

gaa-tha runs quick commerce ads for brands in India: sponsored search and product listing ads, category placements and banner campaigns on Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, structured by SKU and city, switched off where stock is out, and optimised weekly against a target ROAS — with spend paid directly to each platform and reported alongside availability and sales.

The service

What is Quick Commerce Ads?

Quick commerce ads are the paid placements inside 10-minute delivery apps — sponsored products on search results, sponsored slots on category pages and banner placements on the home and category screens — on Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, with Flipkart Minutes and Amazon Now reached through their parent marketplaces' ad systems. Because the shopper is minutes from buying, these ads convert on stock and price as much as on creative, and they only work where the SKU is actually available in that city.

At gaa-tha quick-commerce ads are run by the same team that tracks your availability, so budget follows stock. We structure campaigns by SKU, city and platform, agree a target ROAS from your margin, bid on the search terms shoppers actually type for your category, and pause any city where the product is out of stock. Reporting shows spend, ROAS and sales next to availability and organic rank, because a good ad month on quick commerce is usually a good availability month.

Scope

What's included

  • Ad account setup or audit on Blinkit Ads, Zepto Ads and Swiggy Instamart Ads
  • Campaign structure by SKU, city and platform with a target ROAS derived from margin
  • Keyword research per platform: category terms, competitor terms, occasions and pack-size searches
  • Sponsored product and search campaigns with weekly bid and budget optimisation
  • Category-page and banner campaigns for launches, festive periods and platform sale events
  • Availability-linked pausing so ads do not run in cities where the SKU is out of stock
  • Creative for banners and thumbnails produced in-house to each platform's specifications
  • Coordination with promotions so ads and offers land in the same week
  • Monthly report on spend, ROAS, sales attributed, organic rank movement and availability by city

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and targets

We review current ad accounts, availability by city, margins and past performance, then agree the target ROAS and the SKUs to lead with.

02

Structure and keywords

Campaigns are built by SKU, city and platform, with keyword lists researched per platform and negative terms set from day one.

03

Creative and placements

Banners and thumbnails are produced to each platform's specifications and placements chosen for launches, sale events and always-on search.

04

Launch and optimise

Campaigns go live with daily checks in week one, then weekly optimisation of bids, budgets and keywords, and pausing wherever stock runs out.

05

Report and scale

Monthly reporting on spend, ROAS, sales and rank next to availability, with scaling rules for the SKUs and cities that are working.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Configured ad accounts on each platform with campaign naming conventions
  • Keyword and negative-keyword lists per platform and category
  • Banner and thumbnail creative sets for each campaign
  • Weekly optimisation log with availability-linked pauses
  • Monthly quick-commerce ads report with spend, ROAS, sales and rank by city
