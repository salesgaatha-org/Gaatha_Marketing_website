## Frequently asked questions

### Do you build and manage Shopify stores?

Yes. We design and develop Shopify stores (custom themes, custom sections, checkout and payment integrations, tracking) and run monthly store management for product uploads, price changes, banners, apps and fixes.

### Shopify or WooCommerce — which should I choose in India?

Shopify for most D2C brands: faster to launch, easier to maintain, and the Indian checkout, COD and shipping apps are mature. WooCommerce or a custom build makes sense when you need deep customisation, an existing WordPress site, or very specific B2B pricing logic.

### Can one website serve multiple store locations or franchises?

Yes. We build multi-location Shopify stores where the customer enters a pincode or picks an outlet, and the order is routed to that outlet with WhatsApp notifications and outlet-level availability and pricing rules.

### How long does a Shopify store take?

Two to six weeks depending on catalogue size, custom sections and integrations. Migrations from Amazon or another platform add time for product data cleanup.

### What does D2C performance marketing include?

Meta and Google shopping and catalogue ads, creative testing, ROAS and MER targets, attribution fixes (Conversions API, checkout tracking) and retention flows on email and WhatsApp — all reported against contribution margin, not just ad-platform ROAS.
