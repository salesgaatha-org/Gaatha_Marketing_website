The service

What is AI Chatbot Development?

An AI chatbot is a conversational assistant on your website or WhatsApp that answers customer questions in natural language using a large language model grounded in your own content — catalogue, FAQs, shipping and return policies — and that can look up live data such as order status or stock. A well-built one answers only from approved sources, admits when it does not know and passes the customer to a person instead of guessing.

At gaa-tha a chatbot project starts from your real customer conversations: we read past WhatsApp chats, emails and tickets to find the questions that repeat, then build the knowledge base, the flows for orders and leads, and the handoff rules. The bot is tested in English and the Indian languages your customers write in, connected to Shopify or your CRM, and reviewed every week in its first month using the conversation analytics.

Scope

What's included

  • Conversation audit — the top questions, intents and drop-off points from your existing chats and tickets
  • Knowledge base built from your catalogue, FAQs, size guides and shipping, return and warranty policies, with retrieval (RAG)
  • Website chat widget styled to your brand, on Shopify, WordPress or a custom-coded site
  • WhatsApp Business API setup through Meta's Cloud API or a Business Solution Provider, with message templates and opt-in flows
  • Product recommendation and guided selling from live catalogue data
  • Order tracking, COD confirmation and return-request flows connected to Shopify and your shipping partner
  • Lead capture and qualification pushed to your CRM, a sheet or your sales team on WhatsApp
  • Replies in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish, in a tone set to your brand
  • Human handoff to a shared inbox with full conversation context, plus business-hours rules
  • Analytics on conversation volume, resolution rate, handoffs, unanswered questions and leads captured

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Read the conversations

We study your past chats, tickets and FAQs to list the questions that repeat and the ones that must always go to a person.

02

Build the knowledge and flows

Approved content is structured into a knowledge base, and flows are designed for recommendations, order tracking, lead capture and handoff.

03

Connect channels and systems

The bot is connected to your website, WhatsApp Business API number, Shopify, CRM and helpdesk, and message templates are submitted for Meta's approval.

04

Test in every language

Your team tests the bot with real questions in English, Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish, including awkward and off-topic ones, and answers are corrected before launch.

05

Launch and tune

The bot goes live, unanswered questions and handoffs are reviewed weekly, and the knowledge base is updated as products, prices and policies change.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Conversation audit and intent map
  • Live chatbot on your website and WhatsApp number
  • Knowledge base and approved WhatsApp message templates
  • Shopify, CRM and helpdesk integrations
  • Handoff inbox and escalation rules
  • Analytics dashboard, admin training and all credentials

Good to know

Questions about AI chatbots

Answer-first, so humans and AI assistants get the point quickly.

A rule-based chatbot follows pre-written menus and keywords, while an AI chatbot understands free-text questions and answers them in natural language from your own content. Rule-based bots break when a customer phrases something unexpectedly; AI bots handle variety but need grounding and testing so they stay accurate. We usually combine both — buttons for structured tasks such as order tracking, and AI for open questions.

Meta's policies also require the bot to serve your own customers, for support, orders or leads, rather than act as a general-purpose AI assistant.
