How much does a Shopify website cost in India in 2026?

By gaa-tha· 22 October 2026· 12 min read· Shopify
Blog cover · 16:9

The best budget for a first Shopify D2C store in India is usually about ₹75,000 to ₹2.5 lakh for the build, meaning a premium theme customised by a specialist studio, plus roughly ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 a month for Shopify, apps and upkeep before payment fees and marketing. These are indicative 2026 market ranges, not a quote. A DIY store can launch for far less; a custom-designed theme typically costs ₹2.5 lakh to ₹8 lakh or more.

Key takeaways

  • Indicative 2026 Indian market ranges for the build: DIY up to ₹40,000; freelancer setup ₹25,000–₹75,000; studio customisation ₹75,000–₹2.5 lakh; custom theme ₹2.5–8 lakh+; Shopify Plus or headless ₹8 lakh+.
  • Running costs (Shopify plan, apps, messaging, maintenance) usually exceed the build fee over three years, so budget both.
  • Payment gateway, one-click checkout and any Shopify transaction fees scale with revenue; model them at your expected order volume and COD share.
  • Phase the spend: launch lean on a well-built premium theme, then fund custom sections, CRO and retention once real data arrives.
  • Compare quotes on scope, not headline price: templates, custom sections, integrations, tracking, content, training and support.

What is the best budget to create a Shopify website in India?

For a first D2C brand selling a focused range, ₹75,000 to ₹2.5 lakh for the build is the budget that usually buys the most: a premium Shopify theme customised by a team that understands Indian checkout, COD and tracking, with a handful of custom sections where they matter. That is an indicative 2026 market range, not a gaa-tha price list; your number depends on catalogue size, integrations and content.

Why this band? Below it, stores are usually a theme with products dropped in: fine for testing demand, weak on conversion, tracking and speed. Above it, you are paying for a custom design system, complex features or Shopify Plus capabilities that a first-year brand rarely needs. The money saved is better spent on product photography, the first months of ads and retention.

Three situations justify spending more from day one: a brand with existing revenue migrating from another platform (see Shopify migration), a product that needs a custom tool such as a configurator or kit builder, and a brand whose positioning depends on a distinctive design. If you are still choosing a platform, read why Shopify is the best ecommerce platform for most brands first.

What goes into the cost of a Shopify website?

A Shopify website’s cost is the sum of one-time build costs and recurring running costs, and a quote that shows only one of them misleads. The components:

  1. Shopify subscription. A monthly plan fee, priced in INR for Indian stores, with a discount for annual billing. Plans and prices change, so check Shopify’s current India pricing page. Shopify Plus is a separate, much higher tier.
  2. Theme. Shopify’s own themes are free. Premium themes in the Shopify Theme Store are a one-time purchase, typically in the low hundreds of US dollars (roughly ₹15,000 to ₹45,000 at current exchange rates), with updates included.
  3. Design and development. The largest one-time cost: site structure, page design, theme customisation or a custom theme, custom sections, integrations, tracking and testing.
  4. Apps. Reviews, WhatsApp, loyalty, subscriptions, upsells, GST invoices. Most bill monthly, many in US dollars.
  5. Payment gateway fees. A percentage per transaction charged by Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue or PhonePe, varying by payment method and negotiable at volume. Shopify Payments is not generally available to Indian-registered stores, and Shopify may charge its own transaction fee on orders through third-party gateways depending on plan; confirm on the current pricing page.
  6. One-click checkout. GoKwik, Shiprocket Checkout and Razorpay Magic Checkout are usually priced as a percentage of order value or a per-order fee, sometimes with monthly minimums.
  7. Content and photography. Product photos, lifestyle images, short videos, and copy for products, collections and key pages.
  8. SEO setup. Site structure, redirects, metadata, structured data, collection copy and Google Search Console.
  9. Maintenance and support. Theme and app updates, fixes, new landing pages and campaign changes after launch.
  10. Smaller items. A domain (a .com or .in costs roughly ₹800 to ₹1,500 a year), business email, WhatsApp Business API message charges and shipping aggregator fees.

What does it cost to run a Shopify store every month?

A small Shopify store in India typically costs ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 a month to run before payment fees and marketing, and a scaling D2C brand with retention tools and a management retainer can spend ₹40,000 to ₹1 lakh or more. These are indicative ranges; the mix below decides your number.

What hidden costs catch Indian Shopify stores out?

The hidden costs that hurt most are app creep, COD returns, missing content and broken tracking, because none of them appears in a build quote.

  • App creep. Every trial app left installed bills monthly, and some leave code in the theme after removal.
  • Dollar billing and GST. Many apps and themes bill in US dollars, so exchange rates move the cost, and GST applies to imported digital services; check how it appears on your invoices.
  • Transaction fees. Model the gateway fee and any Shopify fee on third-party gateways together, at your real order volume.
  • COD and RTO. Return-to-origin shipments cost two-way shipping and packaging; a high COD share without verification is the most expensive hidden cost in Indian D2C. See COD vs prepaid.
  • Content gaps. Many quotes assume you supply photos and copy; if you cannot, the launch slips or the store goes live with weak content.
  • Migration losses. Moving from WooCommerce or a custom site without redirects and customer and order history costs rankings and repeat sales.
  • Theme upkeep. Heavily edited themes are harder to update; budget a little each year to keep the theme current.
  • Tracking fixes. A broken Meta Pixel, Conversions API or GA4 setup silently wastes ad budget; pay for verification, not just installation.

How should a first D2C brand phase its Shopify budget?

Phase Shopify spend in three stages so each rupee follows evidence rather than guesses.

Phase 1: launch (months 0–2)

A premium theme customised to the brand, product pages with real photography, an Indian payment gateway with COD rules, shipping integration, Meta Pixel with Conversions API, GA4, basic SEO and no more than eight to ten apps. Spend most of the one-time budget here but hold back 20 to 30 percent.

Phase 2: learn (months 3–6)

Use analytics and session recordings to find where buyers drop off, then spend on the specific fixes: a bundle or kit section, better product page content, a one-click checkout if prepaid conversion is weak, WhatsApp flows. This is where conversion rate optimisation earns its fee.

Phase 3: scale (months 6–18)

Once the store converts and the unit economics hold, invest in a custom theme or design refresh, loyalty and subscriptions, marketplace and ERP integrations, and international selling. Shopify Plus, a headless storefront or a mobile shopping app belong here, and only when order volume justifies them.

Why do Shopify website quotes in India vary so much?

Shopify quotes vary because they describe different scopes, not because some providers are cheap and others greedy. The biggest drivers:

  • Custom design versus theme customisation.
  • The number of unique templates: home, collections, product types, landing pages, about, blog.
  • Custom features such as combo builders, kit selectors, pincode checkers, quizzes and subscription flows.
  • Integrations: one-click checkout, shipping, WhatsApp, Tally or ERP, marketplaces.
  • Catalogue size and data work: products, variants, metafields, and migrating customers and orders.
  • Content: who writes copy and who shoots the products.
  • Tracking and speed work: Pixel, Conversions API, GA4 and Core Web Vitals.
  • Post-launch support: bug-fix window, training and any retainer.

The Kitcoz store shows the custom-feature end of the range: a concern-led store with a kit selector, combo builder, pincode checker, GoKwik checkout and Pixel and Conversions API tracking, all of which add scope beyond a theme setup.

Does building in India make sense for an international Shopify store?

Yes, for many brands in Canada, the UK, the Gulf and elsewhere, an Indian Shopify team offers strong value, provided the brief, time zones and communication are managed well. The cost picture differs abroad: Shopify Payments and Shop Pay are available in many countries, which simplifies checkout and fees, while apps and plans bill in local currency or US dollars. Ask an Indian partner about overlapping working hours, experience with your market’s payments, taxes and shipping, and who owns the accounts.
