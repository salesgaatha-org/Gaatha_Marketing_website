## What is Outsource Website Development to India?

Outsourcing website development to India means hiring an India-based team to design, build and maintain websites while you keep ownership of the strategy, the accounts and the code. Done well, it gives you senior development capacity with clear communication and documentation; done badly, it creates code nobody can maintain.

gaa-tha builds websites for its own clients, including a managed ecommerce site for a retailer in Canada, and works as a development partner for agencies. Every project starts with a written scope, runs on preview links and a shared task board during US business hours, and ends with code in your GitHub and documentation your next developer can follow.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it a good idea to outsource website development to India?

It can be, when the partner works in your accounts, documents everything, communicates in your hours and starts with a small paid project. gaa-tha works US business hours, keeps code in your GitHub and signs an NDA before the brief.

### Which platforms do you build on?

WordPress, Webflow and Shopify for marketing and ecommerce sites, and Next.js with React and Node.js for custom sites and web apps. We recommend the platform in discovery based on who will edit the site and what it must integrate with.

### Who owns the code and the website?

You do. Hosting, domains, CMS accounts and repositories are set up in your name, and code is handed over with documentation.

### Can you work from our agency's designs?

Yes. Many agencies send Figma files and we build them white-label, with preview links you can share with your client.

### How do we communicate during the project?

Through your Slack, Teams or email, a shared task board and scheduled calls in US business hours. You get preview links throughout the build.

### Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes. A support window is included after launch, and an optional monthly retainer covers updates, security patches, content changes and new pages.
