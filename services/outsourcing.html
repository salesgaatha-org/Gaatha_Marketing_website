Outsourcing and white-label services for US and UK agencies and brands

An India-based team that works your hours — for US and UK agencies and brands.

Outsourcing & White-Label is the gaa-tha practice for agencies and brands outside India. US and UK agencies use us as an unbranded delivery team for Shopify builds, websites, apps, ERP work, paid ads, social media and video editing; brands hire us directly for the same work on monthly retainers. We work US business hours, sign an NDA before the brief, never contact an agency's clients, agree to non-solicitation and start every relationship with a paid pilot.

Working together

How we work with US and UK teams

US business hours

Our team works your workday, so stand-ups, Slack replies, reviews and launches happen in US business hours. UK hours overlap with our day too.

NDA before the brief

We sign your NDA, or ours, before you share client names, designs or data.

Unbranded by default

For agencies, every deliverable, report and document carries your brand. We never contact your client unless you ask us to.

Non-solicitation

We agree in writing not to approach or accept work from your clients.

Paid pilot first

Every relationship starts with one fixed-scope project or batch, so you judge quality before committing to a retainer.

Easy to pay

International invoices paid by bank wire (SWIFT), PayPal or card through Stripe. Ad spend, licenses and subscriptions stay on your own accounts.

Process

How an engagement starts

Five steps, from first call to a monthly rhythm.

01

Intro call

A call in your business hours about the work, your clients or product and what good looks like.

02

NDA and brief

NDA signed, then a real brief. We reply with a written scope, team and timeline.

03

Paid pilot

One fixed-scope project or batch to test quality, speed and communication.

04

Retainer or team

Monthly capacity or a dedicated team, working inside your tools and accounts.

05

Monthly review

Quality, turnaround and pipeline reviewed every month so capacity scales with you.

Good to know

Questions about outsourcing & white-label

An India-based team gives US and UK agencies and brands senior delivery capacity across development, paid media and creative without hiring for every skill. It works when the partner communicates in your hours, works in your accounts and starts with a small paid project; gaa-tha does all three.

Yes. Our team works US business hours, so meetings, Slack replies and launches happen during your day. UK clients get overlap across the UK working day.

Shopify builds and store management, website and app development, ERP and Odoo work, Meta and Google ads management, social media management, reel and short-form video editing, and strategy support — all unbranded and delivered through your agency.

An NDA is signed before the brief, we work only through your agency and your tools, deliverables carry your brand, and our agreement includes non-solicitation of your clients.

We invoice internationally and accept bank wire (SWIFT), PayPal and card payments through Stripe. Ad spend, software licenses and platform subscriptions are paid by you directly to the providers.

Every engagement is custom-quoted after we see a real brief: a paid pilot first, then a fixed-scope project, a monthly retainer or a dedicated team. We do not publish rate cards because scope, volume and tools change the number.

Yes. gaa-tha runs an ongoing retainer for New Rave, a trading-card and hobbies retailer in Canada, covering strategy, Canada-wide campaigns, Instagram content, product data and its website. Most of our other clients are brands and manufacturers in India.
