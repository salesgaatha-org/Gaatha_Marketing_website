The service

What is Marketplace Listing & Catalogue Optimisation?

Marketplace listing optimisation, often called marketplace SEO, is the work of making a product listing rank higher in a marketplace's search results and convert more of the shoppers who see it: researching the keywords buyers use on that platform, writing titles, bullets and descriptions around them, filling every attribute so the listing appears in filters, adding backend search terms, building an image stack that answers the buyer's questions, and adding A+ or rich content where the platform supports it. Each marketplace has its own algorithm, so the same product is optimised differently on Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.

At gaa-tha optimisation is measured, not assumed. We benchmark the listing against the top competitors for its keywords, change the elements most likely to move rank and conversion first, and track organic rank, click-through, conversion and sales for each SKU before and after so the effect of the work is visible in the monthly report.

Scope

What's included

  • Listing audit against each platform's ranking factors and the top competitors for your keywords
  • Keyword research per platform using search-term data, autocomplete and competitor listings
  • Titles, bullets, key features and descriptions rewritten for search relevance and readability
  • Attribute completion so listings appear in filters: size, material, colour, fit, concern, pack size and more
  • Backend search terms and platform-specific fields such as Amazon's search terms and Flipkart's key features
  • Image stack planning: main image to spec, lifestyle, infographic, size, texture and packaging shots
  • A+ content and rich-content modules with comparison tables and brand story
  • Variation and parent–child structure fixes so reviews and rank are consolidated
  • Rank and conversion tracking by SKU with a before-and-after report

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and benchmark

Every SKU is scored against the platform's ranking factors and compared with the top listings for its keywords, and the SKUs with the most to gain are prioritised.

02

Keyword research

Search terms are gathered per platform from search data, autocomplete, competitor listings and category browse paths, then mapped to each SKU by intent.

03

Copy and attributes

Titles, bullets, descriptions, attributes and backend keywords are rewritten to the platform's rules, with variations structured so rank and reviews consolidate.

04

Images and A+ content

The image stack is planned and produced or retouched, and A+ or rich-content modules are designed to answer objections and cross-sell.

05

Upload, track and refresh

Changes go live in batches, rank and conversion are tracked by SKU, and the listing is refreshed each quarter or when the algorithm, competitors or season change.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Listing audit and competitor benchmark per SKU
  • Keyword map by platform and SKU
  • Optimised titles, bullets, descriptions, attributes and backend keywords, uploaded
  • Image stack briefs and finished images to platform specs
  • A+ and rich-content modules, published
  • Before-and-after rank and conversion report

Questions about Listing optimisation

Marketplace algorithms rank on relevance plus performance — conversion rate, sales velocity, price, availability and reviews — rather than on backlinks and site authority. Keywords still matter, but a listing that converts poorly will not hold rank however well it is written, so images, price and reviews are part of the work.
