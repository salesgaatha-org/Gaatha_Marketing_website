# Shopify speed and conversion checklist for Indian stores

> A fast Shopify store loads its first meaningful content on a mid-range phone in a couple of seconds, carries as few apps as possible, and presents the product, price, delivery and trust information without scrolling. A converting store then answers objections on the product page, removes surprises in the cart, and makes checkout short. This checklist covers both, in the order that produces the largest gains.

## Key takeaways

- Mobile is the store; test on a real mid-range phone, not a desktop.
- Images and apps are the usual causes of slow stores.
- The product page decides most purchases; the cart and checkout lose them.
- Measure before and after every change.

## What slows a Shopify store down?

- Uncompressed or oversized images, especially in hero banners and collections.

- Too many apps, each injecting scripts on every page, including apps that were uninstalled but left code behind.

- Third-party scripts for chat, reviews, pop-ups and tracking loaded before content.

- Heavy themes with unused features and fonts.

- Autoplaying video above the fold.

Audit with a browser performance tool on a throttled mobile connection, then remove or defer what is not earning its place. [Shopify development](https://gaa-tha.com/services/shopify-development) includes a speed pass on every build.

## What should the product page contain?

Above the fold on mobile: product name, price, variant selector, delivery estimate or pincode checker, and the add-to-cart button. Immediately below: the three reasons to buy, reviews, ingredients or specifications, size guidance, and returns policy. Combos and kits should be selectable without leaving the page. Copy should answer the questions customers actually ask, which [copywriting](https://gaa-tha.com/services/content-writing-copywriting) for product pages is built around.

## Where do carts and checkouts leak?

Carts leak on shipping surprises and coupon hunting; show delivery charges early and keep a visible coupon field or automatic discounts. Checkouts leak on payment friction and COD doubt; offer UPI and cards prominently, use a one-click checkout provider where it fits, and set COD rules by order value and pincode with confirmation by WhatsApp. Watch checkout completion rate by device and by payment method weekly.

Free-shipping thresholds and automatic combo discounts do double duty here: they lift order value and remove the reason to leave the cart in search of a code.

## How do you build trust without clutter?

Real reviews near the price, a returns policy near the add-to-cart, contact details in the footer, an About page with the people behind the brand, and honest delivery times. Avoid fake urgency counters and pop-ups on first visit; they cost trust and speed. The [Kitcoz](https://gaa-tha.com/work/kitcoz) build applied these principles with concern-led navigation, a combo kit selector, a pincode checker and a coupon-aware cart drawer.

Work through the checklist, measure conversion by device and source for two weeks after each change, and keep what moves the number. [Conversion rate optimisation](https://gaa-tha.com/services/conversion-rate-optimisation) makes this a monthly cycle.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good load time for a Shopify store in India?

Aim for the main content to appear within roughly two to three seconds on a mid-range phone over a mobile connection. Measure with a performance tool on a throttled connection rather than on office wifi.

### How many apps should a Shopify store have?

As few as the business needs. Each app adds scripts; audit quarterly, remove unused apps, and clean leftover code from the theme after uninstalling.

### Does a pincode checker improve conversion?

It removes a common doubt before add-to-cart by confirming delivery availability and timing, which tends to reduce drop-off, particularly for COD-heavy audiences. Measure it on your own store.

### Should I use a one-click checkout provider?

For many Indian D2C stores, providers such as GoKwik or Shopflo shorten checkout and add COD verification. Test against the standard checkout and compare completion rates.
