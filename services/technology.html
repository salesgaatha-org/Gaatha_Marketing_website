Websites & Technology

Sites, apps and tools built to convert and to be found.

Websites & Technology is the gaa-tha practice that builds the digital products behind a brand: fast, search-ready corporate and B2B websites, web and mobile app design, admin consoles and analytics dashboards, and the small custom tools — lead trackers, batch-number lookups, document automation — that make a sales team faster.

Good to know

Questions about websites & technology

Corporate and B2B sites for manufacturers and exporters, real-estate and project sites, investor-relations pages, landing pages for campaigns, and ecommerce stores (see D2C Ecommerce). All are built for speed, accessibility, SEO and AI-search visibility.

Landing pages in about a week; corporate sites in two to five weeks; larger sites with catalogues, tools or multiple languages in six to eight. Timelines depend mostly on how quickly content and approvals arrive.

Yes — UI/UX for web and mobile apps, admin consoles and dashboards, delivered as clickable prototypes and developer-ready design systems, with development for web apps in-house.

Small, purpose-built software that removes manual work: a lead tracker with a WhatsApp follow-up SOP, a product batch-number lookup for customers, a sales dashboard pulling from Sheets, or automated quotes and catalogues from a price list.

Every site ships with clean semantic HTML, structured data, fast Core Web Vitals, an XML sitemap and an llms.txt, and the copy is written answer-first — the same SEO/AEO/GEO approach we use on gaa-tha.com.
