## What is Influencer & UGC Marketing?

Influencer marketing is partnering with creators who already have an audience's trust so they feature a brand in their own content; UGC (user-generated content) marketing is commissioning authentic, creator-made videos and photos for the brand to use in its own ads, pages and listings. The first borrows an audience, the second borrows a voice — and the strongest campaigns use both.

At gaa-tha creator work is planned like media, not like a favour. We shortlist creators by audience fit and real engagement rather than follower count, write briefs and scripts that keep the message on-strategy while leaving room for the creator's style, negotiate usage rights upfront, and track every collaboration with codes and links so the report shows enquiries and sales, not just views.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between influencer marketing and UGC?

Influencer marketing pays a creator to feature your brand on their own profile, so you reach their audience. UGC (user-generated content) pays a creator or customer to make authentic-looking video or photos that you publish on your own channels, ads and product pages. gaa-tha runs both, often in the same campaign, with usage rights sorted upfront.

### How much do influencers charge in India?

It ranges from product-only barter for nano creators to significant fees for macro creators and celebrities, and it changes with platform, format, exclusivity and usage rights. We share a rate card for your shortlist before anything is committed and plan the creator mix to your budget rather than the other way round.

### How do you choose creators and avoid fake followers?

By audience fit first — where their followers live, their age and interests — and then by engagement quality: comments that read like real people, view-to-follower ratios and consistency across recent posts. We check past brand collaborations and audience location data before proposing anyone.

### Who owns the content and can we use it in ads?

Only if the agreement says so, which is why usage rights are negotiated before the shoot. We specify where the content can be used (ads, website, marketplace listings, in-store screens), for how long, and whether the creator's face and name can appear in paid media.

### How do you measure influencer marketing ROI?

Each creator gets a unique code, link or WhatsApp keyword, and we combine that with platform insights to report reach, engagement, profile visits, clicks, codes redeemed and cost per result. For awareness campaigns we also track branded search and follower growth during the campaign window.

### Can we do barter collaborations instead of paid ones?

Yes, particularly with nano and micro creators and local city influencers, where product, meals or experiences are a fair exchange. Barter works best for seeding and reviews; for guaranteed deliverables, timelines and usage rights, a paid agreement is more reliable.

### Do influencer posts need to be marked as ads?

Yes. ASCI guidelines in India require clear disclosure such as #ad or #collab on paid or barter collaborations. We include the disclosure in every brief and check it before posts go live.

### How many creators does a campaign need?

It depends on the objective. A city launch might use ten to twenty nano and micro creators for saturation; a product launch might pair a few category creators with a batch of UGC videos for ads. We size the mix to budget and reach goals in the plan, not to a fixed package.
