Amazon Account Management

gaa-tha runs Amazon account management for Indian brands as a monthly retainer: listing optimisation, Sponsored Products, Brands and Display ads, pricing and deals, inventory and FBA planning, account health, review and rating management, and a monthly P&L-style report that shows what you actually kept after Amazon's fees, ads and returns.

The service

What is Amazon Account Management?

Amazon account management is the ongoing operation of a brand's Seller Central account: keeping listings optimised and unsuppressed, running Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display ads, planning prices, coupons and Lightning Deals, forecasting inventory and FBA shipments, protecting account health, managing reviews and reconciling payments against fees and returns. It is a weekly operating rhythm, not a one-time setup.

At gaa-tha every Amazon account is run by a named account manager against one number agreed with you — usually net margin after fees, ads and returns. Listings, ads and deals are decisions made in service of that number, and the monthly review shows sales, Amazon fees, ad spend, returns and what is left, SKU by SKU.

Scope

What's included

  • Listing audits and refreshes: titles, bullets, attributes, search terms, images and A+ content
  • Amazon Ads management — Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display with weekly bid and budget work
  • Pricing, coupons, Lightning Deals and event plans for Prime Day and the Great Indian Festival
  • Inventory forecasting, FBA shipment plans and Inventory Performance Index (IPI) monitoring
  • Account health: order defect rate, late shipment, policy violations and IP complaints handled as they appear
  • Review and rating management using Amazon's own tools and policies
  • Brand Store and A+ updates for new launches and seasons
  • Payment reconciliation: settlements checked against orders, fees, returns and FBA reimbursement claims
  • Weekly scorecard and monthly P&L-style report with next month's plan

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and baseline

We review listings, ads, account health, inventory and the last three months of settlement reports, then agree the target number and the SKUs that matter most.

02

Fix the catalogue

Suppressed or weak listings are repaired first: attributes, images, A+ content and search terms, so ads and deals are not spent on pages that do not convert.

03

Ads and deals

Campaigns are restructured by product and intent, budgets set against ACoS and TACoS targets, and coupons, deals and event participation planned on a calendar.

04

Inventory and health

A rolling forecast drives FBA shipments and safety stock; account health metrics are watched weekly and issues answered before they become suspensions.

05

Report and review

A weekly scorecard keeps the team honest; the monthly review shows sales, fees, ads, returns and net margin per SKU, and sets the plan for the next month.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Weekly scorecard: orders, revenue, ACoS/TACoS, rank, stock cover and account health
  • Ads campaign structure and a running optimisation log
  • Deals and promotions calendar for the quarter
  • Inventory forecast and FBA shipment plan
  • Settlement reconciliation sheet with reimbursement claims filed
  • Monthly P&L-style report with next month's actions
