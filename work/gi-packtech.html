The partnership

What the brand needed from us

GI PackTech makes specialised industrial packaging — VCI films, vacuum laminates, machine covers, reactor liners and more. The products are technical, the buyers are international, and there was no material that explained each product clearly enough for an overseas buyer to act on.

The company needed a partner to explain the range, build the website buyers check first, and then run international lead generation month after month — not a single campaign.

What we handle

Every channel, under one retainer

International lead-generation campaigns

Campaigns for buyers across the world on LinkedIn and Meta, a 50-company Sales Navigator outreach tracker with curated message sequences, and a marketing playbook for both channels — with leads qualified before they reach the sales team.

International lead-generation campaigns · 16:10

Website — built and managed

We built and manage GI PackTech's website with product pages, applications and enquiry routes for international buyers, plus AI-produced hero imagery.

Website — built and managed · 16:10

Product catalogue and brochures

A full catalogue with product explanations, applications, customisation options and sales guidance for each line, and product-specific brochures such as the flexible reactor liner brochure.

Product catalogue and brochures · 16:10

AI product imagery

110 AI image prompts across 41 products for the catalogue and website — consistent product visuals without a 41-product photoshoot.

AI product imagery · 16:10

Local SEO

Google Business Profile content so domestic buyers searching for packaging suppliers find a complete, current profile.

GI PackTech now has a website, catalogue and image library an international buyer can act on, and a lead-generation engine that runs every month. International leads, countries reached, meetings booked and RFQs are published once confirmed from the outreach tracker.
