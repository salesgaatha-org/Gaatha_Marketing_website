## What is AI Chatbot Development?

An AI chatbot is a conversational assistant on your website or WhatsApp that answers customer questions in natural language using a large language model grounded in your own content — catalogue, FAQs, shipping and return policies — and that can look up live data such as order status or stock. A well-built one answers only from approved sources, admits when it does not know and passes the customer to a person instead of guessing.

At gaa-tha a chatbot project starts from your real customer conversations: we read past WhatsApp chats, emails and tickets to find the questions that repeat, then build the knowledge base, the flows for orders and leads, and the handoff rules. The bot is tested in English and the Indian languages your customers write in, connected to Shopify or your CRM, and reviewed every week in its first month using the conversation analytics.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an AI chatbot and a rule-based chatbot?

A rule-based chatbot follows pre-written menus and keywords, while an AI chatbot understands free-text questions and answers them in natural language from your own content. Rule-based bots break when a customer phrases something unexpectedly; AI bots handle variety but need grounding and testing so they stay accurate. We usually combine both — buttons for structured tasks such as order tracking, and AI for open questions.

### Can the chatbot run on WhatsApp?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business API with a verified business; the free WhatsApp Business app only offers simple greeting, away and quick-reply messages, not a real bot. Meta charges for business-initiated template messages by category, while replies to customers who message you first are generally free within the customer-service window — check Meta's current rates for India. Meta's policies also require the bot to serve your own customers, for support, orders or leads, rather than act as a general-purpose AI assistant.

### Can it reply in Hindi, Gujarati and Hinglish?

Yes. Current models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google handle Hindi, Gujarati and mixed Hinglish well, and the bot replies in the language the customer writes in. We test with the phrasing your customers actually use, including Hindi typed in Roman script, and keep product names and prices exactly as they appear in your catalogue.

### Will the chatbot make things up?

It is built not to: answers come only from your approved knowledge base and live data, and the bot is instructed to say it does not know and hand over rather than guess. We test it with off-topic and trick questions before launch and review unanswered questions weekly. Prices, stock and order status are read live from Shopify or your system, never from the model's memory.

### Does it integrate with Shopify?

Yes. The bot can read your Shopify catalogue for recommendations, look up order and fulfilment status for tracking questions, confirm COD orders and send cart reminders on WhatsApp to customers who have opted in. It can also pass on shipping updates from partners such as Shiprocket or Delhivery where their APIs allow.

### How much does an AI chatbot cost?

Setup cost depends on the channels (website, WhatsApp or both), the number of languages, the size of the catalogue and knowledge base, and integrations with Shopify, a CRM or a helpdesk. Running costs are WhatsApp message charges, any provider platform fee and AI model usage per conversation, all billed to your own accounts. We estimate the monthly running cost from your current chat volume before you commit.

### Can a person take over the conversation?

Yes, and they should for complaints, custom orders and high-value leads. Handoff rules send the chat to your team's shared inbox with the full conversation and customer details, the bot pauses while a person replies, and outside business hours it collects details and sets a callback.

### Is customer data safe with an AI chatbot?

Yes, when it is set up properly: conversations are stored in your accounts, access is role-based, and AI providers are used under API terms that do not train on your data by default. We collect only what each flow needs, show opt-in and privacy notices on WhatsApp and the website, and set retention rules so old chats are not kept indefinitely.
