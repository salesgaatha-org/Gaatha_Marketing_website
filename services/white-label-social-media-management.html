The service

What is White-Label Social Media Management?

White-label social media management means a partner plans, creates and publishes social content that an agency delivers to its clients under its own brand. The agency approves the work and owns the client conversation; the partner does the planning, design, editing, copywriting and scheduling.

gaa-tha already runs social media for brands in India and Canada, with content pillars, design, reels and community posts. For agencies we work in your approval tool and brand kits, write in American or British English to match the client, and deliver reports in your template.

Scope

What's included

  • Monthly content calendar built on agreed content pillars
  • Post, carousel and story design in the client's brand kit
  • Reels and short-form edits from client footage or stock
  • Captions and hashtags in US or UK English
  • Scheduling in Meta Business Suite, Later, Sprout Social, Hootsuite or Planable
  • Community management and comment replies during US business hours
  • Monthly performance report in your agency's branding
  • Paid social boosts coordinated with the ads team when needed

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Onboarding

Brand kit, tone of voice, audience, competitors and approval process agreed under NDA.

02

Pillars and calendar

Content pillars and a month-ahead calendar for your approval.

03

Create

Designs, reels and captions produced in batches in your approval tool.

04

Publish and engage

Approved content is scheduled and comments are handled within agreed hours.

05

Report

A branded monthly report with what worked and next month's plan.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Monthly content calendar
  • Designed posts, carousels and stories
  • Edited reels and short videos
  • Scheduled publishing and community log
  • Monthly white-label report
