## What is White-Label Shopify Development?

White-label Shopify development means an outside team builds and maintains Shopify stores that an agency delivers to its clients under its own name. The agency keeps the client relationship, the design direction and the account management; the development partner turns approved designs into a fast, editable Online Store 2.0 theme, handles migrations and integrations, and fixes issues after launch.

gaa-tha builds Shopify stores for its own clients and for agencies. We work from your Figma files or brief, develop on preview themes with Shopify's GitHub integration, test on real devices and Core Web Vitals, and hand over documentation in your agency's name. US and UK brands that want to outsource directly get the same team, with the store, apps and code always owned by them.

## How we work

1. **Brief and access** — You share designs or a brief; we request collaborator access and agree the repository, preview theme and QA checklist.
2. **Build plan** — We break the store into templates, sections and integrations with a timeline you can share with your client.
3. **Develop on preview** — Development happens on an unpublished theme connected to GitHub, with progress links you can review any time.
4. **QA and speed** — Cross-browser and device testing, Theme Check, accessibility basics, tracking verification and Core Web Vitals checks.
5. **Launch and support** — We launch with you, monitor the first days, fix issues in the agreed window and document everything unbranded.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I outsource Shopify development to India safely?

Yes, if the partner works in your accounts and your repository, signs an NDA and starts with a small paid pilot. gaa-tha develops on collaborator access and preview themes, keeps code in your GitHub, and never sets up stores or apps in its own name.

### Do you work from our designs?

Yes. Most agencies send Figma files and we build them pixel-accurately as editable Online Store 2.0 sections. If you only have a brief, our design team can produce the designs under your agency's name.

### Do you handle Shopify Plus?

Yes. We work on checkout extensibility, Shopify Functions for discounts and delivery rules, B2B catalogs and price lists, Shopify Markets for international selling and headless builds with Hydrogen when they are justified.

### Can you migrate a store from WooCommerce or Magento?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and orders, rebuild the design on Shopify, and map every old URL to a new one with redirects so search rankings carry over.

### How do you communicate with US teams?

Our team works US business hours, joins your Slack or Teams, and posts progress on your project board. You get preview links throughout the build, not just at the end.

### Who owns the code and the store?

You or your client. The store, apps, domain and repository are in your names; we work through collaborator or staff accounts and hand over all code and documentation.

### Can you also manage the store after launch?

Yes. Many agencies pair a build with our outsourced Shopify store management retainer for product updates, banners, app audits and fixes every month.
