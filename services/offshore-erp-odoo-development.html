The service

What is Offshore ERP & Odoo Development?

Offshore ERP development means an outside team configures, customizes and integrates your ERP while your team owns the processes and decisions. For most growing companies that means Odoo or ERPNext configured to their workflows, with custom modules only where the business is genuinely different.

gaa-tha maps your processes first, then configures, customizes, migrates data, integrates your other systems and trains your team. ERP partners and agencies can use us as white-label development capacity. Hosting, licenses and code stay in your name.

Scope

What's included

  • Process mapping and fit-gap analysis
  • Odoo and ERPNext configuration: sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, accounting, CRM
  • Custom Odoo modules and ERPNext apps in Python
  • Integrations with Shopify, QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe, shipping and marketplaces
  • Data migration from spreadsheets and legacy systems
  • Reports and dashboards for management
  • User training and documentation
  • Ongoing support and enhancement retainer

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Process mapping

Workshops in your hours to document how orders, stock, production and money move today.

02

Fit-gap and plan

What the standard ERP covers, what needs configuration and what needs custom code, in phases.

03

Configure and build

Configuration, custom modules and integrations on a test instance you can review.

04

Migrate and train

Data migration, user acceptance testing and role-based training.

05

Go live and support

Phased go-live with hypercare, then a support and enhancement retainer.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Process map and fit-gap document
  • Configured ERP test and production instances
  • Custom modules with source code in your repository
  • Integration documentation
  • Training material and support plan
