## What is Portfolio & Brand Website Design?

A portfolio or brand website is a site whose main job is to make someone believe in a person, studio or new brand — through the work, the story behind it and a clear point of view — and then make it easy for the right client, collaborator, employer or investor to get in touch. Unlike a corporate site that lists services, it is judged on taste, pacing and proof: how the work is framed, how quickly the images load and whether a stranger understands what you do within ten seconds.

At gaa-tha these sites start as a story, which is what our name means. We choose the projects that win the work you want next, write each case study as a brief, an approach and an outcome, design the layout around your visuals rather than a template, and add motion only where it helps the story. For new brands, the launch site carries the identity, the founder story and a waitlist or pre-order, ready for the first campaign.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a portfolio website cost?

The cost of a portfolio website depends mainly on whether the design is custom or template-based, how many case studies need writing and laying out, how much motion and video is involved, and which platform it runs on. A single-page personal portfolio and a studio site with a CMS, journal and custom animation are very different jobs, so gaa-tha scopes each one after a short call. Platform subscriptions such as Webflow hosting are billed by the platform, separately from the build.

### Webflow, WordPress or custom code — which is best for a portfolio site?

Webflow suits most designers and studios who want visual control and an easy CMS; WordPress suits people who publish often or need specific plugins; custom code in Next.js or Astro suits sites with unusual interactions or strict performance goals. The right choice depends on who will update the site and how often, so we recommend after seeing your content and team rather than defaulting to one platform.

### Is Behance, Instagram or Dribbble enough, or do I need my own website?

Platforms are good for discovery, but your own website is where serious clients check you before they commit. It lets you control the order and story of your work, appear for your name in Google and AI assistants, and send enquiries straight to you instead of into a feed. Most people keep both and link their platform profiles to the site.

### How long does a portfolio or brand launch website take?

Personal portfolios usually take two to four weeks, and studio or brand launch sites four to eight. The biggest variable is content — choosing projects, collecting high-resolution images and writing case studies — so we draft the stories with you rather than wait for a finished brief.

### Will heavy images and animation make my site slow?

Not if they are built carefully. We serve modern image formats at the right size for each screen, lazy-load media below the fold, stream video instead of embedding large files, and keep animation light with a reduced-motion fallback. Speed is tested on mid-range phones before launch, because that is where many clients first see your work.

### Do you build launch websites for new brands?

Yes. A brand launch site usually carries the new identity, the founder story, a product teaser and a waitlist, pre-order or enquiry form, and is ready for the first ad and influencer campaign. If the brand will sell online, we can build it on Shopify from day one so you do not rebuild after launch.

### What makes a good portfolio website, and how do I choose who builds it?

A good portfolio website shows fewer, stronger projects, explains each one in plain words, loads fast on a phone and makes contacting you obvious. When choosing a designer or agency, look at portfolio sites they have actually built, ask who writes the case studies, check which platform you will be left with, and confirm you will own the domain and logins. gaa-tha writes the case studies with you as part of the project, because that is where most portfolios fall short.

### Who owns the website, and can I update it myself?

You own it. The domain, hosting account, CMS and code are set up in your name, and every login is handed over at launch. You are trained to add projects, swap images and post updates without a developer, and an optional care plan covers larger changes.
