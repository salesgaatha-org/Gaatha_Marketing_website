The service

What is Marketplace Ads Management?

Marketplace ads management is the ongoing work of running paid placements inside marketplaces — Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display on Amazon, Product Listing Ads and Product Contextual Ads on Flipkart, and sponsored listings and banners on Myntra, Nykaa and AJIO — so that products win search positions and category placements at a cost that fits the margin. It covers campaign structure, keyword and placement selection, bids, budgets, negatives, event-period pushes and reporting on ACoS, TACoS and the organic rank the ads help build.

At gaa-tha marketplace ads are run against the unit economics of each SKU rather than a single account-wide target. Launch SKUs are allowed a higher cost of sale to earn rank and reviews; mature SKUs are held inside their margin; event periods get their own budgets and bid rules. Ad spend stays in your own ad accounts and is reported alongside organic sales so you can see whether the ads are growing the business or just replacing sales you already had.

Scope

What's included

  • Ad account audit or setup on Amazon Ads, Flipkart Ads, Myntra Ads and Nykaa or AJIO placements
  • Campaign architecture by product, match type and intent: branded, category, competitor and defensive
  • Keyword and placement harvesting from search-term reports, with negatives to cut waste
  • Weekly bid, budget and placement optimisation against per-SKU ACoS and TACoS targets
  • Sponsored Brands and Display creative, including brand-store and video placements where available
  • Event-period plans and budgets for Prime Day, Great Indian Festival, Big Billion Days, End of Reason Sale and platform sale events
  • Coordination with listing, pricing, stock and reviews so ads are not sent to pages that cannot convert
  • Monthly report on spend, sales, ACoS, TACoS, organic rank movement and new-to-brand customers

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Audit and targets

We review current campaigns, listings, margins and stock, then set ACoS and TACoS targets per SKU group — launch, growth and mature — instead of one number for the account.

02

Structure and keywords

Campaigns are rebuilt by product and intent, keywords and placements are chosen from search-term and competitor data, and negatives are set from day one.

03

Launch and learn

Campaigns go live with daily checks in the first two weeks to catch runaway spend, then bids and budgets are moved to the terms and placements that convert.

04

Events and scale

Event budgets, bid rules and creative are planned ahead of Prime Day, Big Billion Days and End of Reason Sale, and winners are scaled with rank as the check.

05

Report and refine

A monthly report shows spend, sales, ACoS, TACoS, organic rank and cannibalisation by SKU, and sets the next month's budget and tests.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Campaign architecture document and naming conventions per platform
  • Keyword and placement map with negatives, updated monthly
  • Weekly optimisation log
  • Event-period budget and bid plan for each sale event
  • Sponsored Brands and Display creative assets
  • Monthly ads report on ACoS, TACoS and organic rank by SKU
