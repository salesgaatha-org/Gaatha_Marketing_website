The service

What is Campaign Planning (Festive & Launch)?

Campaign planning is the work of taking one message to market across every channel at once — outdoor, digital ads, social content, influencers, cinema, in-store and on-ground — for a fixed period such as Diwali, a product launch or a season, with one budget, one key visual and one measure of success. A 360° campaign is the opposite of posting a festive creative and hoping.

At gaa-tha a campaign starts with the idea and the number it must move — footfall, launch bookings, orders — and then works backwards to the media plan. Because reels, AI ads, design, Meta ads, influencer management and cinema films are all in-house, the same key visual can become a store-front hoarding, an auto-rickshaw wrap, a reel, a WhatsApp broadcast and a cinema ad without three agencies in the loop.

Scope

What's included

  • Campaign idea, message and key visual, adapted to every format and size
  • Media plan and budget split across outdoor, digital, influencers, cinema and in-store
  • Outdoor — store-front branding, hoardings, auto-rickshaw and bus panels, with vendor coordination
  • Meta ads and Instagram content for the campaign window, including reels and AI ad shoots
  • Influencer collaborations with local city creators and category creators
  • Cinema and in-store video — script, production and placement
  • WhatsApp broadcasts, offers and landing page or store-locator setup
  • On-ground activation support — launch events, sampling, in-store displays and staff briefing
  • Campaign calendar with production deadlines, approvals and go-live dates
  • Post-campaign report on reach, engagement, enquiries, footfall or orders against the plan

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Objective and budget

We agree what the campaign must move — footfall, bookings, orders — the dates, the cities and the total budget, then set the one number it will be judged on.

02

Idea and key visual

One campaign idea, headline and key visual are developed and approved, with adaptations planned for every size from a reel to a hoarding.

03

Media plan

Budget is split across outdoor, Meta ads, influencers, cinema and in-store based on where your buyers are during the campaign window, with vendor quotes shown transparently.

04

Produce and launch

Creative is produced in batches, outdoor is printed and installed, creators are briefed, ads and cinema slots go live to the calendar, and store staff are briefed.

05

Monitor and report

Ads and content are optimised daily during the window, and a post-campaign report compares reach, enquiries, footfall or orders with the plan and records what to repeat.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Campaign concept, headline and key visual with format adaptations
  • Media plan with budget split and vendor quotes
  • Print-ready outdoor artwork and installed placements
  • Reels, ads, influencer content and cinema or in-store video
  • Campaign calendar and on-ground activation plan
  • Post-campaign performance report
