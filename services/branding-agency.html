The service

What is Branding & Brand Identity?

Brand identity is the complete system of name, logo, colour, typography, imagery, tone of voice and usage rules that lets people recognise a business before they read a word. It is the difference between a logo file and a brand: the logo is one asset, the identity is the set of decisions that keeps every asset looking and sounding like the same company.

At gaa-tha branding starts with the story, not the sketch. A discovery workshop with the founders sets the positioning and the one idea the brand stands for; the logo, colour, type and graphic system are then designed to carry that idea across a favicon, a WhatsApp post, a pack of snacks and a highway hoarding, and written into a guidelines PDF so the identity survives new designers, printers and platforms.

Scope

What's included

  • Brand discovery workshop with founders and the sales team
  • Positioning, brand story and messaging pillars
  • Naming and sub-brand naming support
  • Logo design or logo refresh, with responsive versions from favicon to hoarding
  • Colour, typography and graphic system
  • Tone of voice and writing rules
  • Stationery and collateral: visiting cards, letterheads, email signatures
  • Packaging direction and label system
  • Brand guidelines PDF
  • Social media templates for Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Discover

A half-day workshop with the founders covers the origin story, customers, competitors, ambitions and what must not change. We audit the current identity and every surface it appears on.

02

Position

We write the positioning, brand story and messaging pillars, and agree them before any visual work starts, so the design has something to say.

03

Design

Two or three logo and identity directions are presented as real mock-ups — packaging, hoarding, Instagram grid, website — not on white paper. One direction is chosen and refined.

04

Build the system

Colour, type, graphic devices, photography style, stationery, packaging direction and social templates are built out and tested at every size.

05

Hand over

You receive the guidelines PDF, all source and export files and a launch checklist; we can then roll the identity out across your website, socials and print.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Positioning and brand story document
  • Primary logo, secondary marks and favicon in SVG, PNG and PDF with Illustrator and Figma source
  • Colour, typography and graphic system with usage rules
  • Brand guidelines PDF
  • Stationery and collateral files ready for print
  • Editable social media templates in Canva or Figma
  • Packaging or label direction with dielines where required

Good to know

Questions about Branding

Answer-first, so humans and AI assistants get the point quickly.

A brand identity includes the logo and its variants, the colour palette, typography, graphic devices and imagery style, tone of voice, and the rules for using all of them — usually collected in a brand guidelines document. It often also covers stationery, social templates and packaging direction. gaa-tha delivers all of these in one project so nothing is left to interpretation later.

Branding fees in India vary widely: a logo-only job from a freelancer costs a small fraction of a full identity system with research, packaging and rollout from a studio, and the price follows the depth of discovery, the number of deliverables and the seniority of the team. gaa-tha quotes a fixed project fee after a short call, scoped to exactly what you need, so there is no open-ended billing.
