## What is Offshore ERP & Odoo Development?

Offshore ERP development means an outside team configures, customizes and integrates your ERP while your team owns the processes and decisions. For most growing companies that means Odoo or ERPNext configured to their workflows, with custom modules only where the business is genuinely different.

gaa-tha maps your processes first, then configures, customizes, migrates data, integrates your other systems and trains your team. ERP partners and agencies can use us as white-label development capacity. Hosting, licenses and code stay in your name.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I outsource Odoo development to India?

Yes. Odoo and ERPNext are widely implemented by India-based teams. Choose a partner that maps your processes before customizing, keeps code in your repository and supports you in your business hours, as gaa-tha does.

### Odoo or ERPNext?

Both cover sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing and accounting. The right choice depends on your processes, the modules you need, hosting preferences and licensing; we compare them for your case during process mapping.

### Can you integrate the ERP with Shopify and QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes. Orders, customers, stock and payouts can sync between Shopify and the ERP, and financial data can flow to QuickBooks or Xero where accounting stays outside the ERP.

### Do you work with ERP partners as a white-label team?

Yes. Implementation partners and agencies can use us for development, migration and support capacity under their brand, with an NDA and non-solicitation.

### Who owns the code and the data?

You do. Custom modules live in your repository, the ERP is hosted in your account and your data stays yours.

### Do you provide support after go-live?

Yes. A hypercare period follows go-live, then an optional monthly retainer covers support, fixes and enhancements during US business hours.
