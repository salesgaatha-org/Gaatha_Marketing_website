Top ecommerce & quick-commerce agencies in India: honest comparison

By gaa-tha· 25 September 2026· 5 min read· Commerce
Blog cover · 16:9

The best ecommerce or quick-commerce agency for a brand is the one whose scope matches the channels the brand actually needs, whose pricing model fits its stage, and whose proof is specific and verifiable. Indian agencies fall into a few types: marketplace specialists, quick-commerce operators, D2C and Shopify studios, performance-only shops, and full-service teams. This comparison explains the types and the questions that separate them, and states plainly where gaa-tha fits.

Key takeaways

  • Compare agency types before comparing names; the type determines what you will get.
  • Ask for scope, pricing model, reporting and proof in writing.
  • Beware of guaranteed results and of fees that are not explained.
  • gaa-tha is a full-service, storytelling-led team; that suits some brands and not others.

What types of ecommerce agency exist in India?

TypeWhat they do wellWhere they fall short
Marketplace specialistsAmazon and Flipkart onboarding, listings, ads, account healthOften weak on brand, content and the D2C store
Quick-commerce operatorsPlatform relationships, availability, purchase orders, q-com adsMay not cover creative, D2C or marketplaces
D2C and Shopify studiosStore builds, CRO, retentionMay not run marketplaces or quick commerce
Performance-only shopsMeta and Google ads at scaleCreative, store and channel operations left to the brand
Full-service teamsBrand, content, store, marketplaces, quick commerce and ads as one planFewer deep specialists; check depth per channel

This post deliberately does not rank named agencies. Claims about other companies’ results and fees change and cannot be verified from outside, and a list that ranked competitors would not be honest.

What questions separate good agencies from the rest?

  • Which platforms have you onboarded brands on in the last year, and what was the timeline?
  • What exactly is included in the monthly fee, and what is billed separately?
  • How is ad spend handled, and is it paid directly to the platform?
  • What does the monthly report contain, and can we see a sample?
  • Who will work on our account day to day?
  • Can we speak to a current client in a similar category?
  • What happens to our accounts, assets and data if we leave?

What pricing models will you see?

Most agencies charge a one-time onboarding or setup fee plus a monthly management fee, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms. Some charge a percentage of ad spend or of marketplace revenue; some offer project fees for store builds and creative. Fees vary widely by scope and change over time, so compare on what is included rather than on the headline number, and be cautious of any guarantee of sales, rank or return.

Where does gaa-tha fit?

gaa-tha is a full-service, storytelling-led agency founded in 2024 in Gujarat by Abhishek Khuthiya and Getansh Savla. We run quick commerce, marketplaces and D2C stores as one operation, with brand, content, imagery and ads produced in-house, and we report monthly on availability, sales, ad efficiency and margin. Our pricing follows the onboarding-plus-monthly model, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms; we share the current structure before anything is signed.

We are the right fit for brands that want one team across channels and value creative as much as operations. We are the wrong fit for a brand that only wants a single marketplace managed at the lowest possible fee. Our work with Charliee, a 200-plus SKU snack brand, shows the breadth: store, catalogue, imagery, ads and sales enablement from one team. Judge us by the same questions above; a strategy conversation is the usual first step.
