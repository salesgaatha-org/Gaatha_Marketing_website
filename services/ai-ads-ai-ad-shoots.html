The service

What is AI Ads & AI Ad Shoots?

An AI ad shoot is the production of product, model and lifestyle imagery or video using generative AI tools instead of a physical set, camera crew and location, with a human art director controlling composition, light, styling and brand consistency. The output is used the same way a photoshoot would be: ads, social posts, listings, catalogues and outdoor.

gaa-tha's pipeline starts with your real product — its pack, its colours, its details — and builds scenes around it: a kitchen in Mumbai, a Diwali table, a monsoon street, a studio with soft light. Each campaign set is art-directed, graded and typeset to your guidelines, checked for pack accuracy, and delivered in every ad size, with variations so the performance team can test hooks and offers without waiting for a reshoot.

Scope

What's included

  • Product hero visuals on studio, textured and brand-colour backgrounds
  • Lifestyle and model scenes with consistent characters across a campaign
  • Festive and seasonal campaign sets: Diwali, Navratri, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, Christmas and New Year
  • AI video ads, motion loops and animated product reveals
  • Ad variations for A/B testing: hooks, offers, backgrounds and formats
  • Catalogue and listing imagery for Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit and Shopify
  • Brand-matched colour grading, typography and layout
  • Pack accuracy and claims check before release
  • Exports in every Meta, Google, marketplace and print size

Process

How we work

Five steps, in the order they happen.

01

Brief and references

We collect your products, pack files, brand guidelines and campaign objective, and agree the mood, scenes and number of variations.

02

Concept boards

Two or three visual directions are generated as boards for you to choose from before full production starts.

03

Produce

The chosen direction is produced at scale — hero shots, lifestyle scenes, models, motion — with the art director controlling consistency.

04

Retouch and typeset

Every image is checked for pack accuracy and artefacts, then retouched, graded and typeset with offer, price and CTA where needed.

05

Deliver and test

Files go out in every size with variation sets labelled for testing; performance data comes back to shape the next batch.

Outputs

Deliverables you receive

  • Campaign set of art-directed product and lifestyle images
  • AI video ads or motion pieces in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9
  • Variation sets labelled for Meta and Google ad testing
  • Listing-ready images for marketplaces and quick commerce
  • Layered source files and a shared asset library
  • Usage notes on where each asset can and cannot be used

AI ads are ad creatives — images, videos, variations — produced with generative AI tools under human art direction instead of a physical photoshoot. An AI ad shoot is the same idea applied to a whole campaign or catalogue: consistent scenes, models and lighting across dozens of assets. gaa-tha produces both and pairs them with its performance team so the creatives are tested, not just delivered.

Yes, that is the point of the process.
